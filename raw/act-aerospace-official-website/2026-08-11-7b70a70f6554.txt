Who We Are
Applied Composite Technology was founded in a garage in 1985 by Roland Christensen. Today, ACT Aerospace is part of the Thermal Group , which also includes Alexander Machine & Tool and George Industries . With over a century of combined experience, the companies of The Thermal Group have been at the forefront of the design, fabrication, and assembly of mission-critical solutions based on thermal management principles.
