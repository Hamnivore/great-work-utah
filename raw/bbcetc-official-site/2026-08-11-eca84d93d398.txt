Fueling Innovation Businesses Through SEED FUNDING
From ideation to product launch, we assist companies and support organizations
navigate federal funding to support technology growth.
We are committed to one goal — achieving measurable results for every client.
Securing nondilutive R&D funding from a government program is highly competitive and complex. You need to write the most effective proposal possible — and then make sure that money creates a company that’s built for success. That’s why it’s essential to get expert assistance when you’re pursuing funding from SBIR/STTR or other government programs.
BBCetc has been helping small businesses for the past 30+ years with high-value information, expert training (including well-regarded virtual training), and experienced guidance to successfully navigate the process so they can reach their potential.
