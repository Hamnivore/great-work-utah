ELIGIBILITY
Nominees must either live, work, or do business in Utah. A nominee may be an
individual, business, or other organization. Anyone associated with the individual,
business, or organization may nominate. Best of State encourages self-nomination,
as nominees are often best qualified to describe their own achievements and
successes. Nominator identity is not available to judges. All judging is based solely
on information provided in the nomination and is done by a panel of independent
judges. Nominees may be nominated in multiple categories. Best of State reserves
the right to change nominee category placement.

HOW TO NOMINATE
Enter online at www.bestofstate.org. Entry deadline is March 15, 2020. Category listings
and specific entry requirements for each category can also be found online.

ENTRY FEES
A nominal entry fee applies to each nomination. See website for entry fee details.

Hale Centre Theatre

BEST OF STATE STATUE WINNER
BEST OF THE BEST® IN ARTS & ENTERTAINMENT

S
ince Hale Centre Theatre was founded in
1985, it has performed for more than 5
million patrons in Salt Lake City, West Valley
City, and Sandy City. Ticket purchases come
from 48 of the 50 states and all Utah counties.

Hale Centre Theatre has performed at nearly
100 percent capacity since 2004. As a result,
its volunteer board of trustees, consisting
of 76 local leaders from various areas of
expertise, saw the great need to move to the
Mountain America Performing Arts Centre
in November 2017.

Hale’s new Performing Arts Centre brings
a higher quality of productions, as it has
one of the most advanced stages in the
nation with the ability to incorporate fire,
water, flying, and moving sets. The Scarlet
Pimpernel had jaw-dropping moments
with a three-tiered carousel and a 23-foot
guillotine. In Frank Baum’s The Wizard
of Oz, Hale’s extremely technical flying
system allowed for trained aerialists to
come down from the ceiling, for flying
monkeys and the Wicked Witch of the
West to glide above the audience, and for
Dorothy to swirl around the stage at great
heights during the classic tornado scene.
In Big River, the theater poured, dumped,

and rained 11,000 gallons of water on
the stage, creating an actual river and
mesmerizing backdrop for the musical.

The regional premiere of Tuck Everlasting
allowed Hale to redesign the original
Broadway set and utilize the moving pieces
of the Sorenson Legacy Jewel Box Stage,
which features a circle and outer rim that
can move in the same or opposite directions.
With so many scene changes and set pieces
in Tuck Everlasting, the stage capabilities

allowed for actors to transition from scene
to scene seamlessly.

Hale Centre Theatre’s capabilities, success,
and relationships with national publishing
houses have led to hosting more regional
and national premieres than other theaters
in Utah. Nearly every year, Hale is granted the
rights to regionally premiere a production.
In 2018, Hale was granted the rights to the
regional premiere of Tuck Everlasting. They
were also handpicked by Alain Boublil and
Claude-Michel Schönberg, the talented duo
that composed Les Misérables, to produce
the first U.S. post-Broadway performance
of The Pirate Queen. Hale was also given
permission directly from the George
Gershwin family to regionally premiere An
American in Paris.

As Utah’s leading venue for both performing
and enjoying the arts, Hale Centre Theatre
takes pride in its mission to provide
world-class theater at prices families can
afford. The new theater produced 751
performances for 563,515 patrons in 2018,
and it’s planning on a long, bright future
of theater that will enrich the lives of those
who participate and attend.

Odyssey House
of Utah

BEST OF STATE STATUE WINNER
BEST OF THE BEST® IN EDUCATION

O
dyssey House’s mission is to
help people understand their
true potential, free of drugs and
alcohol. The comprehensive
education programming at Odyssey is critical
in achieving this goal. Founded in 1971 as a
non-profit, Odyssey House is the largest and
most encompassing addiction treatment and
behavioral health program in the state of Utah.

Teens at Odyssey House enroll in Odyssey
Academy, which is an on-site, fully accredited
high school managed by the Salt Lake City
School District. Adults at Odyssey who failed
to complete high school are also enrolled,
and once they receive their diploma, they
are eligible for a full-ride scholarship to the
University of Utah.

The vast majority of individuals in Odyssey’s
care have had a difficult time holding down
a job or have never participated in the
workforce. Maintaining a job while struggling

with mental illness and addiction is next
to impossible, so Odyssey House provides
hands-on education to ensure people can
obtain a job and live a stable life. Odyssey
teaches them the importance of waking up
on time, how to dress professionally, being
punctual, healthy communication, and
integrity in the workplace.

Odyssey has in-house, mock job placements
for newly stabilized individuals to practice
maintaining responsibilities while taking
care of their personal needs. These
opportunities include running an industrial
kitchen, remodeling, customer service,
IT, cleaning crews, fleet management and
driving, and outdoor maintenance. Everyone
is given a job, and they are coached how to
interact with their co-workers and Odyssey
staff supervisors. Once people stabilize
in these roles, they are then placed in
partnering businesses to practice recovery
techniques outside the treatment facility,

and most importantly, can transition into a
full-time job once they complete care.

To help clients return to a healthy home
environment, Odyssey House provides
family therapy and group sessions, teaching
healthy communication, boundaries, the
risks of enabling, understanding addiction
as a disease, grief and loss counseling, and
various other topics. Odyssey’s therapeutic
daycare teaches parents how to engage in
healthy and developmentally appropriate
ways with their children, while teaching
children to heal and process any trauma
they may have.

Odyssey House’s programs are built on
evidence-based models and lead to healthy
self-sufficiency. Odyssey is proud that the
people they serve change their lives, remain
sober, and become contributing members of
the community.

lackrock empowers researchers

BEST OF THE BEST® IN SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY

Blackrock
Microsystems
Over the last few years, groundbreaking
discoveries and milestones have been
reached in the pursuit of restoring
function to people. Keven Walgamott, an
amputee from Utah, was able to hold and
feel his wife’s hand again thanks to the
Utah Array and a prosthetic arm named
after Luke Skywalker. Previously thought
possible only in science fiction, Keven was
able to move the prosthetic hand with his
thoughts, and the hand communicated

more natural hearing to people who are deaf
or hard of hearing, an improvement over the
current cochlear implant.

Blackrock Microsystems has the largest
portfolio of FDA-cleared and CE-marked
products for neurophysiology in the world.
The design restrictions to achieve these

certifications have resulted in products that
are better documented, more reliable, and
safer than their competitive counterparts.
Approximately 500 organizations and
neuroscientists across the globe use
Blackrock’s technology, and thousands of
scholarly peer-reviewed articles have been
published citing the Utah Array. Blackrock
Microsystems enables researchers to do the
unthinkable and to achieve solutions that
can benefit the world.
