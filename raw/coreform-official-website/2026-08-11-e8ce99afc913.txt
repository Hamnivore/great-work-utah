WHY WE EXIST
Model prep is the bottleneck. We attack it from both ends.
Getting from a design to an accurate simulation usually means hours of geometry cleanup and meshing before you ever hit solve. Coreform builds the tools that shrink that gap: world-class meshing when you need a mesh, and isogeometric analysis when you use Abaqus and can’t get a timely mesh with your current tools. Either way, you spend less time preparing models and more time trusting results.

THE TECHNOLOGY
Two technologies, built on deep foundations.
Coreform products rest on decades of research. One reimagines how analysis runs on geometry, the other comes from the lab that has led hex meshing for a generation.
COREFORM IGA
Analysis on exact CAD
Coreform IGA runs simulation on the same smooth spline math your CAD is built from, so you get accurate results with far fewer elements and the manual meshing step largely disappears.
Read the technology deep dive →
COREFORM CUBIT
Leading hex meshing algorithms from Sandia
Cubit is developed by Sandia National Laboratories, which has led the world in hexahedral meshing research for decades. Coreform is its exclusive commercial distributor, bringing that capability to industry.
