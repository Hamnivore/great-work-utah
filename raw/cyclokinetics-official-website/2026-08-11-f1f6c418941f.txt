CycloKinetics is a U.S.-based propellant technology company developing high-performance, drop-in fuels for aerospace and defense applications.
Built to outperform conventional propellants, CycloKinetics solutions enable platforms to fly farther, higher, faster, longer, and carry more.
Mission Profiles
Unlocking the Future of Aerospace and Defense
CycloKinetics develops advanced propellants with unique molecular properties which both enable next-generation aerospace and defense platforms and enhance the performance of existing platforms. Its fuels are drop-in replacements for the current fuels which power turbine-powered platforms, Collaborative Combat Aircraft (CCA), Unmanned Aircraft Systems (UAS), and Counter-Unmanned Aircraft Systems (C-UAS), long-range and attritable missiles, next-generation propulsion, and space launch systems, allowing these platforms to fly farther, faster, higher, longer and carry more. Military testing across more than ten applications, including both ground and flight tests on currently fielded systems, has demonstrated measurable improvements in mission performance.

01
CycloJP
Advanced drop-in fuel for aerospace and next-generation propulsion systems
CycloJP is a high energy density replacement for conventional aerospace fuels, including Jet A, JP-5, JP-8, and JPTS, designed to enhance performance across both manned and unmanned aircraft platforms.
By significantly increasing energy density, CycloJP enables aircraft to fly farther and longer, extending mission range and endurance without additional fuel volume. Its advanced formulation also supports higher-altitude operations, with superior low-temperature performance and viscosity characteristics that maintain fuel integrity in extreme conditions.
Improved thermal stability enables more efficient high-speed flight, while cleaner combustion produces lower-luminosity flames and exceptionally lower soot emissions vs. Jet A, extending engine life and maintenance intervals.
CycloJP supports a wide range of mission profiles, including:
Turbine-powered platforms, CCA, UAS and C-UAS
Attritable systems
Special missions, including high-altitude, long-endurance ISR platforms
Next-generation high-speed propulsion systems
02
CycloRP
Advanced propellant for space launch and in-space propulsion
CycloRP is a high-performance, drop-in replacement for RP-1 and RP-2 kerosene-based rocket fuels, designed to improve launch efficiency and payload capacity. With significantly higher volumetric and gravimetric energy density, CycloRP enables launch systems to carry more payload without increasing fuel load, directly improving launch economics and cadence. These gains allow rockets to achieve the same energy output with less propellant loading, freeing capacity for substantial increases in payload mass per launch.
Superior thermal stability also improves regenerative cooling performance, supporting more efficient and reliable propulsion system operation in demanding launch environments. Cleaner combustion and lower soot emissions as compared to RP-1 and RP-2 extends engine life and maintenance intervals for reusable engines.
CycloRP is optimized for:
Space launch platforms
In-space propulsion

Enabling Both Domestic and In-Theater Manufacturing
CycloKinetics propellants are produced in the United States, supporting secure and scalable domestic supply for defense and aerospace applications. The company's industrial manufacturing facility in Salt Lake City, Utah became operational in 2025, with deliveries beginning under Department of War contracts in Q2 2025. Our manufacturing facilities are expanding rapidly and ready to support our customers, whether their platforms are at large-scale production or just scaling up.
CycloKinetics' production process is designed to be feedstock-flexible, allowing manufacturing to be optimized for locally available resources. This approach supports resilient domestic supply chains while enabling in-theater production capabilities in contested operational environments.
