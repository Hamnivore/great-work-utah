Built to Perform. Designed to Disappear.
We make high-performance resins and films for electronics that hold up in use and break down when you want them to, so the metals and components inside can be recovered instead of thrown away.
Learn more
The Problem with Electronics that Last Forever
Circuit boards are built to survive heat, chemicals, and years of use. The conventional plastics that make that possible are also what make boards almost impossible to take apart. So most of metals inside get shredded or landfilled. We are generating more than 62 million tonnes of e-waste annually and formally recycle less than a quarter of it.
Circularity Built-In
We design photocurable, degradable-by-design low-dielectric resins for use as electronic substrates and encapsulants. They perform as needed while a device is in use. At end of life, they break down through low-temperature hydrometallurgical methods, which frees the components and precious metals for recovery.
