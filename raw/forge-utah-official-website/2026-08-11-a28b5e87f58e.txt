> ABOUT
A not-for-profit, run by volunteers
Forge Utah Foundation is a not-for-profit dedicated to connecting Utah's developers, data scientists, SREs, and tinkerers. No sponsors pushing product, no recruiter pitches — just people who build things, showing each other how.
If you write code, wrangle data, keep servers alive, or solder things on weekends, you belong here.
