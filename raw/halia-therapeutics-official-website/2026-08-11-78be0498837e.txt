What is Genetic Resilience?
Uncovering Nature's Built-In Defenses
At Halia Therapeutics, we shift the paradigm from treating illness to understanding resilience. Using population-scale pedigrees and genetic data, we uncover protective factors that help some individuals resist disease - even with high-risk genotypes or environmental triggers.
