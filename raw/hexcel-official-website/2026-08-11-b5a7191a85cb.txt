Why Choose Hexcel
Designed for Demanding Composite Applications
Hexcel empowers engineers and manufacturers with high-performance, certification-backed composite materials. Our solutions are crafted to meet rigorous standards in both performance and delivery.
Preferred Carbon Fiber Developer
Hexcel is the world’s largest producer of aerospace carbon fiber and the leading supplier of carbon fiber to U.S. military programs, making it an industry leader in advanced aerospace materials.
Honeycomb Manufacturing
Hexcel is the global leader in honeycomb manufacturing developing lightweight core material offering exceptional light weighting, stiffness, and noise and energy absorption.
Product Safety
We are committed to providing support and customer care to help our customers safely handle and use our products and enable them to fulfill their legal obligations.
Advanced Process Innovation
At Hexcel, a culture of innovation permeates our organization and is a strategic priority. Targeting a breakthrough, aiming for what seems an unrealistic goal, or challenging some long-held paradigm are springboards to discovery and thinking “outside the box.” We embrace the curiosity to explore ideas, the passion to challenge the impossible and the conviction to succeed beyond expectations.
