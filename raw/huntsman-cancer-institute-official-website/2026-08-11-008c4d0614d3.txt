Vision is the fuel of hope and lasting growth.
From an empty hillside to a global leader in research and care in just over 25 years, Huntsman Cancer Institute is transforming the way cancer is understood, prevented, detected, treated, and survived. With more than 1 million square feet at our Salt Lake City location, 3 community clinics, 7 regional affiliate hospitals, and 2 future locations planned, we deliver better outcomes for patients across the Mountain West and beyond.

Caring for you beyond diagnosis and treatment
We understand that behind every diagnosis is a personal story.
We are here to provide medical care and the emotional and compassionate support you and your family deserve during and after treatment. Our services include cancer education, personalized nutrition guidance, pain management, fitness programs, survivorship support, integrative therapies, caregiver resources, and much more.
