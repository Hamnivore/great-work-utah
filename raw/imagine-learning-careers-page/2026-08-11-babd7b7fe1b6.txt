Recruiting Scam Alert
There is a recruiting scam involving fraudulent job offer letters using Imagine Learning’s name and logo. Imagine Learning requires candidates to complete online job applications prior to consideration for an interview and offer. We never ask for an upfront payment or deposit from job candidates, nor do we provide monetary stipends for candidates to purchase their own equipment. If you are uncertain whether you are communicating with an Imagine Learning representative, please contact us at careers@imaginelearning.com .

How and where we work
As a remote-friendly workplace, most Imagine Learning employees enjoy the opportunity to work from home full-time or several days each week. Coworkers enjoy the opportunity to collaborate in-person at one of our U.S. or international offices and through events, conferences, employee appreciation, and community involvement opportunities.
Ongoing employee development
Shaping the future of education starts with investing in you .
