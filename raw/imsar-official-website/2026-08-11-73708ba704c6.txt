Once Impossible. Now Practical.
IMSAR challenges assumptions about traditional radar. A revolutionary design approach now delivers all-weather, high-fidelity imaging and precise tracking without the historical trade-offs. This paradigm shift provides radar capabilities to tactical platforms to better protect life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness.
Aggressive Timelines. Readily Available.
Designed, built, and supported in Springville, UT, USA. Scaled operations now yield hundreds of radar systems annually to meet high-volume demand. The focus remains on delivering rapid innovation with high quality, ensuring critical capabilities reach the field on relevant timelines.
Add Radar. Maintain Endurance.
Low-SWaP radar systems integrate onto Group 1-5 UAS and manned aircraft without displacing traditional EO/IR payloads. This allows operators to add all-weather radar capability and achieve true Multi-INT collection without compromising platform endurance.
One Radar. Many Missions.
A single multi-mode radar system provides high-resolution imaging, change detection, and wide area search and tracking capabilities. Powerful integrations with EO/IR and EW payloads allow the radar to multiply mission effectiveness. This software-defined versatility ensures readiness for evolving mission requirements.
