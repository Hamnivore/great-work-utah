Utah’s Top
Multi-Service Law Firm
Kirton McConkie is Utah’s premier multi-service law firm, with 200+ attorneys across five offices. We offer practical legal service to clients in all 50 states and in more than 150 countries. Our practice areas span intellectual property law, corporate law, real estate law, and litigation.
Attorneys
Our attorneys are trusted advisers, strategic problem-solvers, and true partners to our clients. With graduates from 52 different law schools, including nine of the top ten ranked nationally, our legal team brings a broad range of experience, insight, and industry knowledge to every case. Nearly half of our attorneys speak multiple languages, and many have practiced law internationally, giving us the perspective and capability to handle legal matters across borders and industries.
