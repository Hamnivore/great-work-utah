Visual collaboration for effective teamwork
A work acceleration platform powered by visual collaboration, Lucid brings everyone and everything together. Capture and execute the full picture of work.
Contact sales
Capabilities
Accelerate work from strategy through execution.
Collaboration
Work across teams to accomplish common business goals. You can:
Easily share boards, documents, and permissions with all the right people.
Facilitate real-time coordination and decision-making in a shared space.
Create visuals and capture ideas faster with pre-made templates.
Gauge team sentiment and get aligned with Visual Activities.
Lucid AI and automation
Empower teams to maximize efficiency and output. You can:
Generate ideas and diagrams automatically from text prompts and files.
Sort, gather, and summarize content on your Lucid canvas.
Leverage an AI assistant to accomplish work in a conversational way.
Shared workspaces
Provide a real-time environment where people can collaborate and access team resources. You can:
Organize teams and centralize documents with team hubs.
Pin a dedicated team space that provides a starting point for coordination and information sharing.
Specify user access levels to team hubs and shared documents.
Switch between purpose-built tools in Lucidchart and Lucidspark using universal canvas.
Facilitation and ideation
Create a collaborative space where teams can brainstorm in real time. You can:
Keep everyone engaged and on track with powerful Facilitator Tools and Controls, such as voting, private mode, laser pointer, and timer.
Give contextual feedback with comments and @mentions.
Convert boards into slide decks with the presentation builder, and use paths to guide participants through content on your board.
Add breakout boards to enable small group work and discussion.
Project planning
Move seamlessly from ideation to planning and action. You can:
Create Timelines to visualize future plans.
Add Dynamic Tables to organize ideas and plan visually.
Plan, organize, and assign tasks with Lucid Cards.
Estimate the effort required for tasks with team estimation activities.
Maintain Agile principles by facilitating collaborative, engaging events.
Data-driven diagrams
Connect diagrams to data sources and put complex data in context. You can:
Import data to auto-generate org charts and entity relationship diagrams (ERDs).
Link diagrams to dynamic external data sources.
Add conditional formatting rules to provide visual cues based on data.
Apply formulas to diagrams and associated metadata.
Diagram as code with Mermaid.

Frequently asked questions
Learn more about visual collaboration.
How does the Lucid Visual Collaboration Suite support hybrid teams?
The Lucid Suite provides a shared virtual space where teams can collaborate from anywhere in real time or asynchronously. Lucid’s interactive capabilities—Visual Activities, breakout boards, voting, emoji reactions, team estimation activities, and more—keep participants engaged and on track. They also foster connection, which is difficult to cultivate among dispersed teams. As a visual platform, Lucid enables collaborators to facilitate meetings and brainstorming sessions, clarify complex information, create and share documentation, and make informed decisions faster.
Learn more
What integrations does Lucid support for connecting with task management platforms?
Lucid integrates with leading task and project management platforms, including airfocus, Airtable, Asana, Azure DevOps, Confluence, Jira, monday.com, and Smartsheet.
Learn more
How do team hubs and shared workspaces facilitate project planning?
Team hubs provide a shared space for centralizing resources and documentation. Within a team hub, you can pin a team space that serves as a home base for coordination. The team space provides a shared workspace where team members can organize content and resources, assign tasks, monitor progress, and more.
Learn more
What features are available for managing sharing and permissions in Lucid?
You can share documents, boards, and folders from your Lucid home page and from within the editor. You can also use a Join ID that allows collaborators to access a document or board for a limited window of time. To manage permissions for collaborators, you can grant access to edit and share, edit, comment, or view. Additionally, within team hubs, team roles and permissions allow users to assign the right level of access to the team and to shared documents within the hub.
Learn more
What templates are available to help hybrid teams get started quickly?
Depending on what your hybrid team is trying to accomplish, any of Lucid’s templates can help you get started faster. To find the right template for your needs, search the Lucid template gallery by keyword or browse by category, including brainstorming, meetings and workshops, teams and organizations, and more.
Learn more
How do I use breakout boards in Lucidspark?
You can add up to 50 breakout boards to your main Lucidspark board. After you select to start a breakout session from the breakout board panel, all breakout boards will be visible to participants and they can select a board to join.
Learn more
How can I enable team folders in Lucidchart?
Team folders can be enabled in Lucidchart for Enterprise users. Lucid admins can choose to allow anyone in the org to create team folders or limit this functionality to admins. Once a team folder is created, you can invite other team members to collaborate by right-clicking on the team folder or any document within the team folder and selecting to share it.
