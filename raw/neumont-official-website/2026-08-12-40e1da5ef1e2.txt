welcome home
We get you. You can’t get enough tech, and neither can we. At Neumont University, we’ve created a training ground for modern-day tech innovators to hone their analytical, creative, and coding skills to create the software that will change the world. You will earn a Bachelor of Science degree focused on computer science while developing a portfolio of real-world experience. And when you graduate, you’ll be ready to join the tech elite.
THE NEUMONT DIFFERENCE
Our academic approach is simple: learn to master technology in a project-based environment on collaborative teams.

EXPERIENCE NEUMONT IRL
Choosing a college is a big decision, and FReX is the best way to decide whether Neumont is the right fit.
Spend the day building alongside other future Neumont students, learning about student capstone projects, meeting the faculty who you’ll be learning from, and experiencing how we turn classroom concepts into working technology. You’ll also tour our student housing, meet club presidents and student government, and get a feel for life in downtown Salt Lake City.
If you’re considering a career in tech, FReX is for you!

We were very pleased to be awarded the top spot on a list of bachelor’s-degree-granting institutions giving the highest ROI to low-income students in a recent study published by Georgetown University ROI = return on investment, meaning the best bang for your buck, and we couldn’t be prouder of this distinction.
Knowing that Neumont has the best return on investment in the nation gives Neumont students and alumni high confidence in their educational investment.
In the newly released 2025 Carnegie Classifications, Neumont was nationally recognized as an Opportunity Colleges; one of only 479 institutions across the U.S. designated for high student access and high alumni earnings.
This new classification highlights colleges that serve students from lower-income or underrepresented backgrounds and ensure strong post-graduation outcomes.
