Nucleus convenes , educates , and funds innovation in Utah — designated by the state to bring together industry, academia, and government to drive economic growth and tackle our biggest challenges.
Our Programs
The role we play:
Every program Nucleus runs maps back to one of the three core roles. Together they form a complete system that works to fill the white spaces in the process of a deep tech idea turning into a life-changing company.
Convene. Educate. Fund.
Convene
Nucleus exists to bring together universities, industry leaders, government officials, and entrepreneurs in a purposeful way whose proximity to each other changes what’s possible.
Educate
Nucleus works everyday to equip Utah’s innovators with the market intelligence, skills, and frameworks needed to move from discover to real-world impact.
Fund
Nucleus closes the funding, commercialization, and policy gaps that prevent great ideas from scaling with the singular mission of giving Utah’s best innovations what they need to grow.
