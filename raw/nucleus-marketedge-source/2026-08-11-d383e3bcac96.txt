Nucleus MarketEdge
Helping Utah researchers take the next step
Transform Insight into Enterprise
Nucleus MarketEdge is designed for professors, PhDs, and postdocs in Utah’s higher education system who are ready to explore the path from research to real-world impact. This virtual, cohort-based program gives innovators the structure, mentorship, and connections they need to navigate commercialization and startup formation with confidence.
What You’ll Experience
Work alongside a select group of peers from across Utah’s higher education system
Learn from seasoned founders, industry experts, and active investors
Access to online learning modules you can complete at your own pace
Participate in weekly virtual discussions to share progress and get feedback
Gain insight into funding opportunities, market readiness, and startup formation
Pitch your technology to active investors and experts
Who Should Join
Professors, PhDs, and postdocs based at a Utah university or technical college
Innovators curious about commercializing their research
Those ready to commit 8–10 hours a week for 8 weeks in a fully virtual program
Cost: Free
Why Join
Build a network that spans academia, industry, and investment
Gain clarity on your commercialization path
Access resources that can help turn your idea into a viable business
Position yourself for potential grant funding upon program completion
