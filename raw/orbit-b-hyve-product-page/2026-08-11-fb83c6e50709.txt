What is B-hyve
What is B-hyve?
B-hyve is smart watering that actually saves you time and water. It's a line of Wi-Fi-connected sprinkler controllers and hose faucet timers that automatically adjust your watering based on local weather—so you don't have to think about it.
The short version: B-hyve connects to your home Wi-Fi, checks hyperlocal weather multiple times a day, and adjusts watering schedules automatically. Rain in the forecast? It skips the cycle. Hot and dry? It adds time. All controlled from your phone, wherever you are.
How It Works
B-hyve uses something we call WeatherSense technology. Once you set up your zones and tell the system a bit about your landscape, it takes over. You can select your local weather station or even connect a personal weather station for maximum accuracy. The system pulls weather data, calculates how much moisture is in your soil, and runs your sprinklers only when needed.
B-hyve gives you more control over your watering than any other system—7 customizable inputs, the most in the industry. You dial in soil type, plant type, sprinkler type, sunlight exposure, rainfall, and slope. The system then factors in application rate, efficiency, plant factor, microclimate, management allowed depletion, field capacity, permanent wilting point, root zone depth, allowable surface accumulation, basic infiltration rate, effective rainfall, and drought factor to create a precision watering schedule.
Want to fine-tune it even more? Run a catch cup test. The B-hyve app walks you through an irrigation audit using catch cups to measure your actual sprinkler precipitation rate. The app automatically adds those results to the calculations—no manual math required.
No rain? No freezing temps? B-hyve waters. Rain in the forecast? It delays. Wind too high? It holds off. You get the right amount of water without the guesswork.
Bluetooth and Wi-Fi: Why Choose?
All B-hyve controllers can work over Bluetooth when you're within range—about 50-100 feet depending on the device. That means you can operate the timer, run zones manually, and adjust settings from your phone even if you don't have Wi-Fi at that location.
But here's the catch: without Wi-Fi, you lose the smart programming features. WeatherSense needs an internet connection to pull weather data and automatically adjust schedules. So Bluetooth gives you manual control, but Wi-Fi gives you true set-it-and-forget-it automation.
For hose faucet timers that don't have built-in Wi-Fi, you'll need a separate Wi-Fi hub to unlock the smart features.

Why People Choose B-hyve
EPA WaterSense certified – Qualifies for rebates from many water utilities
7 customizable inputs – More control than any other system on the market
Built-in catch cup irrigation audit – Test your system, improve accuracy automatically
Choose your weather station – Use local data or connect a personal weather station
Works with Alexa and Google Assistant – Control watering with your voice
Bluetooth and Wi-Fi – Manual control via Bluetooth, smart automation via Wi-Fi
Physical buttons on the controller – Not app-only. You can still operate it manually if Wi-Fi goes down

Who's Behind B-hyve
B-hyve is made by Orbit Irrigation, a company that's been building sprinkler equipment in Salt Lake City since 1970. In 2021, Orbit joined Husqvarna Group's Gardena Division, so we're now part of a global water-products family. The same Utah team still designs and supports every B-hyve product.
Is B-hyve Right for You?
If you want smart watering without the premium price tag, B-hyve fits. It's built for DIY homeowners who want to save water and time without hiring a contractor. If you're in a water-restricted area, the WaterSense label means you might qualify for a rebate. And if you rent or don't have an in-ground system, the hose faucet timers give you smart watering for under $100.
Bottom line: B-hyve gives you weather-smart watering, app control, precision scheduling with 7 inputs, and water savings—without the complexity or cost of other systems.
