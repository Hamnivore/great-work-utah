AI That Acts
in the Real World
Most AI thinks. Palladyne AI acts.
Intelligence that must operate in the physical world cannot wait for the cloud. It must perceive locally, decide in milliseconds, and execute at the point of action, in GPS-denied airspace, on contested battlefields, and on factory floors where latency can mean a failed mission or a missed command. Nature solved this architecture long ago. Palladyne AI built it for machines.
Our embodied AI platform powers two businesses: a vertically integrated Aerospace & Defense company, from software and edge silicon through loitering munitions to sovereign U.S. manufacturing, and an industrial software platform that makes your existing robots poly-functional, not just automated.
This is not theoretical. It is deployed today.

The Architecture: DECA
Our architecture, Decentralized Embodied Collaborative Autonomy (DECA), is built on how biological intelligence actually works: each system perceives locally, decides in real time, acts continuously, and collaborates directly with other systems. Coordination emerges through collaboration, not central control.
The cloud defines the mission and centralized systems define the plan. Palladyne AI executes it.

A Familiar Pattern
Every major computing shift follows the same trajectory: centralization first, then distribution at scale. Mainframes gave way to PCs. Data centers gave way to the cloud. Now intelligence is leaving the cloud and moving to the machines themselves.
This shift is not optional. Physics, latency, and the demands of real-world operation require it.
Palladyne AI is not waiting for that transition to happen. We are building the architecture that defines it.
