How it works
Mature engineering, applied in an innovative, new way
Organic Refrigerant, Not Water
Organic, non-corrosive, low GWP refrigerant used instead of water as the working fluid. It flows down as a cool liquid and vaporizes naturally at depth, rising to drive a turbo expander.
One Moving Part
The turbo expander is the system’s only moving part. Mature, reliable, low-maintenance, and designed to run for 40+ years.
Horizontal Drilling in Permeable Rock
Our system works in permeable basins, using horizontal drilling techniques already proven in industrial energy applications.
Repeatable Pad Development
Elegant design and proven components simplify deployment from one pad to the next.
