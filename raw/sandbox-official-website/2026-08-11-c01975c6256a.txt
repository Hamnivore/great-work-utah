Our Manifesto
Education should be one of the most inspiring things we do, but it isn't. Education has become sterile classrooms, obsession with grades, and cramming for tests. It’s hoop jumping and box checking — we refuse to accept this.
We’re relentless about creating a world where classrooms are replaced by premium workspaces, lectures by hands on building, irrelevant class projects by things you care about, and anonymous student bodies by tight knit, ambitious cohorts. And we're committed to doing all that while still granting fully accredited degrees. Our mission: no more hoops, no more boxes, just education that genuinely inspires.
Chris Crittenden
CEO, Sandbox
chris@sandbox.ing
