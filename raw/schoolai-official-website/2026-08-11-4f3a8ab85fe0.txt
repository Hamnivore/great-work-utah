Built on trust
Schools trust us with their students. Everything we build starts from that responsibility.
Student protection is non-negotiable
SchoolAI flags crises in real time, never poses as human, and connects students to trusted adults. Student safety governs every design decision, from model selection to alert routing.
Grounded in research
Our ESSA-validated logic models and active research pipeline study how AI affects learning. Our Ethics Advisory Council keeps what we build aligned to the evidence, and publishes their recommendations openly so the whole field benefits.
Teachers stay in control
Every safeguard, content boundary, and AI behavior is configurable by educators. Dot operates within the boundaries your district sets.
Your data stays yours
Every AI provider we work with is contractually bound never to train on or sell student data. SOC 2 Type 2 compliant and 1EdTech certified, FERPA and COPPA compliant.
