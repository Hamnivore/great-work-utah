SHARING OUR PASSION FOR MUSIC
Providing free comprehensive music education
after school since 2013
Offering violin, viola, cello, bass, clarinet, trumpet, saxophone, trombone, piano, flute, percussion and bucket band/taiko drumming. A feature of our program are our performance groups "Sistema Strings" and "Secondary Sistema Strings" consisting of our most advanced violin, viola, cello and bass students that offer service concerts in the community. We also have two intermediate secondary ensembles: secondary orchestra ensemble and secondary band ensemble. Our plan is to continue to help our students to cultivate their craft through the ​12th grade!
Sistema Utah has done the REAL work necessary to use music as a vehicle for social transformation and they get better at it every year.
Dan Trahey
Founder, Tuned In @ The Johns Hopkins University
