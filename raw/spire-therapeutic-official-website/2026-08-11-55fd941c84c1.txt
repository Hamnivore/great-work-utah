Surgery-free and drug-free treatments of chronic pain and depression
An ultrasonic device called Diadem noninvasively and precisely resets the deep brain circuits that are hyperactive in chronic pain and depression. Diadem is unique in that it compensates for the ultrasound distortions by the head and hair, and thus provides a controlled and practical therapy.
20M
People in the United States suffer from treatment-resistant chronic pain
6M
People in the United States suffer from treatment-resistant depression
Rapid-acting relief
Because Diadem resets the hyperactive deep brain regions directly using focused ultrasonic waves, clinical studies have shown rapid, immediate improvements in pain intensity and depressive symptoms.
