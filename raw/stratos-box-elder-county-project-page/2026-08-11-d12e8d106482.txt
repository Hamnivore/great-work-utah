Welcome to Box Elder County Stratos Project Information Page
This page has been developed to provide residents, media, and community stakeholders with access to public information related to the Stratos Project, including timelines, public documents, meeting materials, FAQs, and updates as they become available to the county.
The county recognizes there is significant public interest and ongoing community discussion surrounding this project. Our goal is to make county-related information and publicly available resources accessible in one centralized location.
This page will continue to be updated as additional public information becomes available. Residents are encouraged to review available materials and submit questions through the contact form below. Media inquiries should be directed to the County Public Information Officer.
