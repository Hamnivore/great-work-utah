Support the Sundance Institute
Founded by Robert Redford in 1981, the Sundance Institute is a 501c3 nonprofit organization dedicated to fostering new voices in American storytelling and championing independent stories for the screen.
Make sure you’re a part of the transformative impact of the Sundance Institute, where support such as granting, labs, and invaluable mentorship has served a crucial catalyst for filmmakers for over 40 years.
Join us in lifting up the next generation of artists and storytellers by becoming a member , joining Donor Circles , or by making a donation today.
