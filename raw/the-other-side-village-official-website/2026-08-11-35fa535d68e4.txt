Our Mission
The Other Side Village is a therapeutic community that helps individuals move beyond chronic homelessness through permanent housing, personal accountability, meaningful work, and authentic human connection.
Our Beliefs
About Us
The Other Side Village is a master-planned, therapeutic community located in Salt Lake City, Utah. Nestled in a tranquil setting, the Village provides high-quality, permanent housing and a highly supportive environment for individuals transitioning out of chronic homelessness. This unique neighborhood combines affordable housing with a vibrant, peer-led culture that fosters healing, personal growth, and connection.
Guided by principles of dignity, accountability, and empowerment, The Other Side Village offers more than a place to live—it creates a space to thrive. Here, residents become active participants in their own transformation, surrounded by a supportive network that prioritizes mental health, personal development, and community engagement.

Permanent Housing Meets Purposeful Living
At The Other Side Village, we offer more than a roof over your head—we provide the foundation for transformation. Here’s how we help our residents rebuild their lives:
Prep School
A preliminary stabilization program designed to ensure residents are prepared for independent living. This essential first step focuses on mental health stabilization, accountability, and life skills.
Supportive Community
Residents are surrounded by a peer-led, therapeutic culture that promotes connection, growth, and shared responsibility.
Holistic Support
Access to social services, mental health resources, and volunteer-led programs that empower individuals to overcome barriers like substance use and trauma.
Purposeful Engagement
Residents contribute to the Village through work, service, and personal development while rediscovering their self-worth and independence.
