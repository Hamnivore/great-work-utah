About Us
We are
a 45-year industry veteran
We make long-lasting diamond cutters and bearings for applications in down-hole drilling tools. We press our own polycrystalline diamond (PCD) and finish each part to exact specifications to give you the confidence you need that your high-performance product will hold up under extreme conditions.
We are a 45-year industry veteran and a leader in the development and production of polycrystalline diamond cutters (PDCs) for oil and gas exploration. We are focused on an uncompromising commitment to constant innovation, outstanding quality, and superior customer service, allowing our engineering teams to meet high performance standards and match the requirements of the world’s most demanding oil exploration and development projects.
Since 1978, we’ve been working to deliver superior drilling components for down hole, oil drill bits. We’re located in Orem, Utah.
We are a family-oriented business with a unique company culture that values and encourages honesty, unity, respect, hard work, friendship, and an entrepreneurial spirit. We recognize the right of every employee to be properly trained, have access to the right equipment, tools, and materials, and work in a safe environment. This positive culture and our commitment to the well-being of our employees makes US Synthetic an extraordinarily fun and rewarding place to work.
For more information on any of our business unit brands (including US Synthetic Bearings , US Synthetic Mining , and DiamondBack Nozzles ), contact us today or read more about our technology advantage.
What we do
As an industry leader in the development and production of polycrystalline diamond cutters (PDCs) for oil and gas exploration, we’re dedicated to an uncompromising commitment to constant innovation, outstanding quality and superior customer service allows us to meet the highest performance standards and match the requirements of the world’s most demanding oil exploration and development projects.
We’re focused on the development of diamond inserts for use in drill bits for oil and gas exploration. We also provide a variety of diamond-enhanced components for use in downhole exploration and production.
We employ approximately 400 people and are located in Orem, Utah.
