Questions sign shops ask
What Vuely does, what it costs, and where it fits in your workflow.
What is Vuely?
Vuely is signage software built for sign companies. It replaces the scattered mix of notepads, phone cameras, spreadsheets, and design files that most shops use to sell a sign. One project holds the site survey, the AI-generated mockup, the price estimate, and the signed proposal, so the job is ready for production the moment the client says yes.
How does Vuely turn a site survey into a sign mockup?
On site, your rep photographs the storefront and captures measurements against a guided checklist. Vuely uses those measurements to scale the space, then generates a photo-accurate mockup of the proposed sign on the real building. Change the sign type, size, or finish and a new mockup renders in seconds, so you can iterate with the client standing next to you instead of booking a second visit.
How accurate are the mockups and measurements?
Mockups are scaled from the measurements captured during the survey rather than eyeballed, and AI-assisted scaling helps when a reference dimension is hard to reach. The result is accurate enough to sell from and to hand to production. It is a sales and approval tool, not a substitute for final engineered drawings.
Can I price the job and send the proposal from the same project?
Yes. Pricing estimation runs off the same project data as the mockup, so the number your client sees reflects the sign you just showed them. From there you send a polished proposal, capture an e-signature on site or remotely, and export the approved job straight into your production workflow.
How long does it take to go from site visit to a signed proposal?
Minutes rather than days. Shops using Vuely save 10+ hours per project by collapsing the survey, mockup, estimate, and proposal into one pass, and report closing around 30% more deals because the client can approve the sign while the rep is still on site.
Do I need to install anything, and does it work on my phone on site?
No install. Vuely runs in any modern browser, so the same account works on a phone or tablet during the survey and on a desktop back at the shop. Reps capture photos and measurements from the phone they already carry.
How much does Vuely cost?
Basic is $249 per month for a single location and 2 seats. Pro is $699 per month for 5 seats and higher project and mockup limits. Business is $1,499 per month for up to 3 locations with unlimited seats and mockups. Enterprise is custom priced for large, high-volume signage teams and includes dedicated onboarding, system integration, and priority support. Annual billing saves 10%, and monthly plans can be cancelled anytime.
Does Vuely replace our design or production software?
No. Vuely handles the part of the job that happens before production, which is the survey, the mockup a client approves, the price, and the signature. Approved jobs export into the workflow and tooling you already use to design, fabricate, and install.
Is Vuely a fit for a small shop, or only larger sign companies?
Both. Basic is sized for a newer shop running a single location, and plans scale up to multi-location teams with unlimited seats. The pitch is the same either way: a small shop feels a single lost deal more sharply, and showing the sign before it exists is what keeps that deal from walking.
