Changing Lives for the Better by Creating Pathways to Opportunity
Founded in 1997 by 19 U.S. governors, Western Governors University is the nation’s leading nonprofit institution providing accessible high-quality education at a low cost.
From day one, WGU’s online, competency-based model has opened higher education pathways for students who previously lacked access due to location or other barriers. Through student-centered design and tech-enabled innovation, WGU continues to meet students where they are, delivering economic mobility at scale.

<b><span class=\"font-size-medium\">Affordability</span></b></h3>\n<p><span class=\"font-size-small\">WGU’s low, flat-rate tuition model means students pay once every six months and complete as many courses as they can in that time. Because there’s no cap on how fast students can accelerate, many complete their courses in less time and for a much lower cost compared to other universities.</span></p>\n"}}" id="text-4834dea166" class="cmp-text">
Affordability
WGU’s low, flat-rate tuition model means students pay once every six months and complete as many courses as they can in that time. Because there’s no cap on how fast students can accelerate, many complete their courses in less time and for a much lower cost compared to other universities.
Learn More About Affordability
<b><span class=\"font-size-medium\">Competency-Based Education</span></b></h3>\n<p><span class=\"font-size-small\">Competency-based education (CBE) measures demonstrated skills rather than time spent in a classroom, with learning outcomes aligned to industry demands. WGU is the only institution offering competency-based degrees at scale, creating a model other colleges and universities are increasingly striving to replicate.</span></p>\n"}}" id="text-74a4ba0ade" class="cmp-text">
Competency-Based Education
Competency-based education (CBE) measures demonstrated skills rather than time spent in a classroom, with learning outcomes aligned to industry demands. WGU is the only institution offering competency-based degrees at scale, creating a model other colleges and universities are increasingly striving to replicate.
Learn More About CBE
<b><span class=\"font-size-medium\">Equity</span></b></h3>\n<p><span class=\"font-size-small\">Since its inception, WGU has endeavored to advance equity by increasing access to higher education through affordable tuition, online learning options and competency-based education. We strive to expand access, without excluding anyone, by addressing barriers of affordability, location, and time. In addition, we advance equity by improving attainment through personalizing instruction, mentorship, and support so each student might have an equal probability of success. &nbsp;</span></p>\n"}}" id="text-11d322836c" class="cmp-text">
Equity
Since its inception, WGU has endeavored to advance equity by increasing access to higher education through affordable tuition, online learning options and competency-based education. We strive to expand access, without excluding anyone, by addressing barriers of affordability, location, and time. In addition, we advance equity by improving attainment through personalizing instruction, mentorship, and support so each student might have an equal probability of success.
