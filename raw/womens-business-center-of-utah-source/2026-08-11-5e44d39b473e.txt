Real Stories from Utah Women
“As a new business owner, stepping out of your comfort zone is scary but with WBCUtah by your side, it feels like having a friend helping you grow and do more!”
Malavika Deo, founder of Deolicious LLC & Spice Symphony, turned her love for Indian cooking into a thriving business with preservative-free spice blends and meal prep. Since connecting with WBCUtah in 2018, she’s gained training, launched her brands, and even hired her first employee!
“WBCUtah is such a supportive community. The networking is fantastic, and their resources keep us informed and on track!”
Adventure meets empowerment at Climb Moab, a community-driven climbing gym founded by Britt Zale and Kaya Lindsay in Moab, Utah. Their business is more than a gym, it’s a space where climbers of all levels come together to challenge themselves, build confidence, and embrace the thrill of movement.
“I built this business to nourish others and WBCUtah helped me believe I could through their extensive mentorship and support.”
Kaitlyn Maestas, founder of Raw Eddy’s Superfood Snacks and Dopamine Space in Salt Lake City, launched her wellness-centered brand during the pandemic with a mission to connect, comfort, and care through food. Kaitlyn credits the Women’s Business Center of Utah with providing the support and confidence she needed to grow from idea to impact.
