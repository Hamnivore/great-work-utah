Zanskar Geothermal
Always On Energy. Delivered Today.
With the majority of the world's geothermal resources still untapped, Zanskar uses data and an AI-driven playbook to unlock this limitless energy source at scale.
what we do
Abundant energy is here today, buried beneath our feet.
Geothermal energy is affordable, carbon-free, and capable of delivering 24/7 power at massive scale. But most of it remains hidden. Zanskar is on a mission to find and harness this underground energy, turning the earth’s heat into a cornerstone of our future power grid. Our projects are online today, delivering firm, utility-scale power and paving the way for geothermal to realize its true potential.
Zanskar is the first AI-native geothermal energy company.
We use modern geoscience, artificial intelligence, and advanced field and drilling methods to accurately map the Earth’s subsurface and develop geothermal resources that have been overlooked or considered too risky.
By transforming geothermal exploration and development into a repeatable, data-driven process, we're opening up thousands of new sites across the western U.S. with the potential to deliver energy at terawatt scale.
