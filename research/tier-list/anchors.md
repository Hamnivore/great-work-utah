# Tier list — calibration anchors

**Generated working file, 2026-08-11. Not a wiki page. The normative rubric is `wiki/meta/tiers.md`
(with the metric it rests on in `wiki/meta/charter.md`) — read both first; this file only fixes the
scale so that independent raters land in the same place.**

Every anchor below was placed by hand against the rubric. If your page is clearly above one anchor
and clearly below another, its tier is between them. **Anchor first, then rubric, then judgment** —
in that order, because the failure mode of this exercise is fifteen raters each inventing their own
scale and producing a ladder nobody can read.

## The one-sentence version

Impact = displacement in joy: depth × breadth, permanence dominates, **ranked by the maximum
estimated bounds of the displacement rather than by whether it is good or bad**, and always
counterfactual — the world with this entity minus the world without it, not the entity's own account
of its importance.

## Anchors, highest to lowest

### S — world-historic

- `utah-computer-graphics-program` — Catmull to Pixar, Warnock to Adobe, Clark to SGI and Netscape,
  Kay to PARC, Gouraud and Phong shading, the Utah teapot. Every rendered frame descends from it, and
  the counterfactual is strong because it was *the* school rather than a school.
- `capecchi-gene-targeting` — the knockout mouse; the technique is in every mammalian genetics lab on
  earth and is the substrate of modern disease modeling.
- `myriad-genetics-brca` — millions of actionable clinical results, **and** *AMP v. Myriad*
  permanently redrew what can be patented in the United States. The patent enforcement that
  suppressed competing tests until 2013 is counted as part of how far it moved the world, not subtracted for being ugly.
- `northrop-grumman-promontory` — the boosters under every Artemis mission and the solid stages of the
  nuclear deterrent, from a near-monopoly site.
- `utah-population-database` — the linked genealogy-plus-medical-records instrument that is *why*
  hereditary disease genetics happened here; BRCA is downstream of it.
- `bingham-canyon-mine` — copper that electrified a country, a permanent excavation, a contaminated
  aquifer.
- `jarvik-7-artificial-heart`, `utah-array-bci-platform`, `golden-spike-transcontinental-railroad`.

  counterfactual.
- `intermountain-clinical-quality-improvement`, `recursion-pharmaceuticals`, `blackrock-neurotech`,

## The five mistakes this exercise keeps making

1. **Grading on a Utah curve.** "Big for Utah" is not a tier. The scale is the world.
2. **Rewarding throughput instead of counterfactual.** An accelerator that ten good companies passed
   through did not cause ten good companies.
3. **Promoting a large unverified claim.** No evidence means no estimable bounds, which means the
   bottom of the ladder — not a large claim politely discounted.
4. **Demoting an unfinished bet to its current revenue.** Tier is the ceiling. That is the entire
   point of rule 2.
5. **Flinching because the work is ugly.** Weapons, surveillance, extraction, and waste disposal rank
   on how far they could move the world. Whether that's a blessing or a disaster goes in the page's
   prose, never into the letter.
