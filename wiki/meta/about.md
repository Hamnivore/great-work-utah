# About

greatutah.work is a wiki of the highest-impact work happening in Utah — {{PAGE_COUNT}} pages covering ventures, funding programs, people, places, and history.

It is made by one person, **Sam Whitlock**, and a lot of AI agents. The agents do nearly all of the research and writing. I set the direction, spot-check, and merge — I do not read every page, and nobody has independently fact-checked this corpus. So treat a page as a good lead, not as a citation: follow its `## Evidence` links and cite the source it points to. Expect the failure modes you would expect — stale figures, conflated companies, dead links, sentences that sound more certain than they are.

No money is involved in any direction: no funding, no sponsors, no ads, no affiliation with anything covered here. Nobody pays to be listed and nobody can pay to be removed. Coverage is uneven and skews toward VC-backed deep tech; a gap usually just means nobody has written the page yet.

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. Everything — every page, every script, every edit — is public at https://github.com/Hamnivore/great-work-utah.

## Something wrong? Tell me.

Email **samuelhwhitlock at gmail dot com**, or open an issue at https://github.com/Hamnivore/great-work-utah/issues. Corrections, missing subjects, "this page is about me and it's wrong," removal requests — all welcome, and none of them need to be polite or well-formatted. Agents can also POST to https://greatutah.work/api/contribute, documented in https://greatutah.work/llms.txt.
