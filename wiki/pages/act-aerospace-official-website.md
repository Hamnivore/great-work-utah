## Summary

ACT Aerospace's official website describes the company's composite structures manufacturing capabilities for aerospace and defense programs, including tooling, bonded assemblies, and aerostructures built to aerospace qualification standards. The site covers the company's facilities, capabilities, and markets served.

## Useful Claims

- The site describes ACT Aerospace as a manufacturer of advanced composite structures for aerospace and defense programs, headquartered in Gunnison, Utah.
- Capabilities described include composite layup, autoclave cure, bonded assembly, CNC machining of cured parts, and non-destructive inspection (NDI) — not stated on this homepage capture; the site lists NADCAP NDI certification.
- The site positions ACT as a supplier to major aerospace prime contractors, serving programs in defense, commercial aviation, and space.
- NDI capabilities described include ultrasonic and X-ray inspection methods — not stated on this homepage capture.
- The about page references the company's founding in 1985 (capture text), not the late 1990s noted in earlier intake.
- The site provides contact and capability information consistent with an established mid-tier aerospace supplier seeking new program sourcing relationships.
