## Summary

The foundation's mission page, naming Thame, Nepal as the starting point for its work.

## Useful Claims

- Mission quote: **"Without an education, we have no choice." – Apa Sherpa**
- The foundation's stated mission is to **empower individuals throughout the world to follow the example of Apa Sherpa in overcoming adversity**.
- Work begins in **Apa's home village in Thame, Nepal**, and expands outward from there.
- The page repeats the self-reported superlative that Apa Sherpa **"rose to become the greatest climber in the history of Mount Everest."**
