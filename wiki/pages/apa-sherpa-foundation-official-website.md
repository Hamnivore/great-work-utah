## Summary

The Apa Sherpa Foundation's public homepage as captured 2026-08-11. It describes the organization's Nepal education mission and publishes a Salt Lake City post-office contact; it does not state a summit count.

## Useful Claims

- The foundation describes itself as a **non-profit organization on a mission to support disadvantaged individuals in Nepal**, providing **food, shelter, clothing, and schooling**.
- The homepage quotes Apa Sherpa: **"WITHOUT EDUCATION WE HAVE NO CHOICE."**
- The site states the foundation has **since 2010** "relentlessly striven to provide a positive path for the people of Nepal."
- Contact published on the homepage: **PO Box 711116, Salt Lake City, UT 84171**, phone **801-735-9403**.
- The footer states the entity is a **nonprofit corporation exempt from federal income tax under Section 501(c)(3)**.
- The homepage calls Apa Sherpa **"the greatest climber in the history of Mount Everest"** — a superlative on the foundation's own site, not a counted record.
