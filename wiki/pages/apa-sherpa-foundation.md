## Summary

The Apa Sherpa Foundation is a Utah 501(c)(3) that funds education and basic necessities for disadvantaged people in Nepal, starting from founder Apa Sherpa's home village of Thame in the Everest region. Its public materials center on schooling as the lever — the founder's line is "Without an education, we have no choice" ([mission page](apa-sherpa-foundation-mission-page.md)).

The legal entity registers in **Lehi, Utah** (EIN 27-2198169) while the website publishes a **Salt Lake City PO Box** for donations ([IRS record](apa-sherpa-foundation-form-990-record.md), [official site](apa-sherpa-foundation-official-website.md)). Apa Sherpa himself lives in **Draper**, Utah ([Wikipedia reference capture](wikipedia-apa-sherpa-reference.md)) — a separate fact from the foundation's registered address.

## Who It's For

Disadvantaged children and families in Nepal — especially around Thame in the Khumbu — who lack reliable access to schooling and basic necessities. Donors and volunteers supporting that work from Utah and abroad.

## What It Provides

From the foundation's own captured pages: food, shelter, clothing, and schooling; fundraising treks and programs under headings such as Teachers, Meals, and community projects ([official site](apa-sherpa-foundation-official-website.md)). The mission page frames the theory of change as education and experience leading to stronger families and communities, beginning in Thame and expanding outward ([mission page](apa-sherpa-foundation-mission-page.md)).

The portering alternative the lead suggested is not spelled out on the captured pages in those words, but the same materials tie Apa's climbing career to a desire that younger Sherpas have **choices beyond guiding** — a quote captured on [Wikipedia's article](wikipedia-apa-sherpa-reference.md), which the foundation site does not reproduce verbatim.

Scale is modest: IRS filings show roughly **$45,000–$87,000** annual contribution revenue in recent years and assets near **$287,000** ([IRS record](apa-sherpa-foundation-form-990-record.md)).
