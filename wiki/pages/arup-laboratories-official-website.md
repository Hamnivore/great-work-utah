## Summary

ARUP Laboratories' "About" page — the lab describing its own identity, accreditation, scale metrics, and
client services. This is the source for self-reported figures on test-menu size and expert headcount; it
does not publish employee totals, client-hospital counts, or specimen volume.

## Useful Claims

- ARUP describes itself as a **national, academic, nonprofit reference laboratory** and a **nonprofit
  enterprise of the University of Utah and its Department of Pathology**.
- It states **CAP-, ISO 15189-, and CLIA certification** and **more than 40 years** of experience
  serving hospital and health-system clients.
- Its on-page fact sheet reports **3,000+ tests and test combinations**, **150+ academic experts and
  consultants**, **99% testing performed on-site**, and **170,000 continuing education credits awarded
  annually** — all self-reported, undated.
- It says it has **more than 150** pathologists, clinical scientists, variant scientists, and genetic
  counselors available for consultation, with most on-site.
