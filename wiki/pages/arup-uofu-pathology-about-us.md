## Summary

The University of Utah Department of Pathology's "About Us" page, including a short affiliated-organizations
blurb on ARUP. This is university self-description of the ownership relationship and ARUP's role in
clinical and anatomic pathology testing — not an independent audit of ARUP's finances or scale.

## Useful Claims

- The Department of Pathology states that **ARUP Laboratories is owned by the University of Utah**.
- It describes ARUP as a **national reference laboratory** offering **highly complex and unique medical
  tests in clinical and anatomic pathology**.
- It characterizes ARUP as a **worldwide leader in innovative laboratory research and development** —
  the department's own claim, not independently verified here.

## Verbatim

> "ARUP Laboratories is a national reference laboratory and a worldwide leader in innovative laboratory research and development. Owned by the University of Utah, ARUP offers an extensive test menu of highly complex and unique medical tests in clinical and anatomic pathology."
> — "Affiliated Organizations", ARUP Laboratories blurb

> A majority of clinical decisions are based on the results of diagnostic tests performed by pathologists and clinical laboratory scientists, supported by a highly trained workforce of laboratory professionals.
> — Department overview
