## Summary

BambooHR's official homepage describes the HR platform product surface — hiring, payroll, benefits, employee experience, performance, compensation, and integrations — and marketing claims such as customer counts and cost savings. This capture is the homepage only, not the about or leadership pages.

## Useful Claims

- This homepage capture does not state a founding year; BambooHR's about page (not captured here) has historically cited 2008.
- The company sells a cloud-based HR platform for HR teams, employees, and organizations.
- Public product pages cover hiring and onboarding, HR data and reporting, payroll and time, benefits administration, employee experience, performance management, compensation, integrations, and HR automation.
- The leadership page lists Brad Rencher as CEO, Ben Peterson as co-founder, Ryan Sanders as co-founder, and other executive roles including product, finance, customer, technology, revenue, marketing, and legal leadership.
- The FAQ states that BambooHR is an HR software solution rather than a PEO, and that customer data may be hosted in the United States, Canada, or Ireland depending on customer needs and applicable laws.
