## Summary

Best Friends' national no-kill campaign page, captured **2026-08-11 — seven months after the
organization's own 2025 deadline**. This matters for how the page can be used: it is not a record of
the goal as originally stated, it is the record of how Best Friends talks about that goal now that
the date has passed.

The document was requested at `bestfriends.org/no-kill-2025` and served from `bestfriends.org/no-kill`;
its title is "After decades of work, no-kill is within reach, thanks to you." The campaign no longer
names a target year anywhere. The 2016 goal appears only in the past tense.

## Useful Claims

- Best Friends states it set a goal in **2016 to achieve no-kill nationwide by 2025**, and now
  describes the country as "closer than ever" rather than as having met or missed it. **No new target
  date appears on the page.**
- It states shelter killing dropped from **over 1 million cats and dogs in 2016** to **around
  425,000 pets in 2024** (self-reported).
- The headline claims **2 out of every 3 shelters are now no-kill**. The page's own denominator
  elsewhere: **of roughly 3,900 US shelters, 1,300 are not yet no-kill**, though it says nearly half
  of those are within 100 animals of the threshold.
- It states a dog or cat is killed in a US shelter **every 90 seconds**.
- It dates **the first known city to reach no-kill to 1994**.
- A section titled **"Tracking our lifesaving progress with data"** points to "See the data" —
  evidence that Best Friends publishes progress tracking, though this page does not describe the
  dashboard.

## Reliability Notes

Self-reported tier, and campaign copy rather than a report. The shelter-killing counts are Best
Friends' own statistics — strong evidence of what the organization advocates and how it frames
progress, not an independent audit of shelter outcomes.

**Read this capture for what it is: a post-deadline document.** The organization set a public target
of nationwide no-kill by 2025, and as of this 2026-08-11 capture its campaign page has been retitled
around "within reach," recounts the goal in the past tense, and sets no new date. It neither claims
the goal was met nor concedes it was missed. That is a meaningful fact about the campaign and the
reason this page states the capture date so prominently — a reader who assumed this document was
written before the deadline would misread every sentence in it.

Note also the selection effect in the page's own framing. The headline figure is "2 out of every 3
shelters are now no-kill"; the same page's denominator, further down, is that 1,300 of roughly 3,900
shelters are not. Both are quoted above so the ratio can be read either way.

The data-dashboard claim is only partially established: this page references tracking and links to
data but does not describe a national shelter database, and a dedicated dashboard page was not
captured.
