## Summary

Best Friends' public sanctuary landing page — capacity, acreage, location in Kanab, visitor
amenities, and on-site or nearby lodging options. This is the primary source for sanctuary scale
and hospitality operations near Angel Canyon.

## Useful Claims

- Best Friends reports the sanctuary holds **up to 1,600** dogs, cats, birds, bunnies, horses, pigs,
  and other animals on any given day.
- The page calls it **the largest sanctuary of its kind in the U.S.**, spanning **nearly 5,800 acres
  in Angel Canyon** near Kanab, Utah.
- The sanctuary address is **5001 Angel Canyon Road, Kanab, Utah**.
- Visitors may **grab lunch at the café** at the Visitor Center.
- Lodging options named include the **Best Friends Roadhouse and Mercantile** in Kanab town,
  plus **cottages, cabins, or RV sites** on sanctuary grounds.
- Best Friends describes the sanctuary as **the nation's largest no-kill sanctuary for companion
  animals** (footer boilerplate).

## Reliability Notes

Self-reported tier. Capacity, acreage, and "largest" superlatives are the organization's own
marketing language — useful for what Best Friends asserts, not independent proof of scale relative
to other sanctuaries. Lodging and café references establish that hospitality exists; menu, hours,
and ownership structure are not detailed here.
