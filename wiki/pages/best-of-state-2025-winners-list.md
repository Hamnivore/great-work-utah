## Summary

The Best of State Foundation's own 60-page program book for the 2025 awards: the full medal-winner
directory by division, interleaved with paid advertising, plus — and this is why the page exists —
the program's own account of how winners are chosen.

It is the most useful document the award has produced, because it is the only one that states the
structure a reader needs in order to know what a medal means: a medal per category, one Best of State
Statue per division selected from that division's medalists, a hundred-point score split
50/30/20 across three criteria, anonymous volunteer judges, and tabulation by an outside accounting
firm. Any page in this wiki that mentions a Best of State medal should inherit that framing, which is
assembled on [Best of State Awards](best-of-state-awards.md).

## Useful Claims

- **Two different awards, and they are not the same signal.** Winners in each category receive a
  medal. From each of the ten general divisions, a single winner receives the Best of State Statue
  (the BOSS) — selected by the judges from among that division's medal winners.
- **The judges score nominations, not the field.** Judges review the nominations online, evaluate any
  other submitted material, and assign three scores totalling 100 possible points per nominee.
- **The score weighting is stated:** fifty percent for overall achievement and excellence in service,
  products, or performance; thirty percent for innovation, creativity, and differentiation; the
  remaining twenty percent for contributions that make Utah a better place to live.
- **Judges are anonymous volunteers**, recruited through professional associations, universities,
  industry leaders, and chambers of commerce; their names are withheld to prevent lobbying by
  applicants or by other judges.
- **Ballots are tabulated by Gilbert & Stewart**, an outside accounting firm, which then notifies Best
  of State of the medal winners.
- **The program addresses sponsorship directly**, stating that sponsorship does not improve a
  nominee's chances and that sponsors sometimes win and sometimes do not. This is the program's own
  account of itself, not an independent audit.
- **Winners are granted promotional rights** — the right to display the Best of State logo and the
  year of the award on correspondence, their website, and other promotional material. This is the
  mechanism by which a medal ends up quoted on company websites years later.
- **The program dates itself to 2002.** The copyright line reads "© 2002 - 2025 Best of State".
- **One individual holds five 2025 medals across three divisions.** David F. Pack, Ph.D. is listed as
  the 2025 winner for Actor (Arts & Entertainment), Individual Volunteer and Appointed Official
  (Community Development), and College/University Teacher and Adjunct Professor (Education). The
  category headings are single-line labels, too short for the quote checker, so they are named here
  by division rather than quoted below.

## Reliability Notes

**Self-reported.** Everything here is the awarding organization describing its own process. It is
good evidence of what the program says it does and no evidence that the process works as described;
no independent audit of the judging has been located. The sponsorship disclosure in particular is a
claim about integrity made by the party with the incentive.

**The book is also an advertising vehicle, and the advertisers are the winners.** Full-page ads
throughout carry lines like "15x Best of State Winner" alongside a discount code, so the same
document that certifies a winner is partly funded by winners buying space in it. That is a commercial
relationship worth naming; it is not evidence of anything improper, and the program states that
sponsorship does not affect results.

**Reading order is unreliable in two places, because the source is a two-column PDF.** The
sponsorship passage quoted above begins mid-sentence: its opening clause ("sponsors are clearly
informed that sponsorship does not") sits in a later column in the extracted text, roughly 65 lines
after the fragment that continues it. Anyone quoting further from this capture should check whether a
sentence is complete before treating it as one.

**The capture is the same file the Internet Archive holds.** The live document now sits behind a
content-addressed asset path (`/__l5e/assets-v1/…`) rather than the `/pdf/2025_bos_winners.pdf` path
the site used to serve, and that older path 404s today. The two are byte-identical: SHA-256
`9d652597806ffe0bb492b53bf7331cecea91f1e51d704da998a3fc6c861725bb` for both the live asset captured
here and the 2026-04-19 snapshot recorded in `**Archive:**`. So the snapshot genuinely insures this
document rather than an earlier version of it.
