**Type:** guide

Four doors into the wiki. Pick the one that matches why you are here.

## The tier list

[The tier list](/views/tier-list.md) · `https://greatutah.work/views/tier-list.md` ranks every fact page on one impact ladder. S and A are the gems. Names marked (active) are still being done; an unmarked name is historical, quiet, or unchecked.

## Looking for work

[By kind of work](/views/by-role.md) · `https://greatutah.work/views/by-role.md` is the door: organizations grouped by the kind of contribution they say they need. These are leads from page assessments, not a job board — verify with the organization.

## Map

The [map](/map) · `https://greatutah.work/map` is the place to browse Utah geographically. It plots public sites and coarse regional anchors. Statewide-only and unverified locations are omitted.

## Founder resources

[Guides](/views/guides.md) · `https://greatutah.work/views/guides.md` — capital, advisors, SBIR help, workspace, commercialization. Start there rather than the full resource index.
