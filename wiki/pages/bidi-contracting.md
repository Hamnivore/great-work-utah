## Summary

Bidi Contracting builds estimating and bid-management software for general contractors. Its public product includes plan-based estimating, subcontractor discovery, and bid collection rather than treating takeoff as an isolated calculation.

## Impact

Estimating and subcontractor outreach are costly coordination bottlenecks for small and midsize contractors. Bidi's bet is that combining the two workflows can let contractors evaluate more work with less administrative effort. The public evidence establishes a working, actively maintained product, but not yet its adoption or measured effect.

## Utah Context

Co-founder Baylor Jeppsen's public biography places him and the company in Utah County and identifies the venture as a product of BYU's Sandbox entrepreneurship program. The website publishes live contractor directories for Utah markets.

## What They Need Now
