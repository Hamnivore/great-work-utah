## Summary

Blundell is PacifiCorp's (Rocky Mountain Power) geothermal generating station near Milford in Beaver County, Utah — the longest-running conventional geothermal plant in the county's operating cluster. Third-party generation databases put annual output on the order of ~270 GWh, making it larger than Cove Fort or Thermo No. 1 on energy produced. Ormat historically supplied a binary bottoming-cycle expansion unit at Blundell (see [Ormat Technologies](ormat-technologies.md)).

This page treats the **plant / Utah operating site** as the citable atom for talent matching; PacifiCorp corporate strategy lives elsewhere. Job-seekers should use PacifiCorp / Berkshire Hathaway Energy careers channels and filter for Utah generation or geothermal O&M roles rather than expecting a Blundell-branded ATS.

## Impact

Blundell is proof that Utah hydrothermal geothermal can run for decades as utility baseload. That permanence anchors Beaver County's identity as a geothermal corridor and sets the operational baseline next to which [Fervo](fervo-energy.md), [Ormat](ormat-technologies.md), and [Cyrq](cyrq-energy.md) are judged. Bounds are those of a single utility plant: climate impact scales with continued high capacity factor and eventual life-extension or replacement decisions by PacifiCorp.

## Utah Context

Located in the Roosevelt Hot Springs / Milford geothermal area of Beaver County, near the same corridor as Cove Fort, Thermo No. 1, Utah FORGE, and Fervo Cape Station. Workforce overlaps with other plant O&M roles in the county.

## What They Need Now

Utility geothermal plants typically need plant operators, maintenance technicians, electrical and mechanical craft, and occasional engineering support for outages and capital projects. PacifiCorp hiring is corporate — search Rocky Mountain Power / PacifiCorp careers for Utah generation roles rather than a plant-specific board. Clearance is usually not required; rural relocation willingness is.
