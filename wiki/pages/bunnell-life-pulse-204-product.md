## Summary

Bunnell's product page for the **LifePulse 204** high-frequency jet ventilator (HFJV) — the company's
current model. Describes the ventilator's positioning, commercial availability (sell, service, rent),
and the mechanical rationale the company gives for jet ventilation in fragile infants.

## Useful Claims

- The **204 "Jet"** is described as Bunnell's **latest ventilator model**.
- Bunnell **sells, services, and rents** the **204 LifePulse HFJV**.
- The company positions the device for **very small tidal volumes** and **short inspiratory times** to
  achieve **low peak and mean alveolar pressures**.
- Marketing claims include that LifePulse breaths **help prevent air leaks and even allow them to heal**.
- Footer repeats **FDA indications** for PIE and RDS with pulmonary air leaks, including studied
  gestational-age and birth-weight ranges.
