## Summary

Bunnell's "About Bunnell" page — the company's self-description of its founding, founder, products,
Utah manufacturing footprint, and clinical-support model. This is the source for what the company
says about its history and location; FDA regulatory facts belong to the
[PMA record](bunnell-fda-life-pulse-pma.md).

## Useful Claims

- The company states it was **established in 1980** and focuses on **advanced ventilation systems for
  treating premature infants in acute respiratory failure**.
- It describes the **LifePulse® Ventilator** as a **high-frequency jet ventilator** and claims it was
  **the first high-frequency ventilator ever approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)
  for clinical use (1988)** — a marketing superlative; the FDA record confirms a 1988 PMA approval
  but does not, by itself, establish "first ever" among all ventilator types.
- **Founder named: Bert Bunnell, Sc. D.** The page says he developed the Life Pulse HFJV while a
  doctoral candidate working on aerosolized drug treatments for premature infants.
- **Manufacturing:** products are made in a **Salt Lake City facility**; the site footer lists
  **330 Cutler Drive | North Salt Lake, UT 84054**.
- **Markets named:** United States, Canada, Australia, and Malaysia.
- **Ownership (self-reported):** "Bunnell, Inc is now entirely employee-owned."
- **FDA indications** repeated in the site footer: PIE and RDS complicated by pulmonary air leaks in
  critically ill infants, with gestational-age and birth-weight ranges stated.

## Reliability Notes

Self-reported tier. Strong for what the company says it makes, where it manufactures, and who it
names as founder; weak for superlatives ("first," "revolutionary," "set the standard") and for
patient-outcome claims ("helped healthcare professionals save thousands of babies").

The "first high-frequency ventilator ever approved by FDA" claim is partially checkable: openFDA
shows Bunnell's original PMA **P850064** with decision date **1988-06-30**, the earliest original PMA
among product code **LSZ** ("Ventilator, High Frequency") in the database — but other high-frequency
ventilator PMAs from Nellcor, ZOLL, and Covidien followed, so the device category is not exclusive to
Bunnell today.
