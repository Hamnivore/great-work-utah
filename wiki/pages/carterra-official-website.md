## Summary

Carterra's corporate homepage describing its high-throughput surface plasmon resonance platforms for antibody screening and characterization.

## Useful Claims

- Carterra lists a Salt Lake City street address on its homepage footer.
- The homepage markets Carterra LSA, LSA XT, Ultra, and Vega instruments for high-throughput kinetics, epitope binning, and related antibody-discovery workflows.
- Customer testimonials on the homepage name pharmaceutical and biotech users of the LSA platform; the page does not name a headcount or founding year.
