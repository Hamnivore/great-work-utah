## Summary

Company-issued announcement of the rename from Wasatch Microfluidics, Inc. to Carterra, Inc., and of the forthcoming Carterra LSA high-throughput monoclonal-antibody characterization platform. The captured post header is dated **05.01.17**.

## Useful Claims

- Wasatch Microfluidics, Inc. announced a corporate name change to Carterra, Inc. in Salt Lake City, Utah; the captured news post header reads **05.01.17**.
- The release describes the Carterra LSA as a high-throughput mAb characterization platform using patented microfluidics, SPRi detection, and software; it was slated for availability in Q1 2018.
- The release states Carterra was founded in 2005 and based in Salt Lake City, UT.
