## Summary

CHOICE Humanitarian is the public brand of **Center For Humanitarian Outreach And Intercultural
Exchange** (EIN **74-2494806**), a Utah nonprofit with IRS exemption since **1988** and a **Draper**
office ([990 record](choice-humanitarian-form-990-record.md)). The homepage captured **2026-08-11**
prominently states **"CHOICE Humanitarian Ceases U.S. Operations"**
([official site](choice-humanitarian-official-website.md)).

The lead placed the organization in **West Jordan**; primary records list **Draper** (392 E 12300 S).

## Impact

The village-led development model in the lead matches how the organization describes itself — rural
communities, poverty disruption, expedition travel — and how **James B. Mayfield** is described as
**cofounder** and **University of Utah professor emeritus** on the university's instructor page
([U of U profile](university-of-utah-james-mayfield-instructor-profile.md)). That page names **nine
countries** where he led expeditions; the homepage says **seven countries** for program impact
([official site](choice-humanitarian-official-website.md)) — different speakers, not reconciled.

Financial scale from filed 990 data (API structured year **2023**): revenue **$3.34M**, expenses
**$4.34M**, assets **$1.04M** ([990 record](choice-humanitarian-form-990-record.md)) — mid-size NGO,
not a micro-charity.

The most consequential disproof for a 2026 reader: **U.S. operations status.** The banner says U.S.
operations **cease**; the dedicated blog post URL did not resolve to separate content when probed. What
continues internationally, if anything, is **unresolved** on captured evidence.

**Bet:** that village-led, multi-year facilitation beats one-shot aid — if local leadership capacity
is the measured output, not dollars shipped.
