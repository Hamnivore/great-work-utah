## Summary

CleanJoule's public website at cleanjoule.com, describing its biomanufactured sustainable aviation fuel (CycloSAF) and a feedstock-flexible biochemical process it says yields higher energy density than petroleum-derived equivalents. This capture is the homepage only: product specifications, investor relationships, and dated milestones reported elsewhere are not established by this document. The [GenZero investment in CleanJoule](genzero-cleanjoule-50m.md) is separately documented.

## Useful Claims

- CleanJoule's public website describes CycloSAF as a higher-energy-density sustainable aviation fuel alternative to Jet A, with ASTM International qualification in progress.
- This homepage capture does not state SpaceSAF rocket propellant specs, named airline/strategic investors, a DoD partnership, Salt Lake City headquarters, founding date, or manufacturing-timeline targets; those details were previously listed from other site pages and press releases and are unconfirmed here.
