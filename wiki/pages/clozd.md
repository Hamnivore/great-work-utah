## Summary

Clozd is a Lehi enterprise-software and research-services company focused on decision intelligence: learning why customers buy, decline, renew, or leave. It combines expert-led interviews, AI-assisted and AI-led interviews, CRM and sales-team inputs, and software that organizes qualitative feedback for revenue, marketing, customer-success, and product teams.

Andrew Peterson and Spencer Dent founded Clozd in 2017 after encountering the limits of conventional win-loss data. The company says its platform has been shaped by more than 50,000 interviews. In an earlier company announcement, Clozd reported a $52 million Series A led by Greycroft with Madrona Venture Group and Album VC; the figure and customer counts remain self-reported.

## Impact

Companies routinely make product, pricing, and go-to-market decisions from CRM notes and internal anecdotes that omit the buyer's actual reasoning. Clozd's useful intervention is a repeatable feedback loop: contact buyers after consequential decisions, capture candid qualitative evidence, synthesize recurring drivers, and route those findings back to teams that can act.

That can reduce wasted product and sales effort when the interviews are representative and the analysis is trustworthy. Clozd's current technical bet is that AI can expand the volume and speed of qualitative research without losing the follow-up questions and nuance that make interviews useful. The tradeoff is consequential: automated synthesis can create false confidence if sampling, consent, transcription, or model interpretation is weak. Public materials describe the workflow but do not provide an independent accuracy evaluation.
