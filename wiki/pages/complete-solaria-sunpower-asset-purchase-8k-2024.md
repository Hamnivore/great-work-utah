## Summary

Complete Solaria, Inc. Form 8-K filed 2024-08-06 (accession **0001213900-24-065369**, CIK
**0001838987**) describing its stalking-horse asset purchase agreement for SunPower's **Blue Raven
Solar** business and related assets during SunPower's Chapter 11 cases.

## Useful Claims

- On **August 5, 2024**, Complete Solaria entered a stalking-horse **asset purchase agreement** with
  **SunPower Corporation** and subsidiaries for assets related to the debtors' **Blue Raven Solar
  business**, New Homes business, and non-installing dealer business.
- The transaction was subject to SunPower's **Chapter 11** cases and **Bankruptcy Court** approval,
  with a bidding process for higher offers.
- Exhibit 10.1 to the same filing defines **"Blue Raven Business"** as the solar business offered by
  Blue Raven HoldCo and subsidiaries, excluding Albatross Software except for a licensed CRM.

## Verbatim

> "On August 5, 2024, Complete Solaria, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “ Company ”, “ we ” and “ us ”), entered into a “Stalking Horse” asset purchase agreement with SunPower Corporation (“ SunPower ”) and the direct and indirect subsidiaries of SunPower (the “ Debtors ”) providing for the sale and purchase of certain assets related to the Debtors’ Blue Raven Solar business, New Homes business, and non-installing Dealer network"
