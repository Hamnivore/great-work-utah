## Summary

Crumbl's franchising page describes the company's franchise model, founding story, weekly rotating
menu, and tech-driven operations. Captured 2026-08-11 via manual fetch after the project's capture
script received a bot block on `crumbl.com`.

## Useful Claims

- Crumbl says cousins **Jason McGowan** and **Sawyer Hemsley** founded the company in **Logan, Utah**,
  with **EST. 2017** on the page.
- The franchising page advertises a **weekly rotating menu**, an open-concept kitchen, delivery,
  catering, and an app-driven ordering experience.
- Crumbl describes itself as a **franchise** system; any offer is made only through a **Franchise
  Disclosure Document** from **Crumbl Franchising, LLC** (stated on the page footer).
- The page lists a **$200,000 minimum liquidity** requirement for franchise applicants.
- Counter widgets labeled "Locations worldwide" and "Employees at HQ" rendered as **0** in the plain
  text capture — not quotable store or headcount figures.
