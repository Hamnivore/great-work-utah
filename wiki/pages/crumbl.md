## Summary

Crumbl is a dessert franchise system built around large format cookies, a **weekly rotating menu**,
and tech-heavy ordering ([franchising page](crumbl-franchising-official-page.md)). Cousins **Jason
McGowan** and **Sawyer Hemsley** say they founded the company in **Logan, Utah in 2017** before
expanding into franchising.

Utah's node in the story is real but split: the **founding city is Logan**, while Wisconsin's franchise
registration lists franchisor **Crumbl Franchising, LLC** at **2570 W 600 N, Suite 200, Lindon, UT
84042** ([Wisconsin DFI record](wisconsin-dfi-crumbl-franchise-registration.md)).

## Impact

The lead hypothesis — that Crumbl became one of the fastest-growing U.S. franchise systems on a
social-media-native model — is **not verified here**. No Franchise Disclosure Document, store census, or
court docket was captured in this shard. What the sources do support is the **operating model** the
lead points at: franchising with a **weekly rotating menu**, app-driven ordering, and explicit FDD
gating ([franchising page](crumbl-franchising-official-page.md)).

The Wisconsin registration confirms the **legal franchisor** and a **Lindon headquarters address**,
but also shows filing status **Expired** on the capture date — so it is evidence of what Wisconsin
published, not a live registration certificate ([Wisconsin DFI](wisconsin-dfi-crumbl-franchise-registration.md)).

**Bet:** that rotating scarcity (new flavors weekly) plus delivery/catering rails matters as much as
the cookie — a content cadence franchised at national scale.

The honest counterweight: without an FDD extract or audited store counts, **scale and growth rate are
open**. The company's own counter widgets rendered as zero in capture and were refused as figures.

## Utah Context

Crumbl is a visible Utah County consumer brand: founded in **Cache Valley**, franchisor HQ in
**Lindon**, and a footprint that reads as national/global in the company's own franchising copy. It is
not deep tech, but it is a Utah-origin franchise system whose mechanics (FDD, state registrations,
franchise sales) are inspectable public records — when the registries cooperate.
