## Summary

The Utah chapter page of CTO Breakfast, a multi-city organization whose site carries one page per
local community. It is the only located source that states the chapter's age, its meeting cadence and
venue, and — the substantive part — the five norms the group runs under.

The page is dated **April 13, 2026** on its own face, which matters: it is a standing description of a
recurring meeting rather than a notice for one date, so it goes stale quietly rather than visibly.

## Useful Claims

- The Utah chapter **"has been running for over 20 years"**, gathering CTOs, CIOs, and technology
  leaders.
- It meets **every second Friday at 8:00 AM at the IHOP in American Fork, Utah**.
- Five norms govern the meeting, stated on the page: **go dutch** (everyone pays their own breakfast,
  tip well); **one conversation at a time** (so everyone can participate); **Chatham House Rules**
  (remarks attributed to the room, not the individual); **no politics or religion**; and **intros
  every time** — name, company, role, at every meeting.
- Joining the Slack community requires **emailing a short blurb about yourself** rather than an open
  invite link.
- A separate mailing list exists for in-person event notifications.
- CTO Breakfast is **not Utah-specific**: the site is organized around multiple communities and
  invites readers to start one.

## Reliability Notes

Self-reported tier: this is the organizers' description of their own meeting. It establishes the
**stated** format and norms, and nothing else — not attendance, not whether the norms are observed,
not whether any given month's breakfast happened.

Two specific cautions:

- **"Over 20 years" is unsourced and unaudited.** No founding date, founder, or continuity record is
  given anywhere on the page, and none was located elsewhere. It is a round claim in a promotional
  register, and the chapter's own scheduling messages show at least one month departing from the
  stated cadence — so treat the *number* as approximate even though the longevity is plausible.
- **The stated cadence is not the observed cadence.** The page says "every second Friday" flatly. The
  organizer's own 2026 announcements moved one month to a first Friday and labelled it a change, which
  the page never reflects. See [the 2026 announcements](cto-breakfast-utah-2026-announcements.md).

The norms are the durable content here. They are unusual enough to be worth preserving verbatim —
Chatham House Rules in particular is a specific, named convention with real consequences for what
attendees can repeat afterward, and it is the kind of detail that disappears without a capture.
