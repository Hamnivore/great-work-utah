## Summary

CTO Breakfast Utah is a monthly, open, in-person gathering of technology leaders — CTOs, CIOs, VPs of engineering, and people doing those jobs without those titles. CTO Breakfast Utah provides a bounded benefit: Working technology leaders in Utah County and along the Wasatch Front: CTOs, CIOs, engineering directors, and founders carrying the technical load themselves.

## Who It's For

Working technology leaders in Utah County and along the Wasatch Front: CTOs, CIOs, engineering
directors, and founders carrying the technical load themselves. It is a peer group rather than a
learning event — the value is in the room, so someone looking to be taught will find little, and
someone with a live problem and no one to compare notes with will find a lot.

It is **open**: there is no membership, application, or vetting, and the invitation to bring a friend
is standing.

## What It Provides

A monthly, recurring, agenda-less conversation among people with comparable jobs — and the norms that
make that conversation possible. From the chapter page, in the organizers' own words:

- **Chatham House Rules** — what is said is attributed to the room, not the individual.
- **One conversation at a time** — so the whole table can participate rather than fragmenting.
- **We go dutch** — everyone pays for their own breakfast, and tips well.
- **No politics or religion** — the stated boundary is technology leadership.
- **Intros every time** — name, company, role, at every meeting, so newcomers are never lost.

Chatham House Rules is the load-bearing one. It is what lets an attendee describe a failing vendor, a
layoff being planned, or a technical decision going badly, and the reason a bare summary of "monthly
breakfast for CTOs" understates what the meeting is. It also means **no record of the discussions
exists or should be expected** — including in this wiki.

The absence of sponsorship is structural rather than incidental: no one buys the room, so no one gets
to pitch it.

## How To Use It

Show up. Second Friday, 8:00 AM, IHOP in American Fork; bring a topic. Everyone pays their own way, so
attendance costs the price of breakfast and nothing else.

Two practical notes:

- **Confirm the date before you go.** The published cadence is a flat "every second Friday", but in
  2026 the June meeting moved to the first Friday and returned to the second in July. The move was
  announced only in the Forge Utah Slack `#localevents` channel, where the organizer posts a reminder
  each month, and the website never reflected it.
- **Joining the Slack community requires an email** with a short introduction, rather than an open
  invite link — a deliberate, if small, gate.

## Cost / Eligibility

Free to attend; you buy your own breakfast. No membership, application, or affiliation is required,
and the group is explicitly open to guests.

## Best Fits

Someone senior enough that their hard problems are not technical-first — hiring, vendor selection,
team structure, when to rewrite — and who has no peer inside their own company to test a decision
against.
The Chatham House framing makes it usable for exactly the conversations that cannot be had at a
conference or in a public channel.

It is a poor fit for anyone looking to sell, recruit at scale, or be taught: there is no stage, and
the norms are pointed away from all three.

## Open Questions

- **Is "over 20 years" accurate?** It is the chapter's own round number with no founding date,
  founder, or continuity record attached, and nothing independent was located. The longevity is
  plausible; the figure is not evidenced.
- **Who organizes it, and under what entity, if any?** Derek Carter posts the monthly announcements,
  but no organizing body, registration, or funding arrangement was located. The multi-city
  `ctobreakfast.com` site invites readers to start a community, which suggests a loose federation
  rather than an organization.
- **How many people attend?** No attendance figure exists anywhere, and given Chatham House Rules,
  none is likely to.
- **Why did June 2026 move to the first Friday?** The organizer flagged the change but not the reason.
- **How does the Utah chapter relate to the other CTO Breakfast communities?** Whether anything is
  shared beyond the name and the norms is unestablished.
