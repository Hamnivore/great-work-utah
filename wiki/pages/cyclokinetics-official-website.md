## Summary

CycloKinetics' official website presents the company as a Utah aerospace and defense developer of high-density hydrocarbon propellants.

## Useful Claims

- The company markets CycloJP for aircraft, CycloRP for rocket applications, and CK-10 for missiles.
- It describes the products as higher-energy-density alternatives to conventional petroleum fuels.
- It identifies Salt Lake City as the center of development and manufacturing.

## Verbatim

> "CycloKinetics manufactures a suite of high-performance, 100% drop-in liquid propellants engineered to enhance mission capability across aerospace and defense applications."
> — Home page, "Products"

> "CycloJP is a high energy density replacement for conventional aerospace fuels, including Jet A, JP-5, JP-8, and JPTS, designed to enhance performance across both manned and unmanned aircraft platforms."
> — Home page, "CycloJP"

> "CycloRP is a high-performance, drop-in replacement for RP-1 and RP-2 kerosene-based rocket fuels, designed to improve launch efficiency and payload capacity."
> — Home page, "CycloRP"

> "CK-10 is a next-generation replacement for JP-10, engineered to enhance the performance of long-range missile platforms."
> — Home page, "CK-10"

> "The company's industrial manufacturing facility in Salt Lake City, Utah became operational in 2025, with deliveries beginning under Department of War contracts in Q2 2025."
> — Home page, "Manufacturing"

## Reliability Notes

This is company marketing. Product names and public positioning are directly supported, but performance, delivery, contract, and platform claims require government records or independent testing.
