## Summary

Cyrq Energy operates geothermal power plants in the western United States, including Thermo No. 1 (also called Thermo 1) near Minersville / Milford in Beaver County, Utah. Thermo No. 1 adds firm renewable megawatts from a known hydrothermal resource.

## Impact

Thermo No. 1 adds firm renewable megawatts from a known hydrothermal resource. Impact is real but incremental versus enhanced-geothermal upside narratives — the plant has been producing since about 2013. Cyrq's broader portfolio (including power that Cyrq says supplies University of Utah / Heber City via other plants) matters more as an employer and offtake pattern than as a Utah-only story.

## Utah Context

Thermo sits in the Escalante Valley / Thermo Hot Springs area of southwestern Utah. Public technical papers co-authored by Cyrq and Ormat staff describe ongoing reservoir modeling for field management. Ormat historically supplied equipment / EPC context for the plant's development path (see Ormat page Open Questions / history).
