## Summary

The Deseret Book website is the primary consumer-facing presence for the publishing and retail arm of the Deseret Management Corporation. Bonneville International's website (bonneville.com) covers the radio and broadcasting operations. Together these sites describe the scope of the LDS Church's media holdings — publishing, retail, radio, and television — and serve as the primary public-facing presence for each property.
