## Summary

April 2014 Deseret News report on Weld North Holdings' acquisition of Imagine Learning, describing the company's Provo base and language-and-literacy focus at that time.

## Useful Claims

- In April 2014, Imagine Learning was described as a Provo-based language and literacy company acquired by Weld North Holdings; company officials said it would remain in Provo with no immediate management changes.
- Company officials told the paper the software was used in 6,500 schools nationwide and served English language learners, struggling readers, and early childhood students.
- Founder Susan Preator told the reporter revenue grew from about $500,000 in 2004 to nearly $50 million in the year before the article was published; the capture quotes "last year" without naming that calendar year.
