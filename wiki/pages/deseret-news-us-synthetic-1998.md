## Summary

A June 1998 business profile of Louis M. Pope, written while US Synthetic was in the middle of its
growth run. It is by some distance the best account the corpus holds of how the company started,
and it is the source that connects US Synthetic to MegaDiamond by a specific mechanism — Louis Pope
ran MegaDiamond's manufacturing plant for five years before founding his own company — rather than
by the vague family resemblance the other sources offer.

It also records a near-death experience the company's own timeline elides entirely.

## Useful Claims

- **Louis M. Pope founded US Synthetic in Orem in April 1978**, after five years managing the
  manufacturing plant at **Megadiamond Corp**, where he developed products and processes and helped
  design hydraulic presses. He is a Provo native with mechanical engineering and MBA degrees from BYU.
- **Bill Pope, his father, was the first president of US Synthetic**; Louis Pope was executive vice
  president and general manager. This is the detail the company's own history page omits.
- The company's **original product was synthetic diamond grit for the grinding-wheel industry**, not
  drill-bit cutters. The pivot to oil and gas came after the crisis.
- **1985 was the near-death year.** Executives went unpaid, the workforce fell from 13 to six, Pope's
  credit cards were maxed, his house was in foreclosure, and an investor's accountant recommended
  bankruptcy. Pope also set up synthetic diamond plants in China and India that year.
- **Pope became president and CEO in 1992.** The article reports the company ranked **30th in 1995,
  16th in 1996, and 25th last year** among the fastest-growing companies in the United States, and calls
  it the top manufacturer of diamond cutters for the drilling industry.
- Pope's stated goal at the time was to be a **$100 million company by 2000**. Whether that happened
  is not established anywhere in the corpus.

## Reliability Notes

Secondary tier, and a local-business profile — a genre that takes the subject's account of his own
company largely at face value. The growth rankings are almost certainly the *Inc.* 500 and the
article does not say so; treat them as reported, not verified.

**Two internal contradictions worth carrying, because they bound how precisely this source can be
used.**

First, the article is dated June 1998 and says Pope founded the company "10 years ago," then states
in the next paragraph that he launched it in April 1978 — twenty years earlier. The 1978 date agrees
with [the company's own timeline](us-synthetic-company-history.md) and with every other source, so
the "10 years" phrase is an error in the article. The wiki uses 1978.

Second, this article says the company achieved **ISO 9002**; the company's history page says it was
the first PDC manufacturer to reach **ISO 9000**, in 1993. These are different standards in the same
family and the wiki does not adjudicate between them; the fact page states neither as fact.

The transcription typo "bankrukptcy" is in the source and is reproduced above unaltered, as the
verbatim rule requires.
