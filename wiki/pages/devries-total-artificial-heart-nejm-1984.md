## Summary

The University of Utah surgical team's own peer-reviewed report of the Barney Clark implant, published in the *New England Journal of Medicine* fourteen months after the operation. It is the clinical record of the first permanent total artificial heart implantation in a human, written by the people who performed it.

## Useful Claims

- The paper of record is DeVries, W.C., Anderson, J.L., Joyce, L.D., Anderson, F.L., Hammond, E.H., Jarvik, R.K., and Kolff, W.J., "Clinical Use of the Total Artificial Heart", *New England Journal of Medicine*, vol. 310, no. 5, pp. 273–278, February 2, 1984. DOI 10.1056/NEJM198402023100501.
- The authorship is itself the evidence for the fact page's account of who did what: the implanting surgeon (William C. DeVries), the device's designer (Robert K. Jarvik), and the director of the artificial-organs program (Willem J. Kolff) are co-authors on one paper, alongside the Utah cardiology and pathology staff who managed the case.
- The clinical result was reported in a general medical journal under peer review, roughly fourteen months after the December 2, 1982 operation — the Jarvik-7 implant entered the medical literature as a case report, not only as a news event.
