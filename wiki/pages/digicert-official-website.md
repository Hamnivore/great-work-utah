## Summary

DigiCert's own "About" page: how the company describes its business, its scale, its office footprint,
its standards-body participation, and its executive team. This is the source behind the customer-count
and Fortune 500 figures on the venture page, and the only place DigiCert itself names Lehi as one of
its offices.

## Useful Claims

- DigiCert describes itself as "a global leader in Intelligent Trust" and says its DigiCert ONE
  platform "unifies PKI, DNS, and certificate lifecycle management."
- The company claims more than 125,000 customers, including more than 90% of the Fortune 500, and
  says it is chosen by security leaders in over 180 countries.
- DigiCert names four office hubs in one phrase — Lehi, London, Sunnyvale, Singapore — and this is
  the company's own confirmation of the Utah site. It does not publish a street address here.
- DigiCert states that its experts shape industry standards through the CA/Browser Forum, IETF, and
  ETSI, covering identity, encryption, certificate governance, and post-quantum security.
- The CEO is Dr. Amit Sinha, previously President of Zscaler; the page says DigiCert "delivered its
  strongest fiscal year to date in 2025" under his leadership, without publishing a revenue figure.
- The page describes protecting "hospitals, power grids, financial systems" — the company's own
  framing of what depends on its certificates.

## Reliability Notes

Self-reported tier. This is DigiCert marketing copy: good evidence of what the company claims about
its footprint and positioning, weak evidence of any of it being independently true. Nothing here is
audited — the customer count, the Fortune 500 share, and "strongest fiscal year to date" are all
unverifiable from this page, and DigiCert is privately held so no filed financials contradict or
confirm them.

**Fetch note.** `www.digicert.com` sits behind an Imperva/Incapsula WAF that returns a 923-byte
challenge page to an ordinary browser user-agent while serving the real document to the capture
script's own user-agent. That is the reverse of the usual failure and worth recording: a future
re-check that gets a short body has been blocked, not served a shortened page.

For anything about DigiCert's legal identity, domicile, or ownership, prefer the registry records
cited on the venture page (GLEIF LEI, SEC EDGAR, Utah Division of Corporations) over this page.
