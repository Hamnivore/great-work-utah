## Summary

The 2024-08-14 announcement that DigiCert would acquire Vercara, published by TA Associates — one of
DigiCert's three private-equity backers. It is the cleanest public statement of who owns DigiCert and
what the Vercara deal added to the business.

## Useful Claims

- DigiCert is "backed by Clearlake Capital Group, L.P.", "Crosspoint Capital Partners L.P." and "TA
  Associates Management L.P." — three sponsors, named together, which is why the venture page carries
  `Ownership: PE-owned` rather than naming a single owner.
- Vercara is described as a purpose-built, global, cloud-based security platform providing layers of
  protection for businesses' online presence — in practice managed authoritative DNS and DDoS
  protection, which is the capability DigiCert folded into its platform alongside certificates.
- DigiCert is described as a leading global provider of digital trust.

## Verbatim

> "DigiCert, a leading provider of digital trust, backed by Clearlake Capital Group, L.P. (together with its affiliates, "Clearlake"), Crosspoint Capital Partners L.P. ("Crosspoint"), and TA Associates Management L.P. ("TA"), today announced that it has entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Vercara from Golden Gate Capital and GIC."
> — Announcement lede

> "Vercara is a leading provider of cloud-based services that secure the online experience, including managed Authoritative Domain Name System (DNS) and Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) security offerings that protect organizations' networks and applications."
> — Announcement body

> "Vercara is a purpose-built, global, cloud-based security platform that provides layers of protection to safeguard businesses' online presence, no matter where attacks originate or where they are aimed."
> — "About Vercara"

## Reliability Notes

Self-reported tier, and specifically an *owner* announcing about its own portfolio company — which
makes it authoritative about the ownership structure (TA has every reason to state its own stake
correctly) and promotional about everything else. The "leading global provider" framing is marketing.

The deal terms were not disclosed. The completion date (2024-09-23) comes from DigiCert's own
newsroom and Clearlake's, not from this announcement, which describes the deal as agreed rather than
closed.

Ownership stakes are not apportioned anywhere public: three sponsors are named, but who holds what
is not stated here or in any filing the wiki has located.
