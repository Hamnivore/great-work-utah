## Summary

Eide Bailly's official website describes a national CPA and business-advisory firm with a Salt Lake City office at 5 Triad Center, Ste. 600, Salt Lake City, UT 84180. The local page frames the office as a Utah team "backed by national resources" offering tax, audit, advisory, and technology services.
