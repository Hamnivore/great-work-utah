## Summary

ETHW entry on the EIMCO Rocker Shovel Loader Model 12B as an ASME landmark, covering its mechanical design, Utah origins, and international manufacturing spread.

## Useful Claims

- Documents the model's origin from Utah mining practice at the North Lilly Mine in Eureka
- Credits Edwin Burt Royle and John Spence Finlay as early developers
- Notes EIMCO's Salt Lake City manufacturing and international licensing
- Confirms the ASME landmark designation and Park City location of the example unit

## Verbatim

> "The Rocker Shovel Loader 12B, built in 1938, provided a significant boost to underground mining
> productivity by emulating the movements of the human "mucker," the laborer who removed rubble, or
> "muck," from underground mines, particularly in and narrow mine tunnels."
> — ETHW landmark entry, opening

> "Both men worked for the North Lilly Mine in Ureka, Utah, in the 1920s and early 1930s."
> — ETHW landmark entry, design history

> "In 1931, Joseph Rosenblatt of EIMCO, Salt Lake City, met Royle and Findlay, and shortly
> thereafter, Royle joined EIMCO as a consultant and designer."
> — ETHW landmark entry, EIMCO manufacturing
