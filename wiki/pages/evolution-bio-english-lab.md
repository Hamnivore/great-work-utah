## Summary

The English Lab website at the University of Utah Department of Biochemistry describes a research group focused on molecular tools for cell signaling, G-protein coupled receptor (GPCR) pharmacology, and mammalian directed evolution. The lab invented the VEGAS platform and maintains a dedicated VEGAS page with workshop signup and Addgene distribution links.

## Useful Claims

- Lab mission: create and apply molecular tools to control and understand human health and disease.
- Research combines directed evolution, protein engineering, and pharmacology/biochemistry.
- This capture does not establish the VEGAS (Viral Evolution of Genetically Actuated Sequences), VEGAS Workshop, Addgene distribution, or GPCR pharmacogenomics claims reported elsewhere.
