## Summary

Fervo Energy's quarterly report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 — its first as a
public company — filed 2026-06-23. Accession number **0001628280-26-044799**; filer CIK
**0001853868**.

This is the most recent primary record of Fervo's finances and of construction progress at Cape
Station in Milford, Utah. It is a separate artifact from the IPO prospectus and is cited separately;
where the two differ, the later filing governs.

## Useful Claims

- Fervo reported revenue of **$61,000** for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, against **$0** for the
  same quarter of 2025 — the company is pre-revenue in any operational sense.
- Net loss was **$31,830,000** for Q1 2026, against **$9,146,000** for Q1 2025.
- Liquidity at March 31, 2026 was **$280.8 million** of unrestricted cash and cash equivalents
  (including **$274.5 million** in money market funds) plus **$6.0 million** of restricted cash, down
  from **$461.8 million** of cash and equivalents at December 31, 2025.
- Undrawn borrowing capacity at the same date included **$70.0 million** under the Mercuria Credit
  Facility and **$44.5 million** under the Mercuria Letter of Credit facility.
- Cape Station remains **500 megawatts** of capacity under construction in **Milford, Utah**, and
  quarterly capital expenditure was directed primarily at that construction.
- Cape Station Phase I is financed through a facility with HSBC Bank USA, National Association as
  collateral agent.

## Reliability Notes

Primary-tier. A 10-Q is filed under securities-law accuracy duties and reviewed by the company's
auditors, though its financial statements are unaudited (reviewed) rather than audited, which is the
one respect in which it is weaker than the annual report.

Cash fell roughly $181 million in the quarter, which is consistent with heavy construction spending at
Cape Station rather than with the IPO proceeds, since the offering closed in May 2026 — after the
period covered here. Do not read the March 31 cash balance as Fervo's post-IPO position; a later
filing from `https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001853868.json` is required for that.
