## Summary

FireFly Automatix, Inc. Form RW, EDGAR filing date March 16, 2026 (letter dated March 13, 2026). Accession **0001493152-26-010126**; File No. **333-290743**. The company withdrew the S-1 because it determined not to conduct the proposed offering; no securities had been issued or sold. Withdrawal is not a shutdown claim.

## Useful Claims

- FireFly Automatix, Inc. requested withdrawal of its Registration Statement on Form S-1, File No. **333-290743**.
- That registration was initially filed **October 6, 2025**.
- The stated reason: the company "has determined not to conduct the proposed offering that is described in the Registration Statement at this time."
- The Registration Statement "automatically became effective on **November 11, 2025**, but no securities have been issued or sold under the Registration Statement."
- The withdrawal letter is dated **March 13, 2026**.

## Reliability Notes

Primary-tier SEC filing, cited at EDGAR. This document establishes only that the company asked to withdraw File No. **333-290743** and said it would not conduct that offering then. It does not say the company shut down, dissolved, or stopped making machines.

The letter is dated **March 13, 2026**; the submissions JSON lists filing date **2026-03-16** (accepted 2026-03-13T21:40:38Z). Use the EDGAR filing date as **Published:**; quote the letter's own date in the body.

Letterhead spells the name "Firefly Automatix, Inc."; the Re: line spells "FireFly Automatix, Inc." Product, financial, headcount, and headquarters claims belong on [the November 2025 S-1/A](firefly-automatix-s1-2025.md), not here.
