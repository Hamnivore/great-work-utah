## Summary

Salt Lake City maker of autonomous turf harvesters and electric robotic mowers. It filed in 2025 to list on Nasdaq as FFLY, then withdrew that registration in March 2026. The withdrawal is a decision not to complete the offering, not evidence the company shut down.

## Impact

FireFly designs, manufactures, and sells physical machines for turf farms and large-area mowing: diesel-electric PATH harvesters (first ProSlab shipped 2012) and AMP autonomous electric mowers (AMP-L100 introduced late 2023), built in-house in Salt Lake City ([S-1/A](firefly-automatix-s1-2025.md)). As of September 30, 2025 it reported over 790 PATH, AMP, and earlier M220 machines in the field. That is real hardware at operating scale, not a slide-deck robot.

The bound is the category. Turf still gets harvested and golf still gets mowed if this company does not exist; other harvesters and other autonomous mowers already do the job. FireFly's claimed difference is electrification, in-house autonomy (software stack brought in-house in 2020), and a vertically integrated Salt Lake plant — a supplier substitution inside an existing equipment market, not a new kind of work in the world. C is the letter that fits: substantive and replaceable.

The March 2026 Form RW withdrew the Nasdaq:FFLY registration because the company "determined not to conduct the proposed offering … at this time," and stated that no securities had been issued or sold ([Form RW](firefly-automatix-rw-2026.md)). That is an offering decision, not a shutdown.

## Utah Context

The November 2025 prospectus puts the principal executive office at **1130 South 3800 West, Suite 100, Salt Lake City, Utah 84104**, and describes a leased **108,500** square-foot headquarters and manufacturing facility in Salt Lake City ([S-1/A](firefly-automatix-s1-2025.md)). As of October 27, 2025 the company employed 210 full-time and 7 part-time people. It is a Delaware corporation (incorporated November 13, 2015) that began operations in 2010; Utah is the plant and the identity anchor, not a branch office.

Earlier Form Ds used a North Salt Lake street address. That conflict is named under Open Questions; this page follows the prospectus principal-executive-office sentence.

## What They Need Now
