## Summary

The paper itself — the one the March 23, 1989 press conference preceded. Martin Fleischmann and Stanley Pons's report of claimed excess enthalpy and nuclear products from the electrolysis of heavy water at a palladium cathode, published in the April 1989 issue of the *Journal of Electroanalytical Chemistry and Interfacial Electrochemistry*.

## Reliability Notes

Primary tier by type, and this is the case where the tier needs the most care. `peer-reviewed` means the venue, not the verdict. The claims in this paper did not survive replication, and citing it as primary evidence establishes **that the claim was made, by whom, where, and when** — never that the claim is true. Any page citing it must say which it means.

The paper is also the textbook instance of why review is not proof: it was published, and it was wrong. What followed it was an extensive replication literature that did not confirm the excess heat under controlled conditions; the fact page carries that consensus and cites it separately.
