## Summary

A University of Utah College of Science explainer describing the 1991 Oh-My-God particle event at the Fly's Eye observatory at Dugway Proving Ground, written for a general audience. It is the institution writing about its own research programme, so it is good evidence of what Utah says about the detection and weak evidence for the physics.

## Useful Claims

- The account places the Fly's Eye experimental observatory at Dugway Proving Ground and dates the Oh-My-God particle detection to October 15, 1991.
- It describes Fly's Eye as an atmospheric detector watching for air showers caused by high-energy interactions in the upper atmosphere.
- It says University of Utah researchers worked backward from the observed air shower to infer the properties of the cosmic ray.

## Verbatim

> "It was the night of October 15, 1991."

> "The University of Utah had set up an experimental observatory called the Fly's Eye in the isolation of Dugway Proving Ground, a sprawling 800,000-acre tract of land used by the U.S. Army to test biological and chemical weapons since the 1940s."
> — Article, Fly's Eye account

> "Scientists looked at the data they'd collected and worked backward to deduce the properties of the space-borne particle that led to the air shower."
> — Article, analysis account
