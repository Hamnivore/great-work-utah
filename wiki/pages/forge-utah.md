## Summary

Forge Utah Foundation is a volunteer-run not-for-profit connecting Utah's developers, data scientists, SREs, and tinkerers. Forge Utah provides a bounded benefit: Working technologists in Utah, at any level, and people trying to become them.

## Who It's For

Working technologists in Utah, at any level, and people trying to become them. The foundation's own
framing is deliberately wide: "If you write code, wrangle data, keep servers alive, or solder things
on weekends, you belong here." Three distinct audiences are visible in what it runs:

- **Practitioners** looking for a peer group in a specific technology — the meetups are organized by
  stack (Go, Kubernetes, data engineering, AI) rather than by employer or seniority.
- **Engineering leaders** — the roundtables are explicitly small-group, no-slides, and pitched at
  people who have a real problem and want to hear how others solved it.
- **People looking for work.** The `#jobs` channel carries structured postings from Utah employers,
  and `#jobs-wanted` carries the other side. Over the six months to August 2026 those postings named
  51 distinct employers, which is a fair proxy for who is actually hiring in Utah software.

The foundation states it takes no sponsor money in exchange for access and hosts no recruiter pitches.

## How To Use It

1. **Join the Slack.** It is the live layer; the website is a description of the foundation, not a
   schedule. The join link is on the homepage.
2. **Find an event** on [utahtechcalendar.com](utah-tech-calendar.md), which covers the wider Utah
   ecosystem rather than only Forge's own nights.
3. **Go West Conference** is the annual gathering — single-track, priced low deliberately, no expo
   hall. It is the one thing on the list with a ticket.
4. Meetups and hack nights are free to attend and volunteer-run, so the practical commitment is
   showing up.

**Finding the site at all is the first obstacle**, and it is worth stating plainly for anyone
following this page: `forgeut.dev`, `forgeutah.org`, and `forgeutah.com` do not serve a homepage as of
2026-08-11. The live site is **forgeutah.tech**, which is reachable from the foundation's GitHub
organization profile and not much else.

## Open Questions

- **How large is it?** No membership, attendance, or Slack headcount figure is published anywhere
  located for this page. "Utah's community for engineers" is a self-description with no denominator.
- **Why does the IRS ruling date say 2025-07 when the site says "est. 2015"?** Both may be true — a
  long-running volunteer community that only recently sought federal exemption — but no located source
  explains the ten-year gap, so the reason remains unknown.
- **What are its finances?** The BMF row shows zero assets, zero income, and a filing requirement
  consistent with a small organization, which suggests it operates well under the 990-filing
  threshold. That is an inference from a filing code, not a statement of budget.
- **Who runs it?** Clint Berry is identified in the Slack workspace as president of the foundation and
  Derrick Laird as a Go West organizer, but no located public page on the foundation's own site names
  its board or officers.
- Are the named meetups (Utah Go, Utah Kubernetes, UDEM, AI Utah, The Foundry) Forge programs or
  independent groups it lists? The site presents them together; the distinction matters for whether
  each is its own atom here.
