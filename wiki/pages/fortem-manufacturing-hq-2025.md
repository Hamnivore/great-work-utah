## Summary

Press release announcing Fortem's opening of a consolidated advanced manufacturing headquarters in Lindon, Utah in June 2025. States the facility brings radar and interceptor production under one roof with capacity for hundreds of units per month. Reports more than 70,000 test flights and 5,500 successful drone captures across Fortem's test program.

## Useful Claims

- Fortem opened advanced manufacturing HQ in Lindon, UT in 2025
- Facility consolidates radar and interceptor production
- Stated capacity: hundreds of units per month
- 70,000+ test flights and 5,500+ successful drone captures reported
- Deployments in Ukraine, Middle East, East Asia, U.S. border and infrastructure contexts

## Verbatim

> "Fortem Technologies today officially opened a new state-of-the-art headquarters and manufacturing facility designed to meet surging demand for its counter-drone systems. The new 51,000-square-foot building in Lindon more than doubles Fortem's previous capacity and marks a major milestone in the company's mission to secure airspace around the world."
> — Press release, lead paragraph

> "From its expanded base in Utah, Fortem will produce its flagship DroneHunter® and DroneKiller ™ interceptors and TrueView™ radar systems—battle-tested technologies deployed today by military and homeland security customers in Ukraine, the Middle East, and at critical infrastructure sites globally."
> — Press release, second paragraph

> "At full capacity, the Lindon facility can produce: 600 radar units per month 40 DroneHunter® interceptors per month 300+ ground-launched air-to-air munitions (GLAAMs) per month 20 drone hangars per month —a manufacturing capability Fortem previously outsourced"
> — Press release, capacity list

> "To date, Fortem has conducted over 70,000 test flights and 5,500 successful drone captures in Utah alone."
> — Press release, body
