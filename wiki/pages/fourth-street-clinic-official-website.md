## Summary

Fourth Street Clinic's official homepage states its mission, service population, Salt Lake City location, and federal Health Center Program status.

## Useful Claims

- The clinic provides integrated health care for people experiencing homelessness.
- Its main clinic is at 409 West 400 South in Salt Lake City.
- It identifies itself as a federal Health Center Program grantee and FTCA-deemed facility.
