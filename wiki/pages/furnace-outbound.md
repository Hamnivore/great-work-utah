## Summary

Furnace builds and operates outbound-sales systems for companies. Furnace provides a bounded benefit: Early companies often need customer discovery and repeatable sales before they can support a specialized growth team.

## Impact

Early companies often need customer discovery and repeatable sales before they can support a specialized growth team. Furnace's bet is that a managed, instrumented outbound system can supply that capability earlier. The public evidence supports an active service with published plans and named client examples, while its reported campaign performance remains self-reported.

## Utah Context

Furnace emerged from Utah Tech University's Sandbox program. A November 2025 St. George startup event identified co-founders Porter Gardiner and Kyle Nelson and reported that the company had performed outreach to more than 100,000 prospects for clients; that figure is an event-organizer statement, not an audited company record.

## What They Need Now
