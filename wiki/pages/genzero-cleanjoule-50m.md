## Summary

A May 31, 2023 joint press release, published on investor GenZero's site, announcing a $50 million investment round into Salt Lake City-based CleanJoule to accelerate sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) production. The release names every investor in the round and details binding airline purchase commitments — the fullest public account of CleanJoule's 2023 raise.

## Useful Claims

- The $50 million round was led by the principals of Indigo Partners (U.S. private equity, air-transportation focused) and also included GenZero (Temasek's decarbonization investment platform, Singapore), Cleanhill Partners (U.S. private equity, decarbonization focused), and three airlines: Frontier Airlines (U.S.), Wizz Air (Europe), and Volaris (Mexico).
- As part of the round, Frontier Airlines, Wizz Air, and Volaris signed binding agreements to purchase up to 90 million gallons of SAF from CleanJoule.
- The principals of Indigo Partners received a board seat on CleanJoule's board of directors as part of the investment.
- Funds were earmarked to further develop CleanJoule's technology "to produce high-performance SAF from agricultural residues and other waste biomass more cost-effectively."
- CleanJoule co-founder and CEO Mukund Karanjikar is quoted on the raise; the release describes CleanJoule as headquartered in Salt Lake City, Utah, "focused on developing a breakthrough technology to produce superior and cost-effective SAF" with "high efficiency, feedstock flexibility, and a high degree of decarbonisation."
