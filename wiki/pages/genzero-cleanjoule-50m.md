## Summary

GenZero's announcement describes a $50 million CleanJoule investment round and associated sustainable-aviation-fuel purchase commitments.

## Useful Claims

- CleanJoule announced $50 million from a consortium led by Indigo Partners, with GenZero and airline participation.
- Frontier Airlines, Wizz Air, and Volaris entered purchase agreements described as covering up to 90 million gallons.
- The financing was presented as support for scaling CleanJoule's biomass-to-fuel technology.

## Verbatim

> "CleanJoule , a U.S.-based startup focused on the production of Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF), today announced a US$50 million investment round led by the principals of Indigo Partners, a U.S.-based private equity firm."
> — Announcement opening

> "Funds raised from the round will support the further development of CleanJoule’s technology to produce high-performance SAF from agricultural residues and other waste biomass more cost-effectively."
> — Announcement opening

> "As part of the consortium’s investment, Frontier Airlines, Wizz Air and Volaris have signed binding agreements to purchase up to 90 million gallons of SAF."
> — Consortium terms

## Reliability Notes

GenZero was an investor, so this is primary evidence that participants announced the transaction. It is not independent evidence of performance, production volume, or eventual fulfillment.
