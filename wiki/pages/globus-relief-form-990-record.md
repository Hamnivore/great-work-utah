## Summary

Form 990 filing history of **Globous Relief Fund** (EIN **84-1369453**), Salt Lake City — the IRS
legal name for the organization that operates as **Globus Relief**.

## Useful Claims

- Legal name **Globous Relief Fund**; EIN **84-1369453**; location **Salt Lake City, UT**;
  **501(c)(3)** with IRS exemption ruling **May 1998**; NTEE **Nonmonetary Support N.E.C. (Q193)**.
- Dec. 2024 filing flagged **reported conflict of interest transactions** (Schedule L).
- Most recent extracted fiscal year **December 2024**: revenue **$5,089,834**, expenses
  **$9,846,718**, net assets **$2,580,722**.
- Revenue mix (FY ending Dec. 2024): **contributions $3,343,052 (65.7%)**, **program services

- **Daniel Henrie** listed as **CEO** with **$132,000** compensation; **Chase Henrie** as **Srn
  Director** with **$115,600** (Dec. 2024 extract).

## Reliability Notes

Primary-tier IRS data via ProPublica. The IRS spelling **Globous** differs from the public brand
**Globus Relief**; NTEE "Nonmonetary Support" aligns with donated medical supply logistics. FY 2024
shows expenses exceeding revenue by ~$4.8M — the HTML summary alone does not explain whether that
reflects in-kind distribution timing, inventory write-downs, or other non-cash accounting; Schedule M
and the PDF return would be needed for a full reconciliation. The organization's website claims
administrative expenses below 1%; the 990 extract shows **$132,000** CEO compensation against
**$9.8M** total expenses (~1.3% for one officer line alone).
