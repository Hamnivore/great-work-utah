## Summary

Globus Relief about page — medical supply recovery and international distribution from Salt Lake City.

## Useful Claims

- Organization states it has provided **lifesaving medical resources since 1998**.
- Self-reported impact counters: **160+ countries served**, **$1 Billion medical supplies given**,
  **26+ years experience**.
- Claims a **self-funding operating model** where overhead is already paid, so **100% of donations
  go directly to humanitarian programs**; **administrative and fundraising expenses below 1%**.
- Claims **Charity Navigator's highest, 4-star rating year after year** (self-reported; not verified
  on this capture).
- Lists **850+ partner charities** and address **1032 West 2610 South, Salt Lake City, UT 84119**.
