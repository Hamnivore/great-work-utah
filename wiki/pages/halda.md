## Summary

Halda is a Provo, Utah education-technology company building an AI enrollment platform for colleges, universities, and K-12 schools. Its agents coordinate prospective-student interactions across institutional websites, email, SMS, voice, and WhatsApp, retaining context between contacts and writing activity back to institutional CRM systems. The product covers inquiry capture, application generation, and yield rather than operating as a classroom-learning tool.

Founded in 2021, Halda says more than 200 institutions use the platform. That reach figure and the conversion improvements on its website are company-reported rather than independently audited; they establish the company's claimed operating scale, not independently verified outcomes.

## Impact

Enrollment offices face a structural mismatch: prospective students research schools and ask questions at all hours, while counselors have limited capacity and fragmented systems. Halda's intended effect is to make that interaction continuous and individualized without requiring institutions to expand staff in proportion to inquiry volume. If it works as described, students receive faster answers and more relevant guidance while enrollment teams spend more time on conversations that require human judgment.

The counterfactual is not no software; institutions already use CRMs, forms, campaign tools, call centers, and enrollment consultants. Halda matters to the extent that one contextual system can replace or coordinate those disconnected tools while preserving student trust. That creates real bounds: enrollment optimization can help a student navigate a suitable institution, but it can also make recruitment more persuasive without improving affordability, academic quality, or fit. FERPA, consent, data minimization, and the accuracy of AI-generated answers are therefore part of the impact case, not peripheral compliance details.

**Bet:** Halda is betting that enrollment teams will trust autonomous, cross-channel agents with institution-specific student communication—and that a student-centered interaction can remain genuinely individualized when produced at marketing scale.

## Utah Context

Halda is headquartered in Provo and sits in Utah's unusually dense education-software corridor alongside [SchoolAI](schoolai.md), [Instructure](instructure.md), and [Pluralsight](pluralsight.md). The company is narrower than those platforms: it works before and during enrollment rather than in classroom instruction, learning management, or professional skills. That specialization makes it a useful Utah landing point for people who combine B2B SaaS operations with higher-education enrollment knowledge.

Its careers pages identify Provo as the hybrid-office location while allowing some roles to work remotely in the United States. The Utah footprint is therefore an operating headquarters and hiring site, not merely a founding connection.

## What They Need Now

Halda's official careers board currently shows three full-time openings: implementation strategist, vice president of revenue, and revenue operations manager. The durable need is consequently concentrated in two role families:

- **Sales and business development:** senior revenue leadership that can unite new-school sales with renewals and expansion, build credible forecasts, shape pricing and packaging, and lead B2B SaaS teams through a growth stage. Higher-education or K-12 buying-cycle experience is directly relevant.
- **Program and project management:** implementation strategists and revenue-operations builders who can translate institutional enrollment workflows into a working deployment, coordinate data and CRM handoffs, and impose operational discipline across sales and renewals.

The board describes roles as remote or hybrid, while the revenue-leadership posting specifically names Provo, Utah, as the hybrid location. Applicants should use the careers landing page because individual openings will change.

## Open Questions

- How many of the 200-plus institutions use the full cross-channel platform rather than one product or a limited deployment?
- Are reported conversion and yield gains independently evaluated, and how do results vary by institution type and student population?
- What review, escalation, and audit controls prevent an AI agent from giving materially wrong admissions, financial-aid, or transfer-credit guidance?
- How does Halda distinguish individualized student support from increasingly automated enrollment marketing, particularly for vulnerable or first-generation applicants?
- What is the company's current financing and ownership structure? Public sources reviewed here do not establish it clearly enough to record.
