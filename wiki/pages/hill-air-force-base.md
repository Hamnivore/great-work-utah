## Summary

Hill Air Force Base is a US Air Force installation in northern Utah (spanning parts of Layton, Clearfield, Sunset, and Roy along the Wasatch Front), hosted by the 75th Air Base Wing. Direct impact is straightforward to state and hard to overstate: Hill sustains a meaningful share of the US Air Force's fighter and attack fleet and the land leg of the nuclear triad.

## Impact

Direct impact is straightforward to state and hard to overstate: Hill sustains a meaningful share of the U.S. Air Force's fighter and attack fleet and the land leg of the nuclear triad. The base's own 2024 Economic Impact Statement reports a total workforce of 26,893 (active duty, Air Force Reserve/Guard, government civilians, and contract civilians combined) and a total annual economic impact of $12.76 billion, supporting an estimated 67,046 direct and indirect Utah jobs ([official source](hill-air-force-base-official-website.md)) — a scale that makes Hill comparable to the state's largest private employers, funded instead by the federal government.

Permanence and bounds cut in different directions. Hill has operated continuously for over 85 years and its sustainment mission (depot maintenance for fielded weapon systems) is inherently durable — aircraft need maintenance for as long as they fly, regardless of which contractor built them. But most of that work is sustainment and repair of existing, mature systems rather than new-system development; the frontier engineering in Utah's defense-aerospace cluster (solid rocket motor redesign, for instance) sits at [Northrop Grumman's Promontory facility](northrop-grumman-promontory.md), not here, with the notable exception of the 309th's software modernization work. Counterfactually, Hill is one of only three Air Force Sustainment Center air logistics complexes in the country and the only one that services the F-35, F-22, F-16, and A-10 together with the ICBM force — there is no ready alternative depot for most of this work in the near term.

The ethical dimension deserves the same treatment given to Utah's other defense-prime work on this wiki: Hill exists to keep combat aircraft and a nuclear deterrent force operational. Whether one reads that as stabilizing deterrence or as a moral weight to carry, it is not neutral, and prospective employees should think about it directly rather than treat depot work as ethically inert simply because it is "maintenance" rather than "weapons development."

## Utah Context

Hill anchors the Wasatch Front's defense-and-aerospace cluster from the north, distinct from but closely linked to the [309th Software Engineering Group](309th-software-engineering-group.md) it hosts, [L3Harris Salt Lake City](l3harris-salt-lake.md)'s communications and EW work, and [Northrop Grumman's Promontory facility](northrop-grumman-promontory.md), which builds the solid rocket motors for the Sentinel ICBM that Hill will eventually sustain. The [Utah Test and Training Range](utah-test-and-training-range.md) — 1.1 million acres of ground range plus a much larger restricted-airspace block in the West Desert — is operated as a Hill tenant activity and used by the 388th/419th Fighter Wings; [Dugway Proving Ground](dugway-proving-ground.md) is a separate Army facility in the same West Desert defense-test geography, not a Hill tenant. [47G](47g-utah-aerospace-and-defense-association.md), the statewide aerospace/defense association, treats Hill as one of the anchors of the ecosystem it organizes around.

Hill's civilian and contractor workforce also feeds a well-documented talent pipeline into Utah defense-tech startups and primes, a dynamic the 309th's own page discusses in more detail.

## What They Need Now

Hill hires along two entirely separate tracks. Uniformed military careers (officer, enlisted, healthcare) route through airforce.com. Civilian federal employment — the larger and more relevant track for most wiki readers — routes primarily through USAJOBS.gov, with two notable shortcuts the base itself advertises: a direct-to-recruiter path for engineering and scientist candidates with a BS or higher (bypassing the standard USAJOBS queue by emailing a resume and transcripts directly to the Science and Engineering Recruitment Team), and periodic Direct Hire Authority windows for a defined list of skilled-trade occupations at OO-ALC that let experienced tradespeople submit resumes directly rather than through USAJOBS.

The base's own employment materials name engineers (mechanical, electrical, software, avionics), scientists, contracting specialists, financial-management specialists, budget analysts, cost estimators, logistics and supply-chain management specialists, security/police officers, and skilled-trade production and maintenance occupations as consistently high-demand civilian career areas. Software specifically is concentrated in the [309th Software Engineering Group](309th-software-engineering-group.md), which maintains its own careers page. Most substantive roles require a Secret or Top Secret clearance; government pay scales (GS/NH/NT bands) apply rather than private-sector compensation, and clearance timelines are an absolute gate for candidates without prior clearance.

## Who Could Help

Useful helpers include clearance-process advisors and veterans'-transition counselors (Hill is a recognized veteran-friendly hiring environment), the Utah Department of Workforce Services (which partners with Hill on Direct Hire Authority trade-occupation recruiting), defense-contractor partners who supplement the civilian workforce on-base, and university engineering/computer-science pipelines that feed the OO-ALC and 309th recruiting teams directly. For candidates weighing the ethics of the work, current or former engineers who have actually worked on weapons-sustainment programs are more useful than generic career coaches.

## Open Questions

- Hill's own hill.af.mil fact-sheet pages give inconsistent OO-ALC personnel counts (roughly 7,000, 8,100+, and 9,000+ on three different subpages) ([official source](hill-air-force-base-official-website.md)) — which is current, and how does it relate to the base-wide total of 26,893?
- What share of the base-wide workforce and economic-impact figures is attributable to OO-ALC specifically versus the fighter wings, the 309th, and other tenant units? The base's public reporting is base-wide, not broken out by unit.
- How is the Sentinel ICBM ramp expected to change Hill's civilian hiring over the next decade, and how does that connect to Sentinel motor production at [Northrop Grumman Promontory](northrop-grumman-promontory.md)?
- No hill.af.mil page publishes a single official street address or coordinate for the installation as a whole (it has three public gates, in Layton, Clearfield/Sunset, and Roy); this page therefore omits a map point rather than publish an approximate one that could misrepresent precision.
