## Summary

The High Resolution Fly's Eye (HiRes) collaboration's *Physical Review Letters* paper reporting the first observation of the Greisen–Zatsepin–Kuzmin (GZK) suppression in the ultra-high-energy cosmic-ray spectrum. HiRes operated in Utah's West Desert, which is why this measurement belongs to the state's scientific record.

## Useful Claims

- The paper is the peer-reviewed record for the HiRes collaboration's claimed first observation of the GZK suppression in the cosmic-ray spectrum.
- It is a collaboration paper, not a single-investigator result; authorship establishes the institutions involved.
