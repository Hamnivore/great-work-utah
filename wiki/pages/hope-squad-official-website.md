## Summary

Hope Squad homepage — peer suicide-prevention programming for schools, Provo headquarters.

## Useful Claims

- Hope Squad describes itself as trusted by **"Thousands of schools"**; **98% of school
  administrators** whose schools implemented Hope Squad **agree** the programming **promotes a
  positive school climate** (self-reported survey claim on homepage).
- Homepage states **95% of students report knowing how and where to get help for their peers**
  with Hope Squad (self-reported).
- A Hope Squad is **"a group of students nominated by their peers"** trained to note distress and
  connect peers to adults; members are **not counselors**.
- Footer lists **5455 N. River Run Dr., Provo UT 84604** and **(801) 342-3444**.
