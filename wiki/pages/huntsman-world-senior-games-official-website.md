## Useful Claims

- The organization's own About copy calls the Games **"the largest annual multi-sport event in the
  world for athletes aged 50 and over"**, with **over 40 sports** over **two weeks each October**
  in **St. George, Utah**, for **over 35 years**.
- The Games began in **1987** as the **World Senior Games**, founded by **Daisy and John H. Morgan,
  Jr.**; **Jon M. Huntsman** became principal sponsor in **1989**.
- The age requirement was reduced from **55** to **50** in the Games' second year.
- The site states a year-round staff plus **more than 2,500** seasonal volunteers hosts **40**
  athletic events each October; athletes from **87 nations** have participated since **1987** (per
  a "Worldwide Reach" section).
- The Games describes itself as a **501(c)(3)** charitable organization with a mission to foster
  **worldwide peace, health and friendship** and **lead the world in active aging**.
- In **2024** the Games took the designation **The Senior World Championships** while remaining
  open to athletes of all skill levels.
