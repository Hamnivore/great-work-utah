## Summary

The **Huntsman World Senior Games** is a two-week October multi-sport competition in **St. George,
Washington County**, Utah, for athletes **50 and older**. The organization's own site calls it
**"the largest annual multi-sport event in the world"** for that age group, with **40+ sports** and
participants from **87 nations since 1987**
([official site](huntsman-world-senior-games-official-website.md)).

The legal entity is **World Senior Games Inc** (EIN **87-0447330**), a St. George **501(c)(3)** tax
exempt since **January 1987**
([Form 990 record](world-senior-games-form-990-record.md)). The event began as the **World Senior
Games**, founded by **Daisy and John H. Morgan, Jr.**; **Jon M. Huntsman** became principal sponsor
in **1989** ([official site](huntsman-world-senior-games-official-website.md)).

## Impact

Washington County is far from Utah's Wasatch Front tech and biotech clusters; this is one of the
state's largest recurring international gatherings in the rural southwest. The depth argument is
physical: **40 athletic events** over **two weeks each October**, a year-round staff, and **2,500+**
seasonal volunteers per the organization's account
([official site](huntsman-world-senior-games-official-website.md)). Health screenings (cancer,
glaucoma, diabetes, blood pressure, cholesterol, bone density) run alongside competition — the Games
frame themselves as active-aging infrastructure, not only a medal chase.

The breadth argument rests on international draw. The site claims athletes from **87 nations** since
**1987** and describes serious competitors from **Japan to Russia** and **Alaska to Australia**
([official site](huntsman-world-senior-games-official-website.md)). That is self-reported reach; no
independent census of participants is in this shard.

The **"largest in the world"** superlative appears twice on the captured About page ("largest annual
multi-sport event" and "globe's largest multi-sport event") and is **the organization's own claim,
not third-party verified**. The lead's hypothesis is therefore **supported only as attributed
marketing**, not established fact.

Primary-tier finances show real operating scale: FY ending **Dec. 2025** revenue **$3.98M** and
expenses **$3.83M**, roughly half from **program services** and half from **contributions**
([Form 990 record](world-senior-games-form-990-record.md)) — a midsize nonprofit event engine, not a
volunteer potluck.

**Bet:** that hosting a branded international senior championship in a desert resort town is a
durable economic and public-health intervention — tourism, volunteer culture, and screenings bundled
with sport.

The honest counterweight: superlatives, nation counts, and volunteer numbers are self-reported; the
2024 rebrand to **The Senior World Championships** is recent and its competitive significance is not
independently documented here.
