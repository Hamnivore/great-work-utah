## Summary

October 2021 Husqvarna Group announcement of its agreement to acquire Orbit Irrigation for integration into the Gardena Division.

## Useful Claims

- Husqvarna Group signed an agreement to acquire Orbit Irrigation on 2021-10-26; Orbit was to become part of the Gardena Division.
- Orbit's net sales during the last 12 months were approximately USD 320m; enterprise value USD 480m on a cash-and-debt-free basis.
- Orbit is based in Salt Lake City, Utah, with approximately 300 employees and over 95% of revenues in North America.
- Orbit's B-hyve smart products had 1.4 million connected devices per this release.
