## Summary

Husqvarna Group's published 2021 year-end report PDF documenting completion of the Orbit Irrigation acquisition in November 2021 and consolidated financial impacts.

## Useful Claims

- In November 2021, Husqvarna Group acquired 100% of U.S.-based Orbit Irrigation; Orbit became part of the Gardena Division.
- Purchase price corresponded to an enterprise value of USD 480m; Orbit's 2021 net sales were USD 324m.
- Orbit is based in Salt Lake City, Utah, with approximately 300 employees; over 95% of revenues are in North America per the phrase "generates over 95% of its revenues in North America" in the filing capture.
- Orbit's B-hyve suite had 1.5 million connected devices per this filing.
