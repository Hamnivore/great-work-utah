## Summary

The federal SBIR award record documents InnoSys's DOE-funded work on fiber-optic glass-to-metal assemblies for neutron-generator alpha-particle detection.

## Useful Claims

- The award describes InnoSys's existing glass-to-metal seal technology and a project to develop leak-free vacuum-barrier fiber-optic couplings for neutron-generator applications.
- The proposed application is alpha-particle detection in neutron generators.
