## Summary

Company-issued press release announcing that Intactis Bio Corp raised $250K from Nucleus Fund and demonstrated iPSC-derived neurons performing task-distinct activity patterns for arithmetic and language inputs on their automated biocomputation platform.

## Useful Claims

- Intactis Bio Corp raised $250,000 from Nucleus Fund (pre-seed).
- The company demonstrated lab-grown neurons producing task-distinct activity patterns for arithmetic and language inputs — the "Hello, World" milestone.
- The platform automates biochip workflows allowing customers to run AI/ML workloads against living-neuron biochips with silicon baselines.
- Intactis is a resident at Altitude Lab in Salt Lake City and spun out of University of Utah research.

## Verbatim

> "Intactis Bio Corp, a leader in biohybrid computing, announced a major milestone: in a controlled laboratory setting, living brain cells (neurons) successfully performed math operations and transmitted the phrase "Hello, World!""
> — Press release, lead paragraph

> "In this recent demonstration, the team grew neurons from induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs), which are commercially available cells that can be turned into many different cell types."
> — Press release, body

> "At the same time, Nucleus Fund invested $250,000 in Intactis Bio Corp to help the company grow its platform, increase the difficulty of the tasks it can handle, and move toward commercialization."
> — Press release, body
