## Summary

Intergalactic's official website describes its aerospace thermal-management systems, component catalog, St. George facilities, team, quality certifications, and recruiting path.

## Useful Claims

- Intergalactic identifies St. George, Utah, as its headquarters and publishes a contact address at 3172 E. Deseret Drive South.
- The solutions page says the current facility includes 10,000 square feet of cleanroom space and describes a planned 70,000-square-foot facility on St. George's Tech Ridge.
- The company markets thermal-management systems for civil aviation, defense and security, space, and land and sea applications.
- Its catalog includes active and passive systems as well as heat exchangers, controllers, pumps, compressors, power-distribution equipment, heaters, accumulators, flow-control valves, and fans.
- The company states that it holds AS9100D/ISO 9001:2015 certification and lists additional aerospace quality, counterfeit-parts, foreign-object-damage, and cybersecurity standards.
- Its company page invites engineers, designers, technicians, and business specialists to work at the southern Utah headquarters or remotely and links to an ApplicantPro board.
- Archived material on the site says St. George-based RAM Company acquired a controlling stake in Airborne Environmental Control Systems and moved it from Washington to St. George in 2019; current ownership is not clearly stated.

## Reliability Notes

This is the company's own source and is strong evidence for its current public product catalog, address, stated facilities, recruiting path, and self-description. Claims about performance, sole-source status, facility expansion, certifications, ownership, and customer programs need independent or documentary confirmation. The site's 2026 footer links careers and terms to GE Aerospace, while older pages name RAM's founders as majority owners; that unresolved conflict is why this source remains Medium confidence.
