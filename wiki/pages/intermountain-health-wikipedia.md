## Summary

The English Wikipedia article on Intermountain Health as of the captured snapshot. It is encyclopedic background on the health system's size, headquarters, and national reputation — not a source on clinical quality improvement, Brent James, or the Advanced Training Program.

## Useful Claims

- Intermountain Health is a not-for-profit healthcare system headquartered in Salt Lake City, Utah, with clinics and hospitals across the Intermountain West.
- In 2009, President Barack Obama publicly cited Intermountain Healthcare as an example of a place that "offer[s] high-quality care at a cost below average."
- The captured article does not mention HELP, LDS Hospital's clinical-informatics lineage, Brent James, the Institute for Health Care Delivery Research, or Intermountain's clinical quality-improvement methods.

## Verbatim

> Intermountain Health (formerly Intermountain Healthcare ) is a United States not-for-profit healthcare system with 385 clinics and 33 hospitals in the Intermountain West (primarily Colorado, Idaho, Montana, Nevada, and Utah). The company's headquarters are in Salt Lake City , Utah.

— Lead

> In 2009, Intermountain Health was identified as a healthcare model by President Barack Obama , "We have long known that some places, like the Intermountain Healthcare in Utah..., offer high-quality care at a cost below average."
