## Summary

A 2012 Standard-Examiner article covering Iomega's continued presence in Roy, Utah a decade after the Zip drive's peak, reporting on the company's history, peak employment, and EMC acquisition. The Standard-Examiner is the primary regional newspaper for Weber County and a reliable source for local business history.

## Useful Claims

- Iomega was founded in 1980 and headquartered in Roy, Utah (Weber County).
- The company reached approximately $1.7 billion in annual sales by 1997 during the Zip drive's peak.
- At peak, Iomega employed approximately 2,000 workers in Utah.
- EMC acquired Iomega in 2008, after which the Roy campus continued operating but at reduced scale.
