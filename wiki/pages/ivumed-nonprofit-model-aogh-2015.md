## Summary

Peer-reviewed description of IVUmed's teach-the-teacher model for urological surgical training in low- and middle-income countries, with Catherine R. deVries as a co-author affiliated with IVUmed and the University of Utah.

## Useful Claims

- The paper of record is Jalloh, M., Wood, J.P., Fredley, M., and deVries, C.R., "IVUmed: A Nonprofit Model for Surgical Training in Low-Resource Countries", *Annals of Global Health*, DOI 10.1016/j.aogh.2015.03.001, PMID 26088092.
- deVries is listed with affiliations at IVUmed, Salt Lake City, UT, and the University of Utah, Salt Lake City, Utah.
- IVUmed's motto is stated as "Teach One, Reach Many" and the paper describes a teach-the-teacher approach.
- The paper states IVUmed has for 20 years supported urological educational programs in 30 LMICs by coordinating a network of U.S. and international providers, institutions, industry partners, and professional societies.
- The paper describes training surgeons rather than performing one-off surgical missions as the durable model.
