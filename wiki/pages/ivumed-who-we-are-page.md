## Summary

IVUmed's history page naming founder Catherine deVries and the Teach One, Reach Many training model.

## Useful Claims

- **IVU was founded in 1995** to address global urological care and training shortages in low-resource areas.
- Founder **Dr. Catherine deVries** began the model after a **1992** invitation from ReSurge International (then Interplast) to address pediatric urological need in **Honduras**.
- **IVU was officially established in 1995** after pilot work in Honduras and **Vietnam (from 1994)**.
- IVU reports **25–30 surgical training workshops each year** as it approaches its **30th anniversary** (self-reported as of capture date).
- The page describes **training physicians and nurses** and building **sustainable surgical teaching programs**, not episodic mission surgery alone.
