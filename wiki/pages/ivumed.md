## Summary

IVUmed (International Volunteers in Urology) is a Sandy, Utah 501(c)(3) that trains surgeons and nurses in low-resource countries rather than running episodic mission trips alone. Founded in **1995** by pediatric urologist **Catherine R. deVries** after pilot work in Honduras (from **1992**) and Vietnam (from **1994**), it operates under the motto **"Teach One, Reach Many"** ([who we are](ivumed-who-we-are-page.md), [homepage](ivumed-official-website.md)).

This is **not** [William C. DeVries](devries-total-artificial-heart-nejm-1984.md), the University of Utah surgeon who implanted the Jarvik-7 artificial heart in 1982. Catherine deVries is a different physician; her ORCID is **0000-0002-9828-3488** with University of Utah affiliation.

## Who It's For

Urologists, pediatric surgeons, nurses, and teaching hospitals in **Africa, Asia, the Caribbean, and Latin America** seeking sustained surgical training partnerships — and U.S. volunteers donating teaching time and travel.

## What It Provides

**Surgical teaching workshops** (the organization reports **25–30 per year** approaching its 30th anniversary), traveling resident scholarships, virtual visiting-professor lectures, and on-site capacity building ([who we are](ivumed-who-we-are-page.md)). The homepage emphasizes **medical and surgical education** plus treatment for children and adults in low-resource settings ([official site](ivumed-official-website.md)).

A **peer-reviewed paper co-authored by deVries** describes IVUmed's model as coordinating urological education in **30 LMICs** over **20 years** with a teach-the-teacher emphasis rather than one-off missions ([Annals of Global Health, 2015](ivumed-nonprofit-model-aogh-2015.md)).

IRS filings show a mid-six-figure operation — **$242,867** revenue and **$745,813** assets in FY2023 ([IRS record](ivumed-form-990-record.md)).
