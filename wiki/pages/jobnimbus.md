## Summary

JobNimbus is a Lehi, Utah vertical-software company building CRM, project-management, communications, estimating, and payment tools for roofing and home-exterior contractors. Its platform follows a contracting job from lead capture through sales, production, documents and photos, material ordering, invoicing, and collection, with mobile access for office and field teams.

The company was founded in Utah in 2013. It took a $53 million investment from Mainsail Partners in 2020 and announced a $330 million Sumeru Equity Partners-led growth investment in November 2024. By 2025, CEO Ben Hodson told *Utah Business* that JobNimbus had reached about $60 million in annual revenue and 200 employees; those are executive-reported figures rather than audited public-company disclosures.

## Impact

Small and midsize contractors often operate through a fragile mix of spreadsheets, texts, paper job folders, accounting software, supplier portals, and individual salespeople's memory. JobNimbus's intended effect is to turn that fragmented operating work into a shared system: leads trigger workflows, crews and office staff see the same job state, estimates and material orders reuse captured information, and invoices and payments remain connected to the work that produced them.

The breadth is potentially large because roofing and exterior contracting are distributed industries dominated by many local operators rather than a few national firms. Better operational software can shorten response and payment cycles and let a contractor grow without adding administrative overhead at the same rate. The bounds matter too: JobNimbus does not repair a roof, train tradespeople, improve jobsite safety, or guarantee workmanship. Its impact depends on adoption by contractors and on whether automation produces better execution rather than simply faster selling.

**Bet:** JobNimbus is betting that a specialized operating system can serve trade contractors better than a generic CRM plus disconnected point tools—and that payments, AI assistance, marketing, and supplier integrations belong inside the same workflow as the job itself.

## Utah Context

JobNimbus is headquartered in Lehi, where its official contact and careers pages publish the company's Utah office. It is a substantial example of Utah vertical SaaS: software aimed not at other technology companies but at field businesses in roofing, fencing, gutters, siding, restoration, and related exterior trades.

The company's current Greenhouse board is predominantly Lehi-based, making the headquarters a live employment node rather than a nominal corporate address. JobNimbus also connects Utah's software ecosystem to the construction trades—a customer base that is economically important but less visible in technology-sector maps than venture-backed software buyers.
