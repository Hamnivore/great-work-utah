## Summary


Josh James co-founded Utah software companies Omniture and Domo, taking both public before Adobe bought Omniture for $1.8 billion. That repeat founder record helped prove Utah could produce major enterprise-software companies, though neither market depended uniquely on him.

## Impact

James's two companies form the clearest through-line of Silicon Slopes as an enterprise software destination. The Omniture acquisition trained a generation of Utah SaaS operators and proved that a Utah-based company could compete at enterprise scale globally. His angel investment in companies like [Xandem](xandem.md) signals continued ecosystem engagement. For later Utah founders, the James playbook — build in Utah Valley, serve enterprise buyers globally, exit at scale — is one of the best-documented templates available.

## What They Offer

Operational depth in building enterprise data products and analytics companies. His primary ecosystem contribution is the Omniture exit and the downstream operator community it created, alongside Domo as an ongoing existence proof of a Utah-built public cloud software company.
