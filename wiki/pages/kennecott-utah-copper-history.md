## Summary

The official history page for Kennecott Utah Copper covers the development of the Bingham Canyon Mine from early twentieth-century origins through current operations. It describes the mine's scale, production history, and the company's role as a subsidiary of Rio Tinto.

## Useful Claims

- Kennecott has been mining and processing minerals from the Bingham Canyon Mine since **1903**.
- The first phase of the South Wall Pushback extended the mine's life to **2026**; stripping for the **$1.5 billion** second phase, extending operations to **2032**, remains on track.
- Feasibility studies are progressing to extend open-pit mining beyond 2032; if approved, the planned Apex expansion could produce around 1.5 million additional tonnes of copper and extend the mine's life to approximately 2040.
- This capture does not state that Bingham Canyon is the largest open-pit copper mine by volume excavated, that it has produced more copper than any other mine in history, or that it holds National Historic Landmark designation.
