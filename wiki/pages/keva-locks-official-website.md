## Summary

KEVA's website describes smart locks and access-management software for rental-property operators, with phone-wallet credentials and no managed Wi-Fi requirement.

## Useful Claims

- KEVA markets digital access for rental properties through Apple Wallet and Google Wallet credentials.
- The company says its locks can operate without managed Wi-Fi and its software can integrate with property-management systems.
