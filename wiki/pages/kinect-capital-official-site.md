## Summary

The Kinect Capital official site describes a Salt Lake City 501(c)(3) nonprofit and mentor-driven accelerator connecting founders, investors, and opportunity.

## Useful Claims

- Kinect frames its founder path around starting, raising money, and changing the world.
- The site describes Investor Ready work, mentor coaching, pitch and deck support, valuation and term-sheet help, and investor access.
- It routes founders through an Apply to Pitch path and also has sponsor, donor, mentor, and investor engagement paths.
