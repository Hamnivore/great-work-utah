## Summary

University of Utah Lassonde Entrepreneur Institute profile (2014) of Wasatch Microfluidics, describing its formation on the U campus from mechanical-engineering research and its commercialization through Lassonde programs.

## Useful Claims

- The profile states Wasatch Microfluidics formed on the University of Utah campus in 2004 with founder Josh Eckman and mechanical-engineering professor Bruce Gale.
- Bruce Gale, a professor of mechanical engineering, is quoted as having originally developed Wasatch Microfluidics; Eckman developed the company with Gale in the Lassonde New Venture Development Center.
- The profile does not use the name Carterra; this capture does not mention any later rename.

## Verbatim

> "After forming on the University of Utah campus in 2004, Josh Eckman, business administration graduate and mechanical engineering masters graduate, made pharmaceutical research technology to a million-dollar company."
