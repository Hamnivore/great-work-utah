## Summary

Leland is a Lehi company building a marketplace and community for career and education guidance. Leland's bet is that a vetted marketplace can make practitioners' tacit knowledge—how a field hires, what good work looks like, and how to navigate a transition—available outside those networks.

## Impact

High-quality career knowledge is unevenly distributed through schools, employers, and personal networks. Leland's bet is that a vetted marketplace can make practitioners' tacit knowledge—how a field hires, what good work looks like, and how to navigate a transition—available outside those networks. Experts set their own rates and availability while Leland supplies discovery, scheduling, payments, messaging, and reputation tools.

The strongest impact case is access: a person can find an experienced practitioner in a narrow field without already knowing one. The caveat is affordability. A marketplace can widen the supply of expertise while still serving mainly customers who can pay, and the public evidence does not establish comparative outcomes or access by income.

## Utah Context

Leland is headquartered in Lehi and describes itself as an in-person company. Its careers page says employees work together at the Lehi office every day. That makes the company a current Utah County hiring node rather than merely a Utah-founded remote business.

The company also creates a second route into its network: Utah practitioners with credible domain experience can apply to become independent experts. That is marketplace participation, not employment, and should not be confused with the employee openings below.
