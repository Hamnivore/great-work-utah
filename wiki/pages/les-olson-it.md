## Summary


Les Olson IT supplies managed technology, cybersecurity, cloud, phones, printers, and document systems to Utah organizations. Outsourcing keeps smaller firms operational without full internal teams, but many providers can deliver the same infrastructure.

## Impact

Small and mid-sized organizations often need secure, reliable infrastructure without maintaining a full internal IT and office-equipment staff. Les Olson IT's potential contribution is a durable regional service layer: technicians and managed-service teams that keep customer systems, networks, backups and workplace equipment working. That effect is broad but incremental; the company becomes unusually consequential when its support prevents downtime or security failures at healthcare, government and other essential-service customers.

**Bet:** A locally staffed, long-lived technology provider can deliver more accountable support to regional organizations than a fragmented set of remote vendors.

## Utah Context

The company publishes a Salt Lake City headquarters and Utah branches in Ogden, Logan, Lindon, Price, Manti, St. George and Cedar City, plus operations outside the state. Its footprint makes it a statewide technical-employment path spanning the Wasatch Front and smaller Utah markets rather than a single-office Salt Lake employer.
