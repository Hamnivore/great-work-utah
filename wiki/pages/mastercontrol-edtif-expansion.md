## Summary

In December 2023 the Utah Governor's Office of Economic Opportunity posted an award notice approving a post-performance tax reduction under the Economic Development Tax Increment Financing (EDTIF) program for MasterControl Solutions, Inc.'s expansion in Salt Lake County. The page identifies MasterControl as headquartered in Cottonwood Heights, Utah, and projects approximately 155 new high-paying Utah jobs over six years.

## Useful Claims

- GOEO's award page states MasterControl projects adding approximately 155 new, high-paying jobs in Utah over six years, and identifies MasterControl as "headquartered in Cottonwood Heights, Utah," with global offices in the United Kingdom, Japan, Australia, and Singapore. [Source: GOEO award page](https://business.utah.gov/tax-credits/mastercontrol-solutions-inc-expands-in-salt-lake-county/)
- The same page's tax-credit projection table lists a 6-year timeline, 155 jobs, $61,099,500 in new wages, $2,268,064 in new state tax revenue, and a 20% new-state-tax-credit rate. [Source: GOEO award page](https://business.utah.gov/tax-credits/mastercontrol-solutions-inc-expands-in-salt-lake-county/)
- A separately linked GOEO board document records a later Economic Development Zone action and incentive recommendation; those details are not present in this captured award page. [Source: GOEO Board of the Governor's Office of Economic Opportunity minutes, June/July 2024](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/files/1144525.pdf)
- EDCUtah's matching release repeats the 155-job, six-year projection, describes MasterControl's ADAPT Platform and its 1,100+ worldwide customers, and quotes GOEO's executive director and EDCUtah's president characterizing the expansion as reinforcing Utah's software/IT, life-sciences, and aerospace-and-defense industry clusters. [Source: EDCUtah release](https://www.edcutah.org/recent-news/mastercontrol-solutions-expands-in-salt-lake-county)
- Utah's public incented-companies dataset lists "Master Control Solutions," approval year 2023, with the same 155-job/$61,099,500-wage/$2,268,064-tax-revenue figures, a 20% credit rate, a 6-year term, and status "Active." [Source: GOEO Incented Companies list](https://business.utah.gov/recruitment/companies/)

## Reliability Notes

This is a primary Utah state-government record (a formal EDTIF/Economic Development Zone action plus the state's public incented-companies dataset), corroborated by an economic-development-corporation press release, so it is High confidence for the facts it states directly: the exact HQ street address, the Cottonwood Heights municipal designation, and the incentive's structure. Job and wage figures are contractual projections tied to the incentive agreement, not verified outcomes — the incented-companies dataset marks the deal "Active," meaning the performance period has not concluded and actual hiring should not be asserted as achieved. Note an unresolved internal discrepancy between GOEO documents: the award page and incented-companies list describe a 20%, 6-year credit, while the June/July 2024 board minutes describe a 25%, 10-year, $2,015,029 credit for what appears to be the same MasterControl Economic Development Zone action; this page does not adjudicate which figure is current and a reader citing the incentive's size should check GOEO directly.

This capture is the GOEO award page only; board-minutes street-address, EDCUtah customer-count, and incented-companies dataset claims were not reconfirmed in this capture.
