## Summary

MasterControl's official website describes a cloud-based quality management system (QMS), manufacturing execution system (MES), and asset-management platform built for life sciences and other regulated manufacturers. It covers the product portfolio (Qx quality management, Mx manufacturing execution, the Qualer-derived asset-management line), the ADAPT AI platform, corporate contact and office locations, careers/culture content, and a news archive of product and certification announcements.

## Useful Claims

- MasterControl describes itself as a provider of cloud-based quality, manufacturing, and asset-management software for life sciences and other regulated industries, and states that over 1,100 companies worldwide use its software. [Source: MasterControl News — SOP Analyzer launch](https://www.mastercontrol.com/news/mastercontrol-launches-ai-powered-sop-analyzer/)
- The company's Advanced Data and Process Technology (ADAPT) Platform is described as a secure, ISO 42001–certified AI foundation that connects quality, manufacturing, and asset-management data and underlies newer AI features such as the SOP Analyzer, a tool that checks standard operating procedures against regulatory requirements. [Source: MasterControl Life Sciences Platform page](https://www.mastercontrol.com/life-sciences-platform/)
- MasterControl announced it achieved FedRAMP Moderate authorization for its Quality Excellence Gov (Qx Gov) offering in May 2025, describing it as the first industry-leading QMS to reach that federal cloud-security standard, sponsored initially by NIAID/NIH. [Source: MasterControl News — FedRAMP authorization](https://www.mastercontrol.com/news/mastercontrol-fedramp-authorization-qms/)
- MasterControl announced the acquisition of Qualer, a San Diego cloud-native asset-management (CMMS/calibration) company, on March 3, 2025, positioning the combination as a single platform spanning QMS, MES, and asset/equipment management; Qualer has since been marketed as MasterControl Asset Excellence. [Source: MasterControl News — Qualer acquisition](https://www.mastercontrol.com/news/mastercontrol-acquires-qualer/)
- The contact page lists corporate headquarters as MasterControl Solutions, Inc., 6350 South 3000 East, Salt Lake City, UT 84121, plus offices in the United Kingdom, Australia, Japan, and India. [Source: MasterControl Contact page](https://www.mastercontrol.com/contact/)
- The careers page describes a mentoring-and-leadership-development culture and reports 117 new hires in 2024, with about 25% of new positions filled internally. [Source: MasterControl Careers page](https://www.mastercontrol.com/careers/)

## Reliability Notes

This is an official company source; product descriptions, platform names, and dated press announcements (FedRAMP authorization, Qualer acquisition, SOP Analyzer launch) are reliable for what the company says it shipped and when. Self-reported scale figures (1,100+ customers, hiring counts, "30 years") and superlatives ("#1 QMS," "first industry-leading QMS") are marketing claims and were not independently verified against a customer registry, headcount filing, or competitive analysis. MasterControl is privately held and does not file public financials, so revenue and valuation figures on this page should be cross-checked against press coverage (e.g., Forbes) rather than treated as audited.
