## Summary

MasterControl's official website describes a cloud-based quality management system (QMS), manufacturing execution system (MES), and asset-management platform built for life sciences and other regulated manufacturers. It covers the product portfolio (Qx quality management, Mx manufacturing execution, the Qualer-derived asset-management line), the ADAPT AI platform, corporate contact and office locations, careers/culture content, and a news archive of product and certification announcements.

## Useful Claims

- MasterControl describes itself as a provider of cloud-based quality, manufacturing, and asset-management software for life sciences and other regulated industries. A separately linked news page makes a customer-scale claim that is not present in this homepage capture. [Source: MasterControl News — SOP Analyzer launch](https://www.mastercontrol.com/news/mastercontrol-launches-ai-powered-sop-analyzer/)

[Source: MasterControl Life Sciences Platform page](https://www.mastercontrol.com/life-sciences-platform/)

## Reliability Notes

This is an official company source; product descriptions, platform names, and dated press announcements (FedRAMP authorization, Qualer acquisition, SOP Analyzer launch) are reliable for what the company says it shipped and when — but this homepage capture does not include those press-release pages. Self-reported scale figures (1,100+ customers, hiring counts, "30 years") and superlatives ("#1 QMS," "first industry-leading QMS") were not present in this capture and were not independently verified against a customer registry, headcount filing, or competitive analysis. The capture shows "FedRAMP-ready" positioning, not a FedRAMP Moderate authorization announcement. MasterControl is privately held and does not file public financials, so revenue and valuation figures on this page should be cross-checked against press coverage (e.g., Forbes) rather than treated as audited.
