## Summary


Cottonwood Heights-based Mercato Partners invests growth capital across technology, consumer brands, and food. Its funds can materially scale portfolio companies, but growth equity is a competitive market with many substitute investors.

## Who It's For

Growth-stage Utah and regional companies with meaningful revenue seeking private equity or growth capital, especially in technology, consumer, and food sectors.

## What It Provides

- Growth-stage private equity and venture-style capital across Mercato fund strategies
- Strategic support for scaling companies including Traverse, Savory Restaurant Fund, Prelude, and Alpha Secondary strategies referenced on the site

Scaling Utah operators past seed stage with substantial revenue. Not a fit for pre-revenue founders or angel-round capital.
