## Summary

MIDA's official organizational overview describes the Utah entity's creation and economic-development role.

## Useful Claims

- MIDA says it is a State of Utah entity created in 2007.
- It describes its purpose as economic development across jurisdictions connecting private and public enterprise with military initiatives.

## Verbatim

> The Military Installation Development Authority (MIDA) is a State of Utah entity created in 2007 to further economic development across multiple jurisdictions, bringing together private and public enterprise and promoting military initiatives.

Locator: “Who We Are.”
