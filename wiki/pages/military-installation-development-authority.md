## Summary

The Military Installation Development Authority (MIDA) is a State of Utah entity created in 2007 to facilitate economic development involving military initiatives and multi-jurisdiction project areas. Its legal powers and limits come from Utah Code Chapter 63H-1; its website publishes current project-area material.

## Who It's For

Local governments, military installations, landowners, developers, public agencies, and residents affected by a MIDA project area.

## What It Provides

Project-area governance, public records, development agreements, infrastructure coordination, and financing or taxing mechanisms authorized by state law.

## How To Use It

Use MIDA's project pages and board records for a specific project, then verify legal authority against current Utah Code and signed instruments. For the Stratos project, see the [MIDA project-area source](stratos-mida-project-area-page.md).

## Cost / Eligibility

Not a general grant or founder-support program. Participation depends on a project area's legal instruments and public approvals.

## Best Fits

Readers tracing authority, commitments, and public process for development tied to military land or a legislatively authorized MIDA project area.
