## Summary

Mountain Heights Academy's public about page: a tuition-free online Utah charter school serving
grades 7–12, with West Jordan contact address and curriculum built partly from open-source materials.

## Useful Claims

- Mountain Heights Academy says it was **founded in 2009** as an alternative to traditional schools.
- The school describes itself as a **tuition-free, online** program for **grades 7–12** available to
  Utah students.
- Contact address on the page: **9067 South 1300 West, Suite 204, West Jordan, UT 84088**; office
  phone **801-721-6329**.
- Curriculum section: the school **builds its own curriculum from existing open-source content and
  from materials our teachers create—all aligned to core standards**.
- The about page does **not** name **Open High School of Utah** or assert a first-in-the-nation open
  licensing claim — that history is documented elsewhere.
