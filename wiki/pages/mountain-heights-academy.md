## Summary


Mountain Heights Academy is a tuition-free online Utah charter school serving grades 7–12 ([NCES record](nces-mountain-heights-academy-record.md)). It expands statewide schooling choice at real scale, but other online schools can provide a close substitute.

## Impact

The lead's first-in-the-nation claim — first U.S. public school to build entirely from openly licensed
materials and release courses under an open license — is **partially supported but not closed**:

- **Supported:** A **2010 Creative Commons** post reports OHSU released **ten semesters** under **CC
  BY**, quotes director **DeLaina Tonks** saying they are **"among the first, if not the first,
  secondary school"** to create and share OER curriculum worldwide
  ([CC post](creative-commons-open-high-school-curriculum-2010.md)).
- **Supported today:** Mountain Heights says it still **builds curriculum from existing open-source
  content** plus teacher-authored materials aligned to standards
  ([about page](mountain-heights-academy-official-about-page.md)).
- **Not supported on current school page:** The 2026 about capture does **not** claim first-in-the-nation
  status or name the 2010 release.
- **Not pulled here:** Utah State Board of Education charter authorization documents that would tie
  the legal charter to an OER-only mandate.

**Bet:** that a charter school can treat openly licensed curriculum as infrastructure — compounding
courseware the way software repos compound code — if teacher labor keeps standards alignment current.

Scale is real in Utah terms: **864 students**, **74 FTE teachers**, ratio **11.66**
([NCES](nces-mountain-heights-academy-record.md)) — a substantial online charter, not a pilot.

## Who It's For

Utah students in grades 7–12 seeking a tuition-free online charter with flexible pacing, 1:1 teacher
access, and concurrent/college-credit pathways ([about page](mountain-heights-academy-official-about-page.md)).
