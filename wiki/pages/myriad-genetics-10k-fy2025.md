## Summary

Myriad Genetics' annual report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, filed with
the SEC on 2026-02-24. Accession number **0000899923-26-000018**; filer CIK **0000899923**; IRS
employer identification number **87-0494517**.

This is the primary record for Myriad's revenue, losses, headcount, headquarters, and laboratory
footprint. Anything on the venture page that a reader would act on — how big the company is, whether
it is growing, where it actually sits in Salt Lake City — should trace here rather than to the
company's marketing site.

`https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyconcept/CIK0000899923/us-gaap/{tag}.json` returns the tagged
financial facts with their source accessions.

## Useful Claims

- Total revenue for FY2025 was **$824.5 million**, down from **$837.6 million** in FY2024 (FY2023:
  **$753.2 million**).
- Net loss for FY2025 was **$365.9 million**, against a net loss of **$127.3 million** in FY2024;
  basic and diluted loss per share was **$3.95**.
- The FY2025 loss includes goodwill impairment charges of **$234.7 million** and intangible asset
  impairment charges of **$82.0 million**, related to the Women's Health and Mental Health reporting
  units.
- Myriad had approximately **2,700 full-time equivalent employees** as of December 31, 2025.
- Principal executive offices are at **322 North 2200 West, Salt Lake City, Utah 84116** — not the
  Research Park address carried by some third-party directories.
- The Salt Lake City corporate headquarters holds approximately **234,000 square feet** of laboratory
  and office space; the lease runs to **2038** with renewal options.
- Laboratories in Salt Lake City, Utah; Mason, Ohio; and South San Francisco, California are CLIA
  certified to perform high-complexity tests.
- Myriad is a Delaware corporation (SIC 2835, in vitro and in vivo diagnostic substances).

## Reliability Notes

A Form 10-K is a primary-tier source: it is filed under securities-law accuracy duties, audited as to
its financial statements, and archived permanently by the SEC. It is the highest-confidence source
available for revenue, losses, headcount, and property, and it supersedes press coverage and the
company's own website on all of those.

Two limits worth stating. First, a 10-K is a point-in-time document — the figures above describe
FY2025 and were superseded for interim periods by the Q1 and Q2 2026 Form 10-Qs (the most recent,
accession 0000899923-26-000072, covers the quarter ended 2026-06-30). Second, forward-looking
statements in the filing are management's expectations, not facts, and carry the filing's own
safe-harbor caveats.
