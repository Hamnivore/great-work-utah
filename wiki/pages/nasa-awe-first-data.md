## Summary

NASA's article announces the first public AWE data release and explains what the early observations contain.

## Useful Claims

- The release covers AWE's first 3,000 International Space Station orbits.
- It includes more than five million infrared images used to derive atmospheric temperature and gravity-wave measurements.
- AWE studies the upward propagation of gravity waves and their influence on the ionosphere.
