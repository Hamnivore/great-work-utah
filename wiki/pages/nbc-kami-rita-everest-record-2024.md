## Summary

AP-distributed report on Kami Rita Sherpa's **30th** Everest summit on **May 22, 2024**, naming the current record holder and nearest competitor.

## Useful Claims

- **Kami Rita scaled Mount Everest for a record 30th time** on **Wednesday, May 22, 2024** (AP via NBC).
- Summit time reported: **7:49 a.m.**; summit elevation cited as **29,032 feet**.
- **Pasang Dawa** is named as the closest competitor with **27 successful ascents**.
- Kami Rita **first climbed Everest in 1994**.
