## Summary


Neighbor Storage runs a marketplace matching unused space with people and businesses needing storage or parking. It can raise utilization and avoid some construction, but climate-controlled and professionally managed facilities remain necessary and public evidence does not quantify displacement.

## Impact

Neighbor's useful bet is that storage capacity can be supplied by existing buildings and parking space rather than only by constructing more dedicated facilities. If the marketplace achieves enough local density, it can give renters a closer option, turn otherwise idle space into host income, and reduce some demand for new storage construction. The counterfactual is not zero storage development—many customers still require purpose-built, climate-controlled or professionally managed facilities—and the company's public materials do not establish how much construction it has displaced.

**Bet:** A trusted, sufficiently dense local marketplace can make underused space a durable substitute for part of the self-storage industry.
