## Summary


NUNUG is a monthly group for dot-NET and C# developers in northern Utah ([official site](nunug-official-website.md)). Its Layton meetings fill a gap north of the Lehi–Provo corridor, but the public calendar does not yet show whether its 2026 comeback is durable.

## Who It's For

.NET and C# developers in Davis, Weber, and northern Salt Lake counties — the Wasatch Front north of the Silicon Slopes corridor, which is under-served by the Lehi- and Provo-centred meetup scene. The group's own framing is people who "want to keep current on new technology and expand our knowledge to other technologies", so it is not narrowly a Microsoft-stack room.

## What It Provides

A monthly hybrid technical gathering with free parking at the advertised Layton venue and an online
option for anyone outside commuting range ([official website](nunug-official-website.md)). The public
site does not publish a current talk schedule.

## How To Use It

Check the official site for an announced meeting before travelling to Mindfire Technology, 872 West
Heritage Park Blvd Suite 200, Layton. The site states a second-Thursday cadence and hybrid format but
currently lists the next meeting as TBA ([official website](nunug-official-website.md)).

Do not treat the dated 2026 update as a current event listing. The site's template contact block also
contains placeholder text with a fictional Washington State address.

## Cost / Eligibility

Open to adults and children alike. The captured official page says food is not provided; it does not
state a price or establish a current event schedule ([official website](nunug-official-website.md)).

## Best Fits

Northern Utah developers seeking a small technical user group rather than a formal training program, and anyone wanting a practitioner's read on where AI coding tools actually help in an enterprise .NET codebase.
