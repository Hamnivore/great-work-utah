## Summary

Nu Skin Enterprises' annual report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025, filed with the
SEC on 2026-02-13. Accession number **0001140361-26-005130**; filer CIK **0001021561**; IRS employer
identification number **87-0565309**; NYSE; Delaware corporation with principal executive offices in
Provo, Utah.

This is the primary record for the scale and trajectory of the largest publicly traded
direct-selling company headquartered in Utah, and it documents a contraction on every operating
measure at once: revenue down 14%, customers down 10%, Paid Affiliates down 11%, Sales Leaders down
19%. The company reported positive net income for 2025 only because it sold a business — the January
2025 sale of Mavely produced a pre-tax gain of roughly $176.2 million.

The filing also gives the cleanest available answer to a question the industry's own vocabulary
obscures: how many of the people in a direct-selling network are actually earning money. Nu Skin
publishes three separate counts — Customers, Paid Affiliates (those who "earned sales compensation
during the three-month period"), and Sales Leaders — and they differ by more than an order of
magnitude.

## Useful Claims

- Revenue for 2025 was **$1.485 billion**, down **14%** from **$1.732 billion** in 2024 (2023:
  **$1.969 billion**) — a decline of roughly a quarter across two years.
- Earnings per share rose to **$3.18** from **$(2.95)** in 2024, but the 2025 result "benefited from
  the January 2025 sale of our Mavely business, which generated a pre-tax gain of approximately
  **$176.2 million**," partly offset by a **$25.1 million** intangible impairment in the Rhyz Other
  segment and a **$28.1 million** non-cash loss on an equity investment. Net income for 2025 was
  **$160.2 million** against a net loss of **$146.6 million** in 2024.
- As of the fourth quarter of 2025, in the core Nu Skin business: **748,796 Customers** (down 10%),
  **129,311 Paid Affiliates** (down 11%), and **30,045 Sales Leaders** (down 19%).
- Nu Skin had approximately **2,800 full- and part-time employees** worldwide as of December 31, 2025,
  plus approximately **5,800 sales employees** in its Mainland China operations, who are counted
  separately.
- Principal executive offices are at **75 West Center Street, Provo, Utah 84601**.
- **Utah footprint per Item 2 (Properties):** corporate headquarters in Provo; principal distribution
  facilities in Provo and Mainland China; research and development centers in Provo and Shanghai; and
  Rhyz manufacturing facilities in **Provo, Draper and West Valley City**. Nu Skin owns these
  properties except that it leases the Provo and West Valley City manufacturing facilities.
- Rhyz — the strategic investment arm Nu Skin formed in 2018, holding consumer, technology, and
  manufacturing companies — generated **$223.6 million**, or **15%**, of 2025 reported revenue
  excluding sales to the core Nu Skin business.
- The company's definitions matter for reading any direct-selling headcount: "Paid Affiliates" are
  those who *earned sales compensation* in the previous three months, while "Customers" includes
  members of the sales force who purchased but excludes consumers who bought from a member of the
  sales force rather than from the company.

## Reliability Notes

A Form 10-K is primary tier: filed under securities-law accuracy duties, audited as to its financial
statements, and kept permanently by the SEC.

Two cautions specific to this filing. First, **the 2025 profit is not an operating result.** Revenue
fell 14% and every sales-force count fell with it; the swing from a $146.6 million loss to a $160.2
million profit is dominated by the gain on selling Mavely. Reading $3.18 of earnings per share as a
recovery would invert what the document says. Second, **Customers, Paid Affiliates, and Sales Leaders
are company-defined operating metrics, not audited figures**, and each is a three-month snapshot;
they are comparable across Nu Skin's own quarters and not comparable to another company's
"distributor" count, because no two direct sellers define these terms alike.

The filing is also the right place to notice what it does *not* report: how much the 129,311 Paid
Affiliates earned. A 10-K carries no distribution of participant income. That figure only appears in
companies' own income-disclosure statements, which are self-reported (see
[dōTERRA's](doterra-earnings-disclosure-2026.md)) or in third-party analysis such as the
[Gardner Institute's](gardner-utah-direct-selling-analysis-2022.md).
