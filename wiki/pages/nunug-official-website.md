## Summary

NUNUG's own site. It establishes the group's identity, cadence, and meeting location — and it is also
a worked example of why a website is weak evidence of whether something is *currently happening*. Much
of this page is stale: the "Latest news" items are dated 2017 to 2020, "Next meeting" reads TBA, and
the footer still carries unedited CMS template placeholder text with a fictional Washington State
address and `email@example.com`.

The page carries a March 2026 update saying hybrid meetings are being hosted that year, but its
"Next meeting" field remains TBA. It supports the group's stated format and cadence, not whether a
particular meeting occurred.

## Useful Claims

- NUNUG is the Northern Utah .NET Users Group, a group of software professionals meeting **once
  monthly on the second Thursday**, open to adults and children of all ages.
- Meetings are hybrid — in person and on Microsoft Teams — as of a site update dated 2026-03-11.
- The in-person venue is **Mindfire Technology, 872 West Heritage Park Blvd Suite 200, Layton, UT
  84041**, with free parking.
- The site states food is not provided.

maintained. The March 2026 update, venue, and cadence are present in the live capture; the older news

what the group publicly advertises, not attendance or completed meetings.
