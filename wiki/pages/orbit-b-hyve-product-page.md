## Summary

Orbit's B-hyve smart-watering product page describing Utah design history and 2021 integration into Husqvarna Group's Gardena Division.

## Useful Claims

- Orbit states B-hyve is made by Orbit Irrigation, which has built sprinkler equipment in Salt Lake City since 1970.
- Orbit states that in 2021 it joined Husqvarna Group's Gardena Division and that the same Utah team designs and supports B-hyve products.

## Verbatim

> "B-hyve is made by Orbit Irrigation, a company that's been building sprinkler equipment in Salt Lake City since 1970. In 2021, Orbit joined Husqvarna Group's Gardena Division, so we're now part of a global water-products family. The same Utah team still designs and supports every B-hyve product."
