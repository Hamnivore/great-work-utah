## Summary


Orbit Irrigation Products makes sprinklers, valves, timers, drip systems, and B-hyve smart-watering controls ([official site](orbit-irrigation-official-website.md)). Utah remains a meaningful design and manufacturing node, but Husqvarna's Swedish ownership means product strategy and capital allocation no longer belong here.

## Impact

Residential outdoor watering is one of the largest discretionary water uses in the arid West. Orbit sits in the supply chain that equips those systems — timers, valves, heads, and increasingly app-connected controllers marketed to cut waste. The 2018 *Utah Business* piece quotes Orbit's CMO describing B-hyve's weather-based programs and **30–50%** water-use reduction claims, with Utah and other states offering rebates
([Utah Business, 2018](utah-business-orbit-product-lifecycle-2018.md)). Those savings figures are company-stated in trade press, not independently measured here.

Scale matters because the products are commodity-adjacent but volume-heavy. Husqvarna's October 2021 release cited roughly **USD 320m** trailing net sales and **1.4 million** connected B-hyve devices
([acquisition press release](husqvarna-orbit-acquisition-press-2021.md)); the 2021 year-end report records **USD 324m** Orbit net sales for 2021 and **1.5 million** connected devices
([year-end report 2021](husqvarna-year-end-report-2021-orbit.md)). Husqvarna framed the deal as creating a **"global market leader in residential watering"** when combining Orbit's North American business with Gardena in Europe — the buyer's characterization, not an independent market-share study
([acquisition press release](husqvarna-orbit-acquisition-press-2021.md)).

**Bet:** that smart controllers and connected timers will matter more for Western water policy than incremental efficiency in industrial cooling — because millions of homeowners each waste a little, and Orbit's installed base is at homeowner scale.

The honest counterweight: post-acquisition strategy is set in Stockholm by Husqvarna/Gardena; Utah remains a manufacturing and design node, but ownership and capital allocation are European. Impact on Utah water outcomes is indirect — through products sold nationwide, not through Utah-specific policy.

## Utah Context

Orbit is one of the larger durable-goods manufacturers still tied to Davis County — North Salt Lake assembly and distribution documented in 2018, with Husqvarna continuing to describe a Salt Lake City-based U.S. business in 2021 filings. That makes it a concrete link between the wiki's `materials-mfg` cluster and Western water use (`energy` as a secondary domain tag for water-as-resource).

The location story is messy: corporate filings and Orbit marketing emphasize Salt Lake City; manufacturing and warehouse work is documented in North Salt Lake; the Bountiful reference remains unresolved. The page records the conflict rather than collapsing it.
