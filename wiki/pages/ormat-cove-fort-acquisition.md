## Summary

Ormat's January 4, 2024 investor release announces the close of its $271 million acquisition of a portfolio of operating geothermal and solar assets from Enel Green Power North America (EGPNA), including the Cove Fort geothermal plant in Beaver County, Utah.

## Useful Claims

- Ormat paid $271 million for 100% of the equity interest in a portfolio of two operating geothermal plants, one triple-hybrid geothermal/solar plant, two standalone solar assets, and two greenfield development rights, located in Nevada, Utah, Connecticut, and California.
- The acquisition raised Ormat's Electricity Segment generating portfolio to 1,215 MW.
- A companion ThinkGeoEnergy report (citing the same transaction) names Cove Fort specifically as "an Ormat-designed binary geothermal power plant" in Beaver County, Utah, selling electricity to the Salt River Project under a long-term power purchase agreement — one of the two greenfield rights acquired sits directly adjacent to the Cove Fort plant.
- CEO Doron Blachar's statement frames the deal as accretive to revenue and EBITDA, with plans to integrate Ormat's own OEC equipment to improve the acquired plants' performance.
