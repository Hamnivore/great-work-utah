## Summary

Ormat Technologies' corporate site describes a Reno, Nevada-based geothermal company that designs, manufactures, owns, and operates geothermal, recovered-energy, and energy-storage power plants worldwide. The investor-relations profile and the careers portal are the two pages most relevant to a Utah-focused reader.

## Useful Claims

- Ormat states it has "over five decades of experience" (founded 1965) and is "the only vertically integrated company engaged in geothermal and recovered energy generation," designing, manufacturing, and selling power plants built around its own Ormat Energy Converter (OEC) binary-cycle units.
- Investor-relations materials give a current total generating portfolio of roughly 1.8 GW (about 1,340 MW geothermal/solar, 495 MW energy storage) headquartered at 6884 Sierra Center Parkway, Reno, Nevada, and trading as NYSE: ORA.
- The corporate careers portal (ormat.com/en/company/careers/main/) is a JS-rendered listing with an interactive "Jobs Map"; it did not return machine-readable Utah-specific openings on a plain fetch, but frames Ormat's hiring around plant operations, engineering, and corporate roles.
- Ormat's 2025 Form 10-K (filed with the SEC) lists Ormat Nevada Inc. as its principal U.S. operating subsidiary and confirms Doron Blachar as CEO.

## Reliability Notes

Official company source, strongest for corporate structure, mission framing, and portfolio totals. The careers/jobs-map pages are JavaScript-rendered and did not yield readable Utah-specific job listings to this fetcher; a human applicant should use the site directly. Utah-specific operating facts (Cove Fort capacity, acquisition terms) are better sourced to the investor press releases and EIA-derived plant data cited on the venture page.
