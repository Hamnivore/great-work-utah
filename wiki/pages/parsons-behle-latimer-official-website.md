## Summary

Parsons Behle & Latimer's official website describes a regional law firm with a Salt Lake City office (plus offices in Idaho, Montana, Wyoming/Jackson, and elsewhere in the Mountain West). Its capability pages cover technology, intellectual property, securities and capital markets, and emerging companies.

## Useful Claims

- The current capture identifies offices across the Intermountain West, including Lehi, Park City, St. George, and Salt Lake City in Utah.
- The homepage describes recent Utah transactional work, including advising Salt Lake City-based Spectrum Engineers in a three-party business combination.
- Earlier claims about technology/IP, securities, emerging-company, and trademark-recognition practices are not present in the captured homepage and require captures of the underlying capability pages.
