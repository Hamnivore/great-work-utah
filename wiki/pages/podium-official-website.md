## Summary

Podium's official website describes a platform for local businesses that combines customer messaging, review management, web chat, payments, phones, automations, and AI-assisted customer communication.

## Useful Claims

- Podium's 2026-08-11 homepage positions the product as an AI Employee for local businesses that manages the customer journey from inquiry through booking.
- The site highlights industry use cases including automotive dealerships, home services, aesthetics, and retail.
- Podium presents an all-in-one AI operating system replacing disconnected tools, with 24/7 lead response and automation features.
- The page previously summarized legacy product categories (customer messaging, reviews, payments, phones, web chat, consolidated inbox); those specific menu labels do not appear on this capture.
