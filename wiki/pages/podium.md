## Impact

Local business software is a large, durable market — tens of millions of small and mid-sized businesses in the United States still manage customer relationships through phone calls, paper, and ad-hoc tools. Podium's bet is that unifying messaging, reviews, and payments in one place creates enough operational leverage that local businesses will pay a recurring subscription indefinitely.

The review-management component has a direct bottom-line impact: online ratings on Google and Yelp drive foot traffic for local businesses, and Podium's automated review-request workflows have a documented track record of increasing review volume and average star rating. Whether that is "impact" or "managed reputation" depends on your frame, but for the businesses involved the effect on revenue is real.

## What They Are Building

The core Podium product is a unified communications inbox: inbound and outbound SMS, Google and Facebook message handling, web chat widgets, automated review-request campaigns, and an integrated payments layer that lets businesses collect payments via text link. The platform integrates with popular vertical-specific business software — dealer management systems, practice management tools, field-service software — so the communication layer sits on top of existing workflows rather than replacing them.

Podium now leans heavily into AI. The current captured homepage emphasizes lead conversion and around-the-clock response rather than the older product taxonomy. [Official Website: Podium](podium-official-website.md)
