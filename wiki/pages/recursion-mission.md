## Summary

Recursion's mission page describes its AI drug-discovery approach, Recursion OS, multimodal biological and chemical data, pipeline focus, BioHive-2 supercomputer, and Salt Lake City headquarters.

## Useful Claims

- Recursion says traditional drug discovery has high clinical failure rates, long timelines, and high cost.
- The page describes more than 50 petabytes of multimodal data across phenomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, ADME, and de-identified patient data.
- The page describes BioHive-2 as the most powerful supercomputer in pharma and notes a 2025 Top500 ranking.
- The page lists the Salt Lake City address.
