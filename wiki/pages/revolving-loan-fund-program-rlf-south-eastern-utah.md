## Summary


SEUALG administers gap loans for startups and expanding businesses in Carbon, Emery, Grand, and San Juan counties that cannot secure ordinary bank financing. That fills a real rural credit gap, but the official program page was unavailable; applicants should request current terms and board timing directly.

## Who It's For

Businesses located in Carbon, Emery, Grand, or San Juan counties that have been turned down by traditional lenders and can document a job-creation or retention plan. Startups and expansion-stage operators in professional, retail, service, IT, and manufacturing sectors are among listed qualifying categories in regional economic-development materials.

## What It Provides

The program is described as non-traditional gap lending, but current loan sizes, repayment periods, and eligible uses are not supported by the captured source.

- Email: [cotto@seualg.utah.gov](mailto:cotto@seualg.utah.gov) (Startup State listing)

## Cost

Application fees, closing costs, and interest-rate terms could not be supported by the captured source. Verify all costs with SEUALG staff before applying.

## Best Fits

Southeastern Utah founders with a bank turndown letter, a feasible business plan, and a credible job-impact story. A weak fit for companies outside the four-county area or seeking venture equity.
