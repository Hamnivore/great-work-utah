## Summary

SafeUT's **Fiscal Year 2025** annual report (July 1, 2024–June 30, 2025), published by the program. This is the best captured source for chat, tip, and emergency-intervention counts; figures are self-reported by the program unless corroborated elsewhere.

## Useful Claims

- Reporting period: **Fiscal Year 2025 (July 1, 2024–June 30, 2025)**.
- **24,716 total chats** across all SafeUT apps; **24,154** K-12 and higher-education chats; **333** Frontline chats; **229** National Guard chats.
- **8,220 total K-12 and higher-education tips**; K-12 tips alone: **8,135**.
- **342 emergency interventions** in FY25, broken down as **199** self-harm/suicide-related, **127** harm-to-others-related, and **16** both; footnotes note how many involved law enforcement/EMS versus de-escalation with school/parent collaboration.
- Top K-12 tip category counts include **Suicide 1,926 (24%)**, **Bullying 1,714 (21%)**, and **Potential School Threat 890 (11%)** — the report labels the last category with an asterisk but the capture does not include the footnote text on the same page spread.
- **~1.9 million** is given as the **total number of people that have access to the apps** (cumulative reach figure, not annual unique users).
- SafeUT counselors are described as holding **master's degrees** and working at **Huntsman Mental Health Institute at University of Utah Health**.
- The report states SafeUT is **nationally recognized** for effectiveness in saving lives and de-escalating **potential school threats** — that recognition claim is the program's own framing, not third-party proof in this document.
