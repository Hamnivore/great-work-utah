## Summary

SafeUT Frontline is a sibling app aimed at health care, fire/EMS, law enforcement, corrections, and public-safety dispatch workers and their families. The page describes the same licensed-counselor staffing model as the main SafeUT app but **without** the school tip feature.

## Useful Claims

- SafeUT Frontline links users to **licensed mental health professionals** who are **trained to understand** frontline workers' challenges.
- Counselors are **licensed with master's degrees in mental health** and **employed by the Huntsman Mental Health Institute at University of Utah Health**.
- Service is described as **confidential**, **free**, and available **24/7, 365 days a year**.
- **SafeUT Frontline does not include the tip feature** that is available on the SafeUT app.
