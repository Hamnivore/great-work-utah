## Summary

SafeUT's public homepage describes a free, confidential crisis chat and school safety tip service for Utah students, parents, and educators, staffed around the clock by licensed counselors employed by Huntsman Mental Health Institute at University of Utah Health.

## Useful Claims

- The homepage states that SafeUT counselors are available **24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year**.
- Chats and tips are handled by **licensed counselors employed by Huntsman Mental Health Institute at University of Utah Health**.
- Counselors are described as **licensed with master's degrees in mental health**.
- The service is described as **always free** to users.
- Users can start a chat, submit a safety tip, or call; the page also references **SafeUT Frontline** and **SafeUT National Guard** as related apps.
- The homepage date range **January 1, 2016–June 30, 2025** appears on a user-testimonials section heading; the capture does not define what metrics that span covers.
