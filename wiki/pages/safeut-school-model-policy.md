## Summary

Utah State Board of Education model policy defining SafeUT roles for schools. It operationalizes the statute's counselor standard in school-facing language.

## Useful Claims

- **SafeUT** is defined as **the crisis line established in Section 53B-17-1202**.
- A **SafeUT Counselor** means a **masters-level mental health counselor employed by HMHI at the University of Utah** who receives SafeUT chats and tips.
- Schools must maintain **Designated Contacts** reachable by SafeUT Counselors when a student is at **imminent risk of harm to self or others**.
