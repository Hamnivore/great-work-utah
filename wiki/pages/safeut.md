## Summary



SafeUT is Utah's statewide, round-the-clock crisis-chat and school-safety tip system, operated by Huntsman Mental Health Institute ([official site](safeut-official-website.md)). Statutory funding and hundreds of annual emergency interventions make it durable state safety infrastructure, though sources do not prove how many attacks it prevented.

## Impact

Breadth is one state and depth is the reason the tier is not lower. In FY2025 the program handled **24,716 chats**, **8,220** K-12 and higher-education tips, and **342 emergency interventions** ([annual report](safeut-annual-report-fy25.md)) — and an emergency intervention in a youth suicide-risk context is about as deep as displacement gets per person reached, which is ruling 6's case for depth substituting for breadth.

Permanence rests on statute rather than on goodwill: [HB373 (2019)](safeut-utah-code-hb373.md) *requires* the operating institute to run the line 24/7 with anonymous school reporting and appropriates **$1.77 million** ongoing from the Education Fund. A program a legislature has written into code and funded on an ongoing basis is structurally different from one dependent on a grant cycle, and that is what makes this a change in how the state works rather than a service the state buys.

The counterfactual is the honest limit on the tier. Crisis lines are not scarce — 988 exists nationally, and other states run school tip lines — so the displacement SafeUT causes is not "these 24,716 conversations would not have happened" but the narrower and still real "they would not have reached **licensed master's-level clinicians** through the same channel that also carries anonymous threat tips, with a designated contact at every participating school." That integration of clinical crisis response with school threat reporting in one statutory channel is the part with no obvious substitute.

Two claims that would raise the tier are **not** supported by anything captured here, and the page declines to lean on them: no captured document publishes a count of averted school attacks (only **890** tips categorized as potential school threats), and no capture confirms that another state completed a replication of the model. If replication were established, the breadth argument would change materially.

## Who It's For

- Utah **K-12 and higher-education** students, parents, and school staff needing confidential crisis chat or school safety tips.
- **Frontline workers** (health care, fire/EMS, law enforcement, corrections, dispatch) and their families via SafeUT Frontline ([Frontline page](safeut-frontline-official-page.md)).
- **Utah National Guard** soldiers, veterans, and families via SafeUT National Guard (referenced on the main site; not separately captured here).

## How To Use It

- Download the **SafeUT** app (iOS/Android) or use **Start a Chat**, **Submit a Tip**, or **Get Help Now** buttons on [safeut.org](safeut-official-website.md).
- Service is **free** and described as **confidential**, with exceptions when counselors believe someone is in imminent danger ([official site](safeut-official-website.md)).
- Schools participate through designated contacts per the [Utah School SafeUT Model Policy](safeut-school-model-policy.md).

Program statistics: see [FY25 annual report](safeut-annual-report-fy25.md) — **24,716** total chats, **8,220** K-12/higher-ed tips, and **342** emergency interventions in FY2025 (July 2024–June 2025), all **self-reported** by SafeUT.

## Open Questions

- **Lead claim not supported in captures:** none of the captured SafeUT documents publish a count of **"averted school attacks"** or similar — only **890** K-12 tips categorized as **Potential School Threat (11%)** in FY25 ([annual report](safeut-annual-report-fy25.md)). Secondary news quotes are not cited here.
- **Other states copying SafeUT:** the [Utah AG page](utah-ag-safeut-far-more-than-an-app.md) states (in **2019** tense) that the commission was **working with several states** — it does not name states or confirm completed rollouts. No capture proves full replication elsewhere.
- **HMHI rename vs statute:** operational pages say **Huntsman Mental Health Institute**; enrolled statute still says **University Neuropsychiatric Institute** ([HB373](safeut-utah-code-hb373.md)). Whether later code sections were updated is uncaptured.
- **Live dashboard counts:** [data dashboard](safeut-data-dashboard.md) metrics load dynamically and were **absent from the HTML capture** — use annual reports for figures.
- **No identifier resolved:** no separate `ein`/`utah-entity` for SafeUT as a standalone legal entity; it operates as a university/state program.
