## Summary

SalesRabbit builds field-sales software for teams that sell door to door or across geographic territories. Its platform supports canvassing, lead and territory management, digital contracts, rep performance and integrations for industries including solar, roofing, pest control and home services.

## Impact

Field selling is operationally difficult: teams work away from desks, leads are geographically scattered and managers have limited visibility into execution. SalesRabbit can reduce that coordination cost and help legitimate service businesses reach customers. The same tooling can also intensify intrusive or misleading sales practices, so product quality includes consent, truthful representation and accountable use—not merely conversion.

## Utah Context

SalesRabbit is based in Lehi and recruits from Utah's software and go-to-market workforce. It is a local path into a mature vertical-software company serving distributed field teams.
