## Summary


Salt Mine Productive Workspace is a 20,000-square-foot Sandy coworking and event facility. Flexible desks, offices, training rooms, and events can support nearby teams, but the effect is tied to occupants and readily replaced by another workspace.

## Who It's For

Startup founders, tech workers, remote teams, and event organizers in the south Salt Lake Valley who need flexible workspace plus meeting and event capacity without managing their own facility.

## What It Provides

Community workspace (weekday access), dedicated desks with 24/7 access, executive private offices, day passes and punch passes, private meeting rooms for 2–14 people, collaborative rooms for 15–30, and large events for 30–250 with AV and catering options. The site also promotes team offsites and conference-room booking.

## Cost

The site publishes Community Space at $149/month, Dedicated Desk at $349/month, day passes at $30, and 5 punch passes at $99. Executive office pricing varies; meeting and event pricing requires inquiry.

## Best Fits

South Valley founders who want published coworking price points, 24/7 desk access, and on-site event infrastructure for teams, accelerators, or community programming.
