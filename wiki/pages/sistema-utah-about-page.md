## Summary

Sistema Utah's about page — program model, enrollment, schedule, cost, and West Valley City sites.

## Useful Claims

- Program began **October 2013** at the **American Preparatory family of schools**, modeled on
  **El Sistema**.
- Serves **nearly 400 students** at **two sites in West Valley City, Utah** — both **Title I**
  schools where **65%** of students receive free or reduced lunch.
- Offered **free of charge** to families; students stay **three hours a day, five days a week**
  (**15 hours** of musical instruction weekly).
- Daily programming includes **group music instruction, general music class, singing class, and
  homework help**, plus a **nutritious snack** daily.
- Advanced performing groups **"Sistema Strings"** and **"Secondary Sistema Strings"** play
  community service concerts.

## Reliability Notes

Self-reported. The IRS-registered address is in Draper (see Form 990 record); operational sites
are described here as West Valley City school locations. The About page does not name Draper as a
program site — the lead's "Draper and West Valley City" pairing reflects the nonprofit's registered
office versus its stated program footprint.
