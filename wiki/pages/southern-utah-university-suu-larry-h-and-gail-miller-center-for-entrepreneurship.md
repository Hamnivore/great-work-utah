## Summary


Southern Utah University's Miller Center gives students across majors startup coursework, mentors, competitions, and hands-on practice. It is a valuable on-ramp in a region with fewer founder resources, but its effect remains campus-scale and replaceable.

## Who It's For

SUU students from any major who want entrepreneurial coursework, mentoring, competitions, and hands-on business experience while in school. Community mentors and partners supporting student ventures may also engage through program events.

## What It Provides

Entrepreneurship programming across education, experience, and elevation pillars; access to staff in BUS 115; event and speaker-series schedules; and pathways to practice starting, managing, and exiting businesses. Specific competitions and funding amounts should be confirmed on active event pages.
