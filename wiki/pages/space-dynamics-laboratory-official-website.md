## Summary

Space Dynamics Laboratory's official website homepage describes SDL as a University Affiliated Research Center and lists public capability areas, news, and contact information. This capture is a thin home page; detailed history, ownership, and workforce claims require About/History page captures.

## Useful Claims

- The homepage describes SDL as "A University Affiliated Research Center (UARC) that delivers cutting-edge mission engineering and technologies that advance critical national priorities across all domains."
- As a UARC, the capture states SDL maintains essential engineering, research, and development capabilities for the DoW.
- Public capability areas listed on the homepage include Satellites, Sensors & Instruments, Ground & Data, and Autonomous Systems.
- Contact address on the capture: 416 East Innovation Avenue, North Logan, UT 84341.
- This capture does not state SDL's founding history, payload flight counts, nonprofit/USU ownership, or dual Space Force and Missile Defense Agency UARC designations.
