## Summary

The county's own hub for Stratos, standing up as a public-records desk for a project the county
approved and then spent the following three months constraining. It links the county's governing
instruments — Resolution 26-11 (MIDA consent), the MIDA–county interlocal agreement 26-15, Ordinance
654 (the 180-day data center moratorium) — plus a project timeline, a referendum page, meeting
recordings, a fact sheet, the Utah DEQ's permitting letter, and the county water master plan. It is
the single best index to the primary records on the county side.

## Useful Claims

- The county publishes Resolution 26-11 (consent to MIDA's creation of the Stratos project area) and
  the Box Elder County–MIDA Stratos agreement 26-15 as public documents.
- The county publishes Ordinance 654, a temporary land use regulation on data centers, adopted after
  the Stratos consent vote.
- The county maintains a Stratos referendum page recording that referendum applications against the
  consent resolutions were filed and rejected.
- The county links a Stratos Project Timeline page, but this hub-page capture does not include
  timeline dates such as MIDA's first formal presentation to the commission or the final
  commission vote at the Box Elder County Fairgrounds.
- The county links out to the developer's own sites, to MIDA's Stratos page, to the Governor's Office
  FAQ, and to MIDA's page on `transparent.utah.gov`.

## Reliability Notes

Self-reported tier, and the self-reporting cuts both ways: this is a county that took an unusual
amount of public heat for the vote, so the page is written partly as a defense of the process. Its
value is as an **index to primary records** — the resolutions, the ordinance, the interlocal
agreement, the DEQ letter — rather than as narrative.

Two caveats. First, the page is a CivicPlus CMS page whose document links are opaque
`/DocumentCenter/View/<id>` URLs; if the county reorganizes, those break, which is why the underlying
documents have their own pages here. Second, **Resolution 26-11 as published is a scanned PDF with no
text layer**, so its text could not be extracted or quoted; the vote it records is corroborated by
the county's own 2026-05-04 press release and by contemporaneous reporting instead.
