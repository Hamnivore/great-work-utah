## Summary

Struvia's homepage — the company formerly named **Bidi Contracting**, whose old domain
`bidicontracting.com` now redirects here. It describes AI construction estimating and bid management
for general contractors: upload plans, get an automated takeoff and itemized estimate, and have
subcontractor bids solicited and leveled.

## Useful Claims

- Struvia performs **automated takeoff and estimating from uploaded plans**, identifying rooms,
  counting doors and windows, measuring area, and flagging structural and permitting details, then
  producing an itemized estimate across trades.
- The stated turnaround is **two days from plan upload to bids in hand**, against a claimed industry
  norm of two weeks.
- It claims a network of **more than 10,000 contractors nationwide** and average savings of **$20K**
  per project.
- Estimates are **calibrated to a customer's own subcontractors' bid history** rather than to a
  national cost database, which the site says could be 20–40% off.
- The company also **solicits and levels subcontractor bids**, contacting qualified subs from both the
  customer's network and Struvia's.
- Named founders: **Baylor Jeppsen (CEO)** and **Weston Burnett (CTO)**, both BYU.
- The old domain `bidicontracting.com` **redirects to this site**, which is how the two names are tied
  to one company. The company's own account of the rename is on
  [its founder's author page](struvia-founder-author-page.md).

## Reliability Notes

Self-reported tier throughout, and the marketing figures are the weakest part. **"10,000+
contractors", "$20K average savings", "2 days", and "trusted by industry leaders nationwide" are all
unattributed vendor claims** with no named customer, no method, and no date. The savings figure in
particular is the kind of number that is derived from a small number of favorable cases; nothing here
allows it to be checked, and it should never be repeated as a measured outcome.

One thing this capture does establish firmly, because it is structural rather than promotional: **the
rebrand.** `bidicontracting.com` resolves with an HTTP redirect to `struvia.co`, which ties the two
names to one company without relying on anyone's assertion that a rename happened. The founders'
identities and the Utah tie come from
[the founder author page](struvia-founder-author-page.md), captured separately.

Nothing on this site is independent of the company. No funding record, corporate registration,
customer name, or third-party review appears in this capture.
