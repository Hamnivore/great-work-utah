## Summary

Struvia claims to automate construction takeoffs, estimates, and subcontractor bid comparisons from uploaded plans. That could improve contractor workflows, but the differentiating claim has the weakest evidence on the page; without corroborated use or scale, there is no impact to rank.
## Impact

Estimating and subcontractor outreach are the coordination bottleneck for small and midsize general
contractors, and Struvia's bet is that automating both together lets a contractor bid more jobs with
the same staff. The product is live, actively developed, and specific about what it does, which is
more than can be said for most software at this stage.

**What is not established is any of it.** Every figure the company publishes — 10,000+ contractors in
its network, $20,000 average savings per project, "trusted by industry leaders nationwide" — is an
unattributed vendor claim with no named customer, no method, and no date. No funding record, corporate
registration, third-party review, or customer reference has been located. A trade blog covered the
product in July 2026, but in a register close enough to the company's own marketing that it does not
function as independent verification.

So: a working product from identifiable founders, and essentially no external evidence about whether
it works at the scale claimed. **Confidence on this page is Low, and it should stay there until an
independent record turns up.**

## Utah Context

Struvia's own site places its CEO in **Utah County** and identifies both founders as BYU graduates —
Baylor Jeppsen (CEO, Information Systems) and Weston Burnett (CTO, Finance). The company publishes
contractor directories for Utah markets.

Its Utah significance is as a **BYU-originated software company aimed at the construction industry**,
which is a pairing the state has more of than most: Utah has both a large residential and commercial
construction sector and a university pipeline producing software founders, and Struvia sits at the
join. The founders' stated route in — the university's Sandbox entrepreneurship program, per the
company's own public author page — is the same route several other entries in this corpus took.

The location is the company's own claim about itself. **No corporate registration confirming a Utah
domicile has been located**, so whether Utah County is the legal headquarters or the founders' base
remains open.

## What They Need Now

No public hiring page was located. The company solicits general contractors and subcontractors to
join its bidding network, which is the participation it asks for.

What it most needs from this wiki's perspective is **anything checkable**: a named customer, a
registration, a funding record, or a third party willing to say the estimates hold up. The product
claims are strong and the evidence base is entirely self-supplied.

## Open Questions

- **Is there any independent public record of this company?** None was located — no SEC filing under
  either name, no corporate registration, no funding announcement, no named customer, no substantive
  press. This is the question that governs the page's Confidence.
- **When and why did Bidi Contracting become Struvia**, and did anything change besides the name? The
  redirect and the stale byline establish that it happened; nothing located says when.
- **Are the "10,000+ contractors" real users or a directory?** The distinction is the whole difference
  between a network and a scraped list, and the site does not draw it.
- **How is "$20,000 average savings" computed, across how many projects?** No method is given.
- **How much of an estimate is generated automatically and how much needs human review?** The company
  claims automation across all major trades; nothing establishes the accuracy in practice.
- **Is Utah County the legal headquarters or the founders' current base?**
- **Beware the name collision:** at least one unrelated venture also used the name "Bidi" in the same
  period and market-adjacent space. Anything attributed to "Bidi" should be checked against the
  construction product specifically before being added here.
