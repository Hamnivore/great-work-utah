## Summary

SunPower Corp. Exhibit 99.2 press release, filed 2021-10-05 with Form 8-K accession
**0000867773-21-000063** (CIK **0000867773**), announcing acquisition of Blue Raven Solar.

## Useful Claims

- SunPower announced it **acquired Blue Raven Solar**, described in the release as **one of the fastest
  growing residential solar providers in the U.S.**
- Purchase price: **cash consideration of up to $165 million**.
- Blue Raven was **founded in 2014** per the release; SunPower describes a direct-to-consumer sales
  and installation model with high growth.
- SunPower said Blue Raven CEO **Ben Peterson** would join SunPower's executive team.
- SunPower stated it would **focus on residential and light commercial growth** and explore strategic
  alternatives for its commercial and industrial solutions business.

## Verbatim

> "Today, SunPower Corp. (NASDAQ:SPWR), a leading solar technology and energy services provider, announced it acquired Blue Raven Solar, one of the fastest growing residential solar providers in the U.S."
