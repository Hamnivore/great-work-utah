## Summary

SunPower Corp. Exhibit 99.1 press release filed 2024-08-06 with Form 8-K accession
**0000867773-24-000086**, announcing voluntary **Chapter 11** petitions and a stalking-horse asset
purchase agreement with **Complete Solaria, Inc.** for Blue Raven Solar and other businesses.

## Useful Claims

- SunPower entered an asset purchase agreement with **Complete Solaria, Inc. (NASDAQ: CSLR)** as
  **stalking horse buyer** for assets associated with SunPower's **Blue Raven Solar** business, New
  Homes business, and non-installing dealer network.
- SunPower and subsidiaries filed **voluntary petitions under Chapter 11** in the U.S. Bankruptcy
  Court for the District of Delaware.
- Under the APA terms stated in the release, Complete Solaria would acquire the assets for **$45
  million in cash**, subject to court approval and higher bids.
- SunPower stated it intended to **operate Blue Raven Solar and New Homes through the Chapter 11
  process** while pursuing sale transactions for remaining assets.
