## Summary

Teal Drones' website (and the Red Cat press releases hosted on it) describe Teal as a Salt Lake City sUAS maker and a Red Cat Holdings subsidiary, centered on the Black Widow reconnaissance drone and the U.S. Army SRR Program of Record.

## Useful Claims

- The homepage banner states Red Cat's next-generation Teal system will be the Army's Program of Record SRR sUAS; per [Red Cat SRR Production Selection](red-cat-srr-production-selection.md), Red Cat announced production selection as the SRR winner on November 19, 2024.
- George Matus is named as Teal's founder and Red Cat's CTO — not present in this homepage capture.
- Red Cat is described as operating two subsidiaries — Teal Drones and FlightWave Aerospace — with a "Family of Systems" including Black Widow, TRICHON, and the NDAA-compliant FANG FPV line — not present in this homepage capture.

## Reliability Notes

This is an official company/parent source: reliable for product names, corporate structure, and the fact of the SRR selection, but not neutral for capability superlatives ("most advanced in its class"). The November 19, 2024 press-release date, George Matus founder/CTO attribution, FlightWave subsidiary structure, and TRICHON/FANG product names were not present in this homepage capture and need corroboration from Red Cat press releases. Quantities (~5,880 systems), contract value (>$250M), revenue figures, and Air Force/NATO opportunities come from secondary trade press and Red Cat financial disclosures and are date-sensitive. Corroborated against army.mil reporting on SRR Tranche 2 production.
