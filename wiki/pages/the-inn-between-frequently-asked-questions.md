## Summary

The INN Between's FAQ page — licensing status, bed count, founding dates, self-reported outcomes
through January 2025, operating budget, and referral criteria. Captured 2026-08-11.

## Useful Claims

- The INN Between is a **licensed Assisted Living Type II Medical Respite Facility**, not a
  homeless shelter; admission requires a **medical referral** and assigned 24/7 bed.
- Provides both **end-of-life placement** for terminally ill individuals and **temporary
  placement for medically frail individuals** recovering from illness or injury.
- **Incorporated December 3, 2014** as a 501(c)(3); **opened August 17, 2015**.
- **80 beds** currently; **28 FTE** staff; **$98** stated daily cost per bed; **$2,623,950**
  annual operating budget to operate 80 beds.
- Outcomes **through January 2025**: **1,00+ clients served** (as printed on the page),
  **184** end-of-life deaths with dignity, **28** clients who rebounded to community housing,
  **25%** of recuperative-care residents in FY24 exited to stable housing.
- **Federal Tax ID 47-2329595**; facility address **1216 E 1300 S, Salt Lake City, Utah
  84105**.
- **Stated budget composition — and it does not reconcile with the organization's own Form 990.**
  The FAQ breaks the $2,623,950 budget into **Government Grants 36%**, **Direct Public Support 35%**,
  **Program Related Income 25%**, **Fundraising Events 3%**, **Other 1%**. The FY2025 Form 990
  ([Form 990 record](the-inn-between-form-990-record.md)) reports contributions at
  **93.5%** of revenue and program service revenue of **$9,872** — roughly **0.4%**, against the
  FAQ's 25%, which on this budget would be about $650,000. The two cannot both describe the same
  year. The FAQ figures are undated and may be a forward projection for 80-bed operation, or may
  categorize government grants as program-related income; the wiki does not adjudicate. Anyone using
  either figure should say which document it came from. Resolving it needs Form 990 Part VIII.
- Executive Director **Jillian Olmsted** is named as a public contact.
- In **2025**, over **400 volunteers** gave over **4,000 hours** of service.
- **2025**: recognized as a **Certified Medical Respite Facility** by the National Health Care
  for the Homeless Council (NHCHC).

## Verbatim

> The INN Between is a licensed Assisted Living Type II Medical Respite Facility, not a homeless shelter. Homeless shelters accept walk-up clients who are seeking emergency shelter on a night-to-night basis. The INN Between is a medical respite facility, meaning it provides temporary housing to individuals who are too ill to be on the streets but not ill enough to be in the hospital. Unlike shelters, The INN Between requires a medical referral for each potential client, and we schedule a projected length of stay, assign a 24/7 bed, and provide access to medical care along with intensive case management and comprehensive emotional and social support.
