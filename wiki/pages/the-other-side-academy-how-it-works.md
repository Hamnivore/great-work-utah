## Summary

The academy's "How It Works" page — its own statement of program length, cost, funding model,
501(c)(3) status, and therapeutic-community philosophy.

## Useful Claims

- Minimum commitment is **30 months** — described as **2 years residential** plus **6 months of
  structured transitional living**.
- Program is **free**; organization states it **does not receive government money** and is
  **self-sufficient**, with **revenues from training schools covering most program expenses** — the
  page does not claim training-school revenue covers every expense.
- Identifies as a **501(c)(3) public non-profit** serving **criminals, homeless and substance
  abusers**.
- Describes a **peer-led therapeutic community** without doctors, clinical therapists, or
  counselors; many students attend as an **alternative to incarceration**.

## Verbatim

> "Qualified students must be willing to make a minimum 30-month (2 years residential accompanied with 6 months of structured transitional living) commitment to a deep rigorous process of personal change."

> "The Other Side Academy is free to everyone who is accepted into the program. We do not receive government money and we are self-sufficient, with revenues from our training schools covering most of our program expenses."
> — "How It Works", "30 Month Commitment"

> "The Other Side Academy is a 501(c)(3) public non-profit organization where criminals, homeless and substance abusers can change their lives, free of charge."

> "We do not have doctors, clinical therapists, or counselors at The Other Side Academy. We are not a program based on counseling or therapy."
> — "How It Works", "The How"
