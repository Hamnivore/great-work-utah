## Summary

The Other Side Village homepage — a related project's own description of its Salt Lake City
therapeutic housing community for people moving beyond chronic homelessness.

## Useful Claims

- Describes itself as a **therapeutic community** helping individuals move beyond **chronic
  homelessness** through **permanent housing**, accountability, work, and connection.
- States it is a **master-planned, therapeutic community located in Salt Lake City, Utah** with
  **permanent housing** for people transitioning out of chronic homelessness.
- An **Our Impact** section publishes outcome statistics (sobriety, employment, housing stability)
  without methodology in the captured text — treat as self-reported organizational claims only.
- The captured text does not use the phrase **tiny home**.

## Reliability Notes

Self-reported tier for both descriptive and outcome content. Endorsements on the page (Mayor Erin
Mendenhall, Rep. John Curtis, etc.) establish political support, not independent program
evaluation. The "Our Impact" statistics — sobriety, employment, cost savings — are published without
denominator definitions, survey methods, or third-party audit in the captured text; the wiki does not
treat them as established outcomes.

Relationship to The Other Side Academy is implied by shared leadership branding and a Rep. Curtis
quote referencing both, but this capture does not define legal or operational structure between the
two entities.
