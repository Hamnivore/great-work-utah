## Summary

Thera-T Pharmaceutics' official homepage describes its MATCH (Multi-Antigen T-Cell Hybridizers) platform for B-cell leukemias, lymphomas, and multiple myeloma, AI/ML-guided dosing models, three listed published works, and leadership including Thomas Gambles as president and co-founder and Jindřich Kopeček as co-founder and scientific advisor.

## Useful Claims

- Thera-T describes MATCH as a multi-antigen T-cell-engager platform targeting B-cell leukemias, lymphomas, and multiple myeloma.
- The company says its platform can produce combinations of bispecific antibody-binding fragments and is developing AI/ML models for personalized bispecific immunotherapeutics.
- The site identifies Thomas Gambles as president and co-founder and Jindřich Kopeček as co-founder and scientific advisor.
- The capture lists three published works, but not the *Advanced Healthcare Materials* title previously asserted.
- Viral-manufacturing, University of Utah laboratory and accelerator ties, funding, animal studies, and the 2025 award do not appear in this capture and require separate sources.
