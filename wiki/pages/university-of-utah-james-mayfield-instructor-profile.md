## Summary

University of Utah Continuing Education / Osher Lifelong Learning instructor biography for **James B.
Mayfield**, describing his University of Utah career and cofounder role at CHOICE Humanitarian.

## Useful Claims

- Mayfield is described as **professor emeritus** after teaching at the **University of Utah for nearly
  forty years**, with specialization in **rural development** and **world poverty**.
- The page states: **"As the cofounder of Choice Humanitarian, James has led expeditioners on two- to
  three-week trips into Mexico, Guatemala, Peru, Ecuador, Bolivia, Egypt, Kenya, India, and Nepal."**
- This is the university's biography of an affiliated instructor, not CHOICE's own program page.

## Verbatim

> "James B. Mayfield is presently a professor emeritus, having taught at the University of Utah for nearly forty years."
> — Instructor bio opening

> "As the cofounder of Choice Humanitarian, James has led expeditioners on two- to three-week trips into Mexico, Guatemala, Peru, Ecuador, Bolivia, Egypt, Kenya, India, and Nepal."
> — Instructor bio
