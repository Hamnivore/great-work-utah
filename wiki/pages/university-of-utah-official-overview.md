## Summary

The university's official overview states its mission and describes its Salt Lake City campus, academic breadth, research role, and academic medical center.

## Useful Claims

- The University of Utah is a public research university in Salt Lake City and a member of the Association of American Universities.
- It is home to Utah's academic medical center.
- Its mission combines student success, knowledge creation, innovation, community engagement, health, and quality of life.

## Verbatim

> Located in the bustling urban landscape of Salt Lake City, the University of Utah stands as the state's flagship institution and the region's hub of higher education. Also known as “the U,” we are a Tier 1 research university and a member of the exclusive Association of American Universities. Home to the state's only academic medical center, we offer world-class health care education and patient care.

Locator: “University Overview.”

> The University of Utah fosters student success by preparing students from diverse backgrounds for lives of impact as leaders and citizens. We generate and share new knowledge, discoveries, and innovations, and we engage local and global communities to promote education, health, and quality of life.

Locator: “Our Mission.”
