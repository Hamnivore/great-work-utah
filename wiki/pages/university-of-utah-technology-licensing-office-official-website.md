## Summary

The official site of the University of Utah Technology Licensing Office (TLO) — the university's centralized invention-disclosure, patent-licensing, and startup-formation office, renamed from the "PIVOT Center" in October 2023. It publishes the disclosure process, faculty commercialization guide, non-dilutive funding programs, and a searchable catalog of technologies available for licensing.

## Useful Claims

- The office was called the PIVOT Center (Partners for Innovation, Ventures, Outreach & Technology) from 2020 until it was renamed the Technology Licensing Office on October 31, 2023; earlier names were the Technology & Venture Commercialization office (2013), Tech Commercialization Office (2006), Tech Transfer Office (1986), and Product and Patent Development Office (1965).
- The office is located at 303 Chipeta Way, #14, Salt Lake City, UT 84108 (Research Park), phone 801-581-7792.
- The disclosure process runs: disclose via the Faculty Portal → technology-manager review and expert assessment → IP protection with outside patent counsel → marketing/licensing decision → license negotiation → royalty/investment income distribution per university policy.
- The office processes 200+ invention disclosures per year and has facilitated filing/issuance of 1,300+ patents for University of Utah faculty; a 2025 AUTM survey ranked the university among the top 10 U.S. universities for invention disclosures (No. 5), licenses executed (No. 5), copyright licenses (No. 4), gross license income (No. 9), and new licensed technologies (No. 3).
- Non-dilutive funding programs include Tech Discovery MicroGrants and Ascender Grants; the office also supports faculty applying to NSF I-Corps (ZAP, BOOM, Nationals tracks) and SBIR/STTR.
- A public Available Technologies catalog lists licensable University of Utah technologies by category (Therapeutics, Diagnostics, Medical Devices, Biotechnology, Computing, Materials, and more), searchable by researcher name.
- Featured 2026 spinout examples include Epitel (wireless EEG monitoring, $26M Series B), NanoKar Therapeutics (CAR-T platform technology), Geothermal Strategy Partners, EnduraCure, Grid Elevated, and CaLycia Bioscience.

## Reliability Notes

This is an official institutional source and is the right evidence for the office's own name history, process, programs, and self-reported statistics. The 2025 AUTM ranking figures are self-reported summaries of an external survey and should be checked against the AUTM survey itself before being treated as independently verified. Provider-reported outcomes (funding amounts, patent counts, rankings) should be treated as institutional claims rather than neutral third-party verification.
