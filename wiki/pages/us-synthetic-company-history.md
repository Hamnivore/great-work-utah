## Summary

The company's own timeline. It is the source for the founding year, the founder's name, the pivot
date, and — the reason it matters for this wiki — the company's own statement that it built **cubic
press technology** for diamond synthesis, which is the apparatus family
[H. Tracy Hall](h-tracy-hall-diamond-presses.md) invented at BYU.

## Useful Claims

- **Founded in 1978 by Louis Pope**, as a diamond grit manufacturer — not as a drill-bit cutter
  company. The pivot came later.
- The company says it developed **proprietary cubic press technology** for high-pressure,
  high-temperature diamond synthesis.
- **1983: first PDC inserts.** The timeline's own gloss on the founding period is "Our main goal back
  then, was just to survive."
- **1993:** first PDC manufacturer to become ISO 9000 certified, by the company's account.
- **By 1997** it describes itself as the industry's leading supplier of diamond products.
- Current stated directions beyond oil and gas: **mining tools, gemstone enhancement technology, and
  diamond bearings** for harsh-environment applications.

Note what this page does *not* say: it names Louis Pope alone as founder and never mentions Bill
Pope, MegaDiamond, H. Tracy Hall, or Duane Horton. The lineage claim comes from
[Deseret News' 2010 feature](deseret-news-bill-pope-obituary-2010.md) and is not corroborated here.

## Verbatim

> "The US Synthetic story started in 1978, when Louis Pope founded a small diamond grit manufacturing company. Over the years, Louis and his team of synthetic diamond experts expanded and evolved the business—by developing innovative proprietary cubic press technology for high-pressure, high-temperature diamond synthesis and manufacturing polycrystalline diamond cutters (PDCs) for the oil and gas exploration industry."
> — "Our Story"

> "Founded by Louis Pope"
> — Company timeline, 1978

> "Our main goal back then, was just to survive. And, in many ways it was a miracle we did."
> — Company timeline, 1978

> "US Synthetic finally found its place and started to become successful at making PDC for the oil and gas industry."
> — Company timeline, 1983

> "In 1993, US Synthetic became the first PDC manufacturer to become ISO 9000 certified."
> — "Our Story"

> "And by 1997, US Synthetic had become the industry’s leading supplier of diamond products."
> — "Our Story"

## Reliability Notes

Self-reported tier, and a corporate timeline is the genre most likely to compress an origin story
around a single name. The omission of Bill Pope is the specific thing to be careful about: a
contemporary newspaper account and this page disagree about who founded the company, and the wiki
page states both rather than picking.

The ISO 9000 and "leading supplier by 1997" claims are unverified against any third party and are
attributed on the fact page rather than asserted.
