## Summary

USANA Health Sciences' annual report on Form 10-K for fiscal year 2025 — a 53-week year running
December 29, 2024 to January 3, 2026 — filed with the SEC on 2026-03-16. Accession number
**0000896264-26-000021**; filer CIK **0000896264**; IRS employer identification number
**87-0500306**; NYSE.

This is the primary record for how large USANA's Utah direct-selling business is and which direction
it is moving. The load-bearing fact in it is a divergence: consolidated net sales rose 8.3% while the
**core nutritional segment — the direct-selling business — fell 6.9%**, with the entire increase
coming from Hiya, a children's-vitamin brand USANA acquired in December 2024 that sells
direct-to-consumer rather than through a sales force.

## Useful Claims

- Consolidated net sales for FY2025 were **$925.3 million**, up **8.3%** ($70.8 million) from FY2024.
- The increase came from **Hiya**, which added **$130.0 million** of incremental sales; the core
  nutritional segment declined.
- Core nutritional net sales were **$793.3 million**, **down 6.9%** year over year.
- Net earnings attributable to USANA fell **74.4%** to **$10.8 million**, against $42.0 million in
  FY2024 and $63.8 million in FY2023 — attributed to a lower operating margin and a higher effective
  tax rate.
- USANA had approximately **1,570 employees working in 22 countries** as of January 3, 2026, measured
  by full-time equivalency; **41%** of employees are in the United States and **28%** in China, and
  **56%** are female.
- USANA counted approximately **387,000 active Customers** worldwide in the core nutritional business
  as of January 3, 2026. "Active Customers" combines Brand Partners (who may sell) with Preferred
  Customers (who may not), and counts anyone who purchased in the most recent three months — it is not
  a count of people earning money.
- Principal executive offices are **3838 West Parkway Blvd., Salt Lake City, Utah 84120**.
- Manufacturing and quality control sit at the Salt Lake City headquarters, with a separate
  54,000-square-foot adjacent facility for food products; USANA states it manufactures approximately
  **56%** of its products in-house.
- Fiscal 2025 was a **53-week** year; fiscal 2024 and 2023 were 52-week years, so year-over-year
  comparisons carry an extra week on the 2025 side.

## Reliability Notes

A Form 10-K is primary tier: filed under securities-law accuracy duties, audited as to its financial
statements, and kept permanently by the SEC. For revenue, earnings, headcount, and headquarters it
supersedes the company's own website and any press coverage.

Three limits. First, the **active Customer count is a company-defined metric**, not an audited
financial statement figure: it merges sellers with discount buyers and resets on a three-month
window, so it cannot be read as "people running a USANA business" or compared across companies whose
definitions differ. Second, the 53-week fiscal year inflates FY2025 against FY2024 by roughly a week
of trading. Third, this filing describes a fiscal year that ended January 3, 2026 and was already
partly superseded when captured: the Q1 FY2026 Form 10-Q (accession 0000896264-26-000034) reports
$250.2 million of net sales for the quarter ended April 4, 2026.
