## Summary

USANA Health Sciences, Inc. is a publicly traded health-products company headquartered in Salt Lake City. It manufactures nutritional, personal-care, skincare and weight-management products and distributes them internationally primarily through direct selling. Its Utah headquarters also houses manufacturing, quality control, distribution, administration, and research and development.

## Impact

USANA's largest durable effect is the combination of a global direct-sales network with substantial Utah manufacturing and quality-control capacity. Products that improve nutrition could affect many customers, while dependable manufacturing employment and technical careers have a more readily observable local effect. The bounds are mixed: supplement benefits depend on evidence for each formulation, and a distributor-led sales model can shift commercial risk from the company toward individual sellers. The wiki should not infer health outcomes from the company's positioning alone.

**Bet:** In-house science, manufacturing and quality systems can make a direct-sold health-products business more trustworthy and useful than one built mainly around branding and outsourced supply.

## Utah Context

USANA's FY2024 Form 10-K identifies 3838 West Parkway Boulevard in Salt Lake City as its principal executive offices. The filing describes a company-owned headquarters containing manufacturing and quality control, distribution, administrative functions, and research and development, plus an adjacent foods-manufacturing facility. The company's careers material calls the site its West Valley City home office; both descriptions refer to the same Salt Lake County operation.
