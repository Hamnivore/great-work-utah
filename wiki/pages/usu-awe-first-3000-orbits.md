## Useful Claims

- The dataset covers 3,000 orbits and more than five million images.
- USU faculty lead mission science, while USU's Space Dynamics Laboratory built the flight instrument.
- The observations examine how lower-atmosphere gravity waves reach the upper atmosphere.
