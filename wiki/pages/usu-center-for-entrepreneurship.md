## Summary

The Utah State University Center for Entrepreneurship page describes a Huntsman School resource for entrepreneurial leadership, classes, internships, competitions, mentoring, speaker access, student resources, and related programs.

## Useful Claims

- The center says it helps students develop skills such as creativity, self-reliance, tenacity, persistence, and resourcefulness through classes, internships, and competitions.
- The public navigation points to an Entrepreneurship Minor, Entrepreneur Leadership Series, student entrepreneur resources, competitions, SEED, Entrepreneurship Club, founders board, calendar, and contact information.
- The page describes mentoring and classes as ways to turn ideas into opportunities.

## Verbatim

> "The Center for Entrepreneurship will help you foster skills such as creativity, self reliance, tenacity, persistence and resourcefulness through classes, internships and competitions that will help optimize your value as an individual to make an impact in the world and become an entrepreneurial leader."

> "Turn your ideas into opportunities for success."

> "We offer dozens of internships where students put what they learn into practice."
