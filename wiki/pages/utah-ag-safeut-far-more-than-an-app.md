## Summary

Utah Attorney General's Office description of SafeUT's origins and partners. Useful for legislative/AG partnership claims and for how Utah officials describe interest from other states — with a stale-date warning on one sentence.

## Useful Claims

- SafeUT is described as a **free statewide service** providing **real-time crisis intervention** to Utah students, parents, and educators.
- Students can message **licensed clinicians**, call a crisis counselor, or submit confidential tips; the app is staffed **24 hours a day, 365 days a year** at the **University Neuropsychiatric Institute (UNI)** — statutory/pre-rename language on this page.
- The program **was developed with funding from the Utah State Legislature** in collaboration with the **Utah Attorney General's Office**, **UNI / U of U Health**, the **Utah State Board of Education**, and the **Utah Anti-Bullying Coalition**.
- The page states that in **2019**, **the SafeUT Commission is currently working with several states to implement similar programs** — the page does not name states or document completed rollouts, and the sentence is dated to 2019 on a page whose footer reads **2026**.
- The page does **not** establish that other states have fully copied SafeUT — only that Utah officials were working with several states as of the page's 2019-tense wording.
