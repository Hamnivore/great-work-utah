## Summary

June 2018 Utah Business feature on Orbit's North Salt Lake product-development, manufacturing, and distribution workflow for the B-hyve smart controller.

## Useful Claims

- Product ideas for lines such as B-hyve begin at Orbit's North Salt Lake headquarters, per Orbit executive Mitch Lord.
- For the classic B-hyve model, final assembly was completed in Orbit's North Salt Lake facilities after mixed local and Asian component sourcing.
- Finished B-hyve products ship from Orbit's distribution warehouse in North Salt Lake.
