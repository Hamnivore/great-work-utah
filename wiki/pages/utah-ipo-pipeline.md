## Overview

**Entrata prices next.** The Lehi property-management company is the only Utah issuer with a live S-1, it is profitable, and Silver Lake already took a dividend off the cap table. Two months without a price is a window problem, not a withdrawal. If the question is who files *after* that, the guess is [Lucid Software](lucid-software.md), not [Podium](podium.md).

**Bet:** Entrata lists on the NYSE as ENT before any other Utah company in this corpus files a new IPO registration. Falsified by a Form RW, or by someone else pricing first.

The rest of this page is the filing record that bet sits on, plus the names people will mention that are not actually next.

## What Counts As Pipeline

A guess still has to name its evidence. The checkable signal is a live Securities Act registration that has not gone effective and has not been withdrawn. Unicorn labels and 2021 pre-IPO headlines are not that signal. As of 2026-08-16, this EDGAR check found one live Utah-city IPO registration: Entrata.

A name is in the live pipeline only if EDGAR shows a Securities Act registration (typically Form S-1 or F-1) that has not gone effective (no Form EFFECT, no Form 424B4) and has not been withdrawn (no Form RW). A confidential draft (DRS) is invisible to a public search until the issuer files publicly; see Open Questions.

The 2026-08-16 check used two EDGAR surfaces:

1. Each issuer's submissions JSON at `data.sec.gov` (the machine-readable filing list).
2. Full-text search for Forms S-1, S-1/A, F-1, and 424B4 that mention Utah cities, dated 2025-01-01 through 2026-08-16.

Two hits were Utah-string false positives and are ignored here: FullPAC (Virginia Beach) and Black Rock Coffee Bar (Scottsdale). Scale, "unicorn" labels, and 2021 "pre-IPO round" headlines are not a live registration.

## The Guess Among Names That Have Not Filed

If Entrata is taken off the board — withdrawn, or already public — the next Utah IPO is not the company the local press has been naming since 2021.

[Podium](podium.md) is the crowd favorite: Lehi, unicorn headlines, the company this wiki's own Open Questions already ask about an IPO path. That is why it is a bad pick. A name that has been "the next Utah IPO" for years and is still private has demonstrated it can stay private. [Qualtrics](qualtrics.md) is private again after the 2023 Silver Lake take-private — a sponsor that just took Entrata to an S-1 is not automatically walking Qualtrics back to Nasdaq on the same clock. [Instructure](instructure.md) is the PE re-IPO shape, but the page's 2024 KKR take-private is early to flip. [Ancestry](ancestry.md) is a Blackstone exit when Blackstone wants one, which is their calendar. [BambooHR](bamboohr.md) and [MasterControl](mastercontrol.md) can stay private; that is the point of how they were built. [Sword Health](sword-health.md) is a dual-HQ company that does not have to list as a Utah event.

The guess among the unfiled names is [Lucid Software](lucid-software.md): South Jordan enterprise SaaS, a 2021 unicorn print, a conventional public-market story (diagramming and whiteboarding against Miro and Microsoft), and no public record of a 2021-style “pre-IPO” fake-start. It is still a guess. This corpus and the 2025–2026 Utah-city S-1 search did not surface a live IPO registration for Lucid or any of the others as of 2026-08-16.

[Cross Creek](cross-creek.md) is a late-stage and crossover investor, not an issuer.
