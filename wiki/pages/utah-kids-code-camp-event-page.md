The public Utah Geek Events page for Kids Code Camp 2026. It establishes the scheduled date, venue,

## Useful Claims

- Kids Code Camp 2026 was held **Saturday, August 1, 2026** at **Neumont University, 143 South Main
  Street, Salt Lake City, UT 84111**.
- **All classes are free.** The camp is for kids aged 5 and up, of all abilities, and runs as a
  half-day to full-day of instruction.
- The format is five sessions with up to four parallel classes each, plus a setup/install session at
  8:15 am for families who need help preparing.
- Platforms taught in 2026: Scratch (four progressive levels), Python, JavaScript, Minecraft, Godot,
  Arduino/electronics, MonoGame, Unity 3D, and Roblox.
- **Computers are not provided** — families must bring a laptop.
- No parking validation or reimbursement is offered; the page directs families to City Creek Mall or
  the Regent Street lot and warns there is no Saturday street parking downtown.
