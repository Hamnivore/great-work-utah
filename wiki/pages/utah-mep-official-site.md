## Summary

The Utah MEP official site describes a statewide manufacturing-extension resource focused on helping Utah manufacturers improve sales, productivity, quality, compliance, employee morale, technology, and production systems.

## Useful Claims

- Utah MEP organizes its marketplace around sales, leadership, compliance, production, technology, and a demonstration lab.
- The site points manufacturers toward custom-tailored plans, contact paths, success stories, events, CONNEX Utah, and manufacturing data.
- Its mission language emphasizes assessing needs and providing solutions through public and private resources for Utah industry.
