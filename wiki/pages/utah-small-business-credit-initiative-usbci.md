## Summary

The Utah Small Business Credit Initiative is a GOEO program meant to expand capital access, especially for underserved businesses. The page is still too thin to recommend a product: founders must verify current lenders, terms, and eligibility on the official USBCI site.

## Who It's For

Utah small businesses seeking credit access — especially operators in underserved communities — and lenders/partners participating in USBCI capital-access products. Not a substitute for venture equity; treat as a credit / capital-access pathway.

## How To Use It

1. Read the official program page for current products and partner lenders.
2. Confirm eligibility (business size, location, use of proceeds) before applying through a participating lender.
3. Contact GOEO capital-access staff if the site does not list a clear apply path.

## How To Access It

Start with the official website, then confirm current programs before recommending.

- [Official website](https://business.utah.gov/usbci/) · https://business.utah.gov/usbci/
- Email: [capitalaccess@utah.gov](mailto:capitalaccess@utah.gov)

## Cost / Eligibility

Not fully specified on the Startup State card. Verify current cost (if any), lender network, geographic coverage, and eligibility on the official page.

## Best Fits

Founders and Main Street operators who need debt / credit access rather than equity, especially when underserved-community mandate is relevant.
