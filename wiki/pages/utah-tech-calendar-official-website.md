## Summary

The Utah Tech Calendar homepage — its live event index — captured 2026-08-12. It carries the site's
own statement of scope, its refresh cadence, its stated base, and a full day-by-day listing as it
stood at capture.

It also shows the **per-entry source attribution** that is the calendar's most checkable feature: each
listing names the upstream feed it came from.

## Useful Claims

- The calendar covers **in-person Utah tech events** — meetups, conferences, founder mixers, and
  developer nights — named across **Salt Lake City, Provo, Lehi, Ogden, and Silicon Slopes**.
- It is **updated nightly** and gives its base as **Cottonwood Heights, UT**.
- At capture it listed **149 entries for August 2026**.
- Every listing carries a **`via <source>` attribution**. Feeds visible in the capture include
  **meetup, eventbrite, luma, 1 million cups**, and **cto breakfast**.
- Listings carry a venue and street address where the upstream feed supplies one, and omit it where it
  does not — so completeness varies by source rather than being normalized.
- Readers can filter by **region, city, tag, group, source, type, date, and online**, in **weekly or
  monthly** views.
- Events already covered by this corpus appear in it, including **CTO Breakfast Utah** at "IHOP ·
  American Fork" and a **UtahJS Lehi** meetup.

## Reliability Notes

Self-reported tier: the site describing its own coverage.

**"Comprehensive" is a claim, not a measurement.** The calendar aggregates from named platforms, so an
event promoted only in a Slack channel or a private list is structurally invisible to it. This corpus
holds a documented instance of that gap — the organizer of Kids Code Camp publicly noted his event
missing from a Utah tech calendar in June 2026
([Kids Code Camp 2026 announcements](utah-kids-code-camp-2026-announcements.md)) — which refers to that
period rather than to this capture, but is the right corrective to keep in view.

**The listings are a one-day snapshot.** The site states it regenerates nightly, so the 149 August
entries are what that night's run produced and not a stable figure. The durable content in this
capture is the structure — the scope statement, the cadence, the filter dimensions, and the
per-entry source attribution.

**On ownership this page is evidence of absence.** Nothing on it attributes the calendar to Forge Utah
or to any organization; the only provenance given is a city. That does not disprove an institutional
relationship, but the site makes no such claim about itself. The positive account of who built it is in
[the origin announcements](utah-tech-calendar-origin-announcements.md).
