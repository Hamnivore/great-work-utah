## Summary

Utah Tech Calendar is a statewide technology-event calendar operated by [Forge Utah](forge-utah.md). Forge's captured official site presents it as a calendar covering both Forge events and events run by others.

## Who It's For

Utah developers, data practitioners, founders, and organizers looking for public technology events.

## What It Provides

A calendar interface for technology meetups and community events across Utah.

## How To Use It

Use the calendar to discover an event, then verify the event on the organizer's own page before attending.

## Cost / Eligibility

Varies by listed event; the calendar itself is a discovery tool.

## Best Fits

People who need a broad event index rather than one organization's schedule.
