## Summary

UtahJS's own homepage: the community's self-description, its meetup cadence, and its conference. The
site is the authority on when and where the meetups run, which is the practical thing a reader wants
from it.

## Useful Claims

- UtahJS describes itself as a community of developers passionate about JavaScript and states it is a
  501(c)(3) organization run by volunteers.
- It runs an annual conference; conference details live at `utahjs.com/conference`.
- Two regular meetups: **Lehi**, the first Thursday of every month, and **Salt Lake City**, the third
  Tuesday of every month, both scheduled through Meetup.com.
- It lists four further JavaScript meetups in Utah alongside its own — Utah Stack JS, React Utah
  Meetup, Angular Community Meetup, and Ionic Utah — and invites others to be added.
- The copyright line reads 2012–2026; it does not by itself establish the organization's founding date.
- Volunteer contact is `hello@utahjs.com`. The site is built with Astro and credits three named
  volunteer maintainers.

## Reliability Notes

Self-reported tier. It is good evidence of cadence and structure and no evidence of size — no
attendance, membership, or conference-registration figure appears on the page.

The 501(c)(3) claim is corroborated primarily: the IRS exempt-organizations file lists **UTAHJS**,
South Jordan, Utah, subsection 03, with a 2024-03 ruling date and, for tax period 2024-12, total
revenue of $80,913 and assets of $33,649 — see
[Source: IRS Exempt Organizations Business Master File, Utah Extract](irs-bmf-utah-exempt-organizations.md).
Those are the only hard numbers the wiki has about UtahJS, and they describe a small volunteer
organization whose annual conference is most of its financial activity.

Meetup schedules are exactly the kind of fact that changes without the page being updated; the
cadence above should be re-checked against Meetup.com before anyone acts on it.
