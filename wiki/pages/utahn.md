## Summary

Utahn is a Utah-focused news publication whose public charter describes a platform for independent journalists rather than a publication governed by an institutional editorial board. Founder and publisher [Clint Betts](clint-betts.md) says it has no investors and frames it as a response to declining newsroom capacity in Utah. Both statements are self-reported.

## Impact

Utahn's proposed contribution is more reporting capacity and a different institutional model: journalists retain control of their beats, the publication does not endorse candidates, and its charter promises primary-source links, disclosed AI assistance, public corrections, sponsorship labeling, and annual revenue-source transparency. These are inspectable commitments, but the captured sources do not yet show a track record sufficient to judge whether the model works.

**Bet:** that removing the editorial board — rather than adding one — increases accountability, by making each journalist personally answerable for their own facts. The bet is contrarian, and its structural failure mode is clear: if bylines are not identifiable, readers cannot tell who is answerable for a story. Whether that problem exists in practice should be tested directly against Utahn's published work and correction process.

## Utah Context

The publication explicitly centers Utah institutions and public life. Its founder also leads [Silicon Slopes](silicon-slopes.md), creating a disclosed conflict when Utahn covers the technology organizations and people in that network.

## What They Need Now

No durable public hiring, contributor, funding, or submission request was verified in the captured sources. Readers should use the publication's current public site for any participation path.

## Who Could Help

Independent verification is more valuable than promotional support at this stage: public records that establish ownership and financing, and observable correction and transparency artifacts, would let readers test the charter against practice.

## Open Questions

- Who legally owns Utahn, LLC, and what public record identifies that entity?
- The founder says Utahn has no investors; what revenue currently supports publication?
- Has Utahn published the annual transparency report and searchable correction archive promised by its charter?
- How are editing and verification handled when final editorial authority rests with individual journalists?
- **Are Utahn's bylines identifiable?** This has not been checked systematically against the site and is the single highest-value open question because the charter places verification responsibility on individual journalists.
- **How much of the content uses AI assistance, and how consistently is that assistance disclosed?** The charter promises disclosure, but neither the disclosure practice nor the actual proportion has been verified.
