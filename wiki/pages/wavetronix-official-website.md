## Summary

Wavetronix's official website describes intelligent transportation systems for vehicle detection, signal timing, roadway data, motorways, midblock monitoring, and integrated traffic applications.

## Useful Claims

- Wavetronix lists systems for intersection performance, stop-bar detection, dilemma-zone protection, motorway data, midblock data, and smaller multi-approach intersections.
- The website says Wavetronix has been innovating since opening in 2000.
- Wavetronix says all of its sensors are made at its headquarters in Springville, Utah.
- The website says some Wavetronix sensors have been operating in the field for more than 15 years.
- The company says it supports and complies with the Build America Buy America Act.
