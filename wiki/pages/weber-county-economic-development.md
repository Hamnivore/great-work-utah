## Summary

Weber County Economic Development coordinates business support with infrastructure districts and the West Weber industrial and renewable-energy hub. That alignment can unlock projects a company or city cannot deliver alone, but the counterfactual remains county-scale and replaceable by competent local government.
## Who It's For

Businesses starting, expanding, or relocating in Weber County; site selectors evaluating Ogden and northern Utah industrial land; operators interested in infrastructure districts or county partnership programs.

## What It Provides

Direct ED director contact, linkage to Northern Utah Economic Alliance, economic development finance tools (public infrastructure districts), West Weber industrial district information, and county-led renewable energy partnership examples (e.g., landfill methane projects).

## How To Use It

Start with the official website, then confirm current programs before recommending.

## Cost / Eligibility

No public fee for county ED consultation. Infrastructure district and incentive eligibility follow Utah statute and project-specific approval — confirm with county and GOEO.

## Best Fits

Weber County expansions needing a county concierge — especially industrial, logistics, clean energy, or advanced manufacturing near West Weber. Route early ideation to SBDC or NUEA first if the question is generic startup formation.
