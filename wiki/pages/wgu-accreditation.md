## Summary

WGU's accreditation and recognition page — institutional accreditation through NWCCU, nationwide
operating authority via NC-SARA, and specialized accreditations by school. The live URL redirects to
`/student-experience/how-you-learn/accreditation.html`; the capture reflects the page content served
on retrieval.

## Useful Claims

- WGU is **institutionally accredited by the Northwest Commission on Colleges and Universities
  (NWCCU)**; NWCCU's region **includes WGU's headquarters of Utah**.
- WGU is a **participating NC-SARA institution**, operating in multiple states/territories **based on
  its approval in the State of Utah** — WGU's own description of nationwide reach.
- CIQG (CHEA/International Quality Group) **recognized WGU for outstanding performance in meeting
  the CIQG International Quality Principles**, noting that the university uses **a competency-based
  learning model with students working online**.
- **The CAHIIM entry on this page is expired on its face.** WGU's own text says the health
  information management accreditation "has been reaffirmed **through 2023**" — three years before
  this capture, with no later reaffirmation stated. Anyone citing this page's accreditation list as
  current should exclude that line, and it is a reminder that a self-maintained accreditation page
  is a snapshot of what the institution has gotten around to updating.
- Program-level accreditations named include **CAEP**, **AAQEP**, **CCNE**, **CAHIIM**, **ACBSP**, and
  **ABET** for specific degree lines.

> WGU received the 2018 CIQG Quality Award from the Council for Higher Education Accreditation/International Quality Group.

> — "Awarded for Outstanding Performance", CIQG citation

> WGU's accreditation comes from NWCCU because its region includes WGU's headquarters state of Utah.
> — FAQ, "Who accredits WGU?"

> The College’s accreditation for the baccalaureate degree in health information management has been reaffirmed through 2023.
> — "CAHIIM accreditation"
