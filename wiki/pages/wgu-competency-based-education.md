## Summary

WGU's competency-based education explainer — how the model works, how students advance, and how WGU
contrasts CBE with seat-time measurement. The live URL redirects to `/student-experience/how-you-learn.html`;
the capture reflects the page content served on retrieval.

## Useful Claims

- WGU defines CBE as measuring **skills and learning rather than time spent in a classroom**.
- Students **progress through courses as soon as they can prove they've mastered the material**,
  rather than waiting for a semester or term to end.
- Education pathways are **focused on learning, not seat time**; students accelerate where they
  already have knowledge.
- Study is **whenever and wherever** there is internet access; resources are **24/7**.
- WGU calls competency units (CUs) **"basically the same as a credit-hour in a traditional college"**
  but earned by demonstrated competency rather than time in class.
- Tuition is charged **per six-month term**, not per competency unit.

## Verbatim

> What is competency-based education? Simply put, it measures skills and learning rather than time spent in a classroom.
> — "How You'll Learn at WGU", overview

> Competency-based education means that students progress through courses as soon as they can prove they've mastered the material, rather than advancing only when the semester or term ends.
> — "Competency-Based Education, Explained"

> Efficient: Education pathways are focused on learning, not seat time. Students are able to spend time on areas where they need more understanding and accelerate where they already have knowledge.
> — "Student Flexibility"

> Flexible: Study happens whenever and wherever it works best for students. WGU learning resources are available 24/7, anywhere there is internet access.
> — "Student Flexibility"

> Competency units or CUs are basically the same as a credit-hour in a traditional college. Because we measure competency instead of time in–class, we call them competency units rather than credits.
> — FAQ, "What is a competency unit?"

> Nope! At WGU we charge tuition per six-month term, no matter how many courses and competency-units you complete during that time.
> — FAQ, tuition
