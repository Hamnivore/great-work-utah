# Where to Find Wet Lab Space in Utah

**Type:** guide
**Status:** Draft
**Confidence:** Medium
**Audience:** life-sciences founders, academic spinouts, SBIR teams needing bench space
**Focus:** wet-lab space, biotech incubators, shared lab, Altitude Lab, university facilities
**Updated:** 2026-08-11

## Overview

Utah has a deep life-sciences research base and a thin shared wet-lab market. If you need benches, hoods, cold storage, and compliance-aware lab neighbors — not just a WeWork with a fridge — start here. This guide routes; it does not restate program pages.

## If You Need Shared Wet Lab Plus Investor Access

[Altitude Labs](altitude-labs.md) is the primary public door. It is a biotech accelerator on Recursion's Salt Lake City campus with cohort residency, lab and office space, mentorship, and pre-seed / fellowship award tracks. Apply at https://www.altitudelab.org/apply. Best for early platform and techbio teams who can commit to a cohort model.

## If Your IP Still Sits At The University Of Utah

Disclose and license first through the [University of Utah Technology Licensing Office](university-of-utah-technology-licensing-office.md) (formerly PIVOT Center). Lab-space conversations that skip IP clearance waste everyone's time. Then return to Altitude or to a sponsored-lab arrangement your PI and department already control.

## If You Need The Broader Life-Sciences Network

[BioUtah](bio-utah.md) and [BioHive](biohive-utah.md) are membership and ecosystem doors — useful for introductions to manufacturers, CROs, and peers who may sublease or share capacity. They are not themselves wet-lab landlords. Use them after you can say what square footage, biosafety level, and equipment list you need.

## If You Are Still Deciding Whether A Company Should Exist

Do not rent lab yet. Start at [Nucleus MarketEdge](nucleus-marketedge.md) and the sequencing in [Commercialize Research in Utah](commercialize-research.md). Paying for benches before customer and regulatory path clarity is a common way Utah spinouts burn runway.

## What Not To Expect

- A large menu of competing private wet-lab landlords comparable to Boston or South San Francisco — the wiki does not currently document one.
- BYU or USU shared commercial wet-lab programs with the same public apply flow as Altitude — verify on-campus before assuming parity.
- Hospital or HCI space as a default startup lab — [Huntsman Cancer Institute](huntsman-cancer-institute.md) and health-system cores serve research missions, not general founder residency.

## Next Steps After Space

Once you have a bench path: [Nucleus Grow](nucleus-grow.md) / [UTIF](utif.md) for SBIR, [Nucleus Fund](nucleus-fund.md) for deep-tech equity, and [Find An Advisor](find-an-advisor.md) for regulatory and fractional operators.
