## Summary

Wikipedia's biographical article on Apa Sherpa, captured 2026-08-11. Used here for dated ascent counts and Utah residence where the foundation's own site is silent.

## Useful Claims

- Apa Sherpa made his **21st Mount Everest summit in May 2011** on The Eco Everest Expedition and **retired** after promising his wife to stop at **21 ascents**.
- He **first summited Everest in 1990**; his **last summit was in 2011**.
- He **held the world record with 21 ascents** until **Kami Rita Sherpa broke the record in 2018** with a 22nd ascent.
- Born in **Thame**, in Nepal's Everest region; worked as a **porter** for mountaineering groups after leaving school.
- Quote attributed to Apa: **"With an education they have a choice. Our goal is to give the younger generation a chance to go to school so they don't have to climb."**
- The family **moved to the United States in December 2006** and **lives in Draper, Utah**.
- **Apa Sherpa Foundation** founded **April 2009**, dedicated to education and economic development in Nepal.
