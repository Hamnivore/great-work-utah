## Summary

williams-int.com is Williams International's official corporate site: a homepage, a thin Products page (the FJ33/FJ44 commercial fanjet family, with a downloadable spec sheet), a Careers page and a set of individual location-specific job postings (including Ogden, Utah CNC machinist roles), an Engineering Rotations page describing its new-graduate rotational program, an Internships page, and a Contact page giving the Pontiac, Michigan headquarters address. It is a marketing- and recruiting-oriented site rather than an investor- or press-relations site: it does not publish employee counts, revenue, a facilities list, or any description of its military/cruise-missile engine business line — that side of the company's history and product line has to be sourced independently (see the Wikipedia and DoD-program sources on the [Williams International](williams-international.md) page).

## Useful Claims

- Williams International describes itself as "the world leader in the development, manufacture, and support of gas turbine engines," founded in 1955, "privately owned and highly vertically integrated."
- The Contact page gives the corporate address as 2000 Centerpoint Parkway, Pontiac, Michigan 48341, confirming Michigan (not Utah) as the company's headquarters.
- The Careers page frames itself around "technology, science, engineering, manufacturing, production" roles and separately lists individual current-opening pages, including one for CNC Machinist positions in Ogden, Utah, confirming active manufacturing hiring at the Utah site.
- The Engineering Rotations page describes a rotational program for new engineering graduates cycling through mechanical design, structural/thermal analysis, aerodynamics, thermodynamic cycle modeling, vibration/rotordynamics, electrical/electronics, control systems, software development, manufacturing engineering, and test — the clearest public statement of the breadth of Williams' engineering work.
- The site's customer-testimonial section on the homepage is entirely about commercial FJ44 business-jet engines (Cessna Citation, Cirrus Vision Jet, Pilatus PC-24 operators); it does not mention military or missile applications anywhere found on the current site.

## Reliability Notes

This is an official corporate source, reliable for company self-description, headquarters location, and current hiring emphasis. It is thin: no financials, no total or site-level headcounts, no facilities overview, and — notably — no public statement connecting the company to its well-documented military cruise-missile engine business (F107/F415 family), which is sourced instead from Wikipedia, a Michigan environmental-permit inspection record, and Utah/Ogden economic-development documents on the [Williams International](williams-international.md) page. Because a load-bearing claim (the military/cruise-missile business) is not corroborated by the company's own current site, this source page alone is graded Low; the venture page's overall Medium confidence rests on combining it with government sources.
