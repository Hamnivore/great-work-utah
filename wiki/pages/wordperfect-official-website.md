## Summary

Corel's current WordPerfect product site, which markets WordPerfect Office as an all-in-one productivity suite for businesses, government, and education. It establishes that the product still ships, and under whose ownership; it says nothing about the software's Utah origins.

## Useful Claims

- WordPerfect is a current, commercially marketed product, sold as an office suite to business, government, and education buyers.
- The product is owned and published by Corel, not by a Utah company.
