## Summary

YESCO's own timeline from founder Thomas Young's emigration to Ogden through Las Vegas sign work and recent family leadership transitions. Critical for what YESCO claims about iconic signs — and for one important qualification on the Welcome sign.

## Useful Claims

- Thomas Young emigrated to **Ogden, Utah** at age fifteen; YESCO's timeline dates founding the **Thomas Young Sign Company on March 20, 1920** in Ogden.
- YESCO says it **literally put Las Vegas on the map, as far as signs are concerned** and installed neon spectaculars for the **Golden Nugget**, Las Vegas Club, Eldorado Club, and others in the 1950s.
- **Vegas Vic**, described as a **75-foot, 12,000 pound** sign, was **built and installed** by YESCO in the 1950s timeline entry.
- On the **Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas sign**, YESCO's timeline states it **was designed by Betty Willis and built for the Clark County Commission** — the company's own history page does **not** claim YESCO designed that sign; it places YESCO in the acquisition of Rainbow Sign Company context, not as the sign's fabricator in that sentence.
- **Fourth-generation family members** Ryan, Joshua, and Nathan Young were named to the board; **Ryan Young** was named **president and CEO** of Young Electric Sign Company.
- YESCO says it **restored** the **Vegas Vickie** sign for Circa Resort & Casino and maintains **Vegas Vic**; Wendover Will was also **built by YESCO** per the timeline.
