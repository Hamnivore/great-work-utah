## Summary

Zanskar Geothermal's official website describes an AI-driven geothermal exploration company along the Wasatch Front, framing exploration risk as geothermal's core bottleneck and positioning machine-learning tools as the solution for identifying subsurface heat resources.

## Useful Claims

- Zanskar describes geothermal as affordable, carbon-free, 24/7 power and states a mission to find and harness underground heat, with projects "online today" delivering firm, utility-scale power.
- The company describes an exploration approach using modern geoscience, artificial intelligence, and field and drilling methods to map the subsurface and develop overlooked or high-risk geothermal resources.
- The homepage states Zanskar is "the first AI-native geothermal energy company" and is located along the Wasatch Front, UT, USA — not a specific Salt Lake City address.
